### Summary

Adam lived through the rise, and fall of the Aezatan Dynasty. He managed to survive the horrors and close extinction of his people. His singular motive throughout his life has been to save their legacy and try to rebuild a semblance of a home or life for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] forged in this new almost alien world.

During  [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Titan War\|The Titan War]], when it was clear that Adam and his people has no chance of ending things peacefully, Adam was one of the lucky survivors and forefront leaders to push the preservation program.

The [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] built Aeternite coffins that would lock their life essence and soul and preserve them for thousands of years. They would not really be living but the devices were built to allow a wake-up mechanism.

Unfortunately with dwindling resources, and imminent death, the work was rushed a good number of these experiments did not go well. Adam himself in fact did not get a full revival.

His body was stored under the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Raijin Mountains\|Raijin Mountains]] and a group of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Titanborn\|Titanborn]] people, which was ironic, found him and treated him like some sort of deity. His body was nonfunctional but he slowly gained power. Before he could take over or fix his own body, the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] invaded the clan of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Titanborn\|Titanborn]] and destroyed Adam’s body. Of course with the power he had left, none of the people within the ruin survived, this allowed Adam’s soul to persevere.

After some years, Adam’s soul was reborn to a high-ranking [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/High Draconian\|High Draconian]] family. As his memories and soul started to ignite the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] Adam, slowly started to make a name for himself within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/Draconians\|Draconians]] people. He took his time to learn about the current world and started working towards the revival of his people.

Of course, the worlds no longer had the magic available to do what he wanted so he needed a long-term plan, also knowing that eventually the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] would be looking for him.

As he became a hero for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/Draconians\|Draconians]] people he eventually did what they thought was impossible. He ushered the corrupted form of a Dragon, but instead of going mad with power, he remained in full control. This is where he earned the title of the Vermillion Emperor, coming from his red fiery scales and unrivaled power. Up to this modern day his name is legendary and the legacy of the Dragon Emperor is still living within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/Draconians\|Draconians]] people.

Having made such a big mark on the world, the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] was not far behind and after many battles and hunts, eventually, the fleet of the Twelve cornered [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]], in his fully powered dragon forms over the seas between [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkoria\|Valkoria]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dragon Lands\|Dragon Lands]]. A massive and deadly battle took place where the vast majority of the fleet was burnt to a crisp, but [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] eventually succumbed to the might of the army of the church and his body sank to the bottom of the ocean. Legend has it, that the place where the battle took place, is now the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Black Lagoon\|Black Lagoon]] and the volcano appeared shortly after the Vermillion Emperor’s death. This of course is a legend and unlikely to be exactly what happened but there is some truth to it.

The [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] was ready this time, and upon his defeat, Adam’s soul was shattered into thousands of fragments, and he could not be reborn again.

### Vermillion Ritual

Although [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] was defeated and his soul shattered by the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]], it would not be his end. He knew this would happen and went to some great lengths to secure his survival.

Decades before his bodily death, he forcefully dislodged a chunk of his soul, and locked it away deep in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dreamscape\|Dreamscape]]. This of course severely weakened him.

As his soul was shattered and fragments of it were being reborn into the people of the worlds. He was slowly transmitting his power and also learning what they were. These people were taking on aspects of his power as both an [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] but now also a Fiery Dragon form. 4 of these people were [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Martell Martin\|Martell Martin]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kodron Blackheart\|Kodron Blackheart]].

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] had devised a ritual that could reverse and join a soul that was one whole. Unfortunately, the people of his time didn’t have the power, know-how or technology to perform it. However, when a fragment of his soul landed in [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] a powerful and brilliant archmagus from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]], the ritual was suddenly possible.

In fact during the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]],  [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Martell Martin\|Martell Martin]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kodron Blackheart\|Kodron Blackheart]] did perform the ritual in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] and the result ended up in conflict between [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]]. Brand took the power of Adam within himself unleashing the madded power of the Vermillion Emperor.

With time and the help of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ajax\|Ajax]], the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] managed to restore Adam to an [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] body was locked away in Aeternite for millennia.

### **Relationships and History**

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ajax\|Ajax]] studies and learns the ways of the world. They figured that the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Azzatans\|Azzatans]] souls of old, are now shattered in thousands of parts and still forming part of the soul cycle. 

The Vermillion ritual was proof that a soul can be brought back together, of course replicating it in mass would be quite difficult. They studied the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Elf/Ashen Elf\|Ashen Elf]] revival through [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Graav (God of Death)\|Graav (God of Death)]]’s power. Soul Transference of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Onakei\|Onakei]], they researched the ways of the Pale one who seems to control his soul at will. And of course, the Vermillion Ritual itself, now performed was the base of something new.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ajax\|Ajax]] would both set off on a journey to try and bring some of their people back to build a new society.
