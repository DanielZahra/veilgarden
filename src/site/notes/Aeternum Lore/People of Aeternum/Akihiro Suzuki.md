### Summary

A man of few words and a shinobi from the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Hangetsu Clan\|Hangetsu Clan]]  in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Himoari\|Himoari]], Yamagato who joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] as a recruit of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]].

He swore an oath of loyalty to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] after he got help un-petrifying some of his old friends within the Red Mire, after [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] defeated a Hag that had taken residence in the swamp.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Akihiro Suzuki\|Akihiro Suzuki]] followed Varris to Valkoria and now is an active member in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].
