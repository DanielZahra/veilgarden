### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] ’ older brother and astromancer in training, though far ahead of Astellios himself. [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Stefanos Lightseeker\|Stefanos Lightseeker]] is very fond of Alekos and makes it obvious he prefers him, especially since Alekos does everything he can to appease his father. Astellios is jealous of Alekos’ greater astromantic power and how much his father favors him, but Alekos has always been nice to him and helps him in his astromancer training. He remains neutral on Lycian Unification, though Astellios suspects he favors it but is afraid to say so.

### **Relationships and History**

After being abducted by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]]’s forces and kept as a prisoner in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Argentcourt\|Argentcourt]], it was clear that working with [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]] was not an option. At the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/End of the Sixth Valkorian War\|End of the Sixth Valkorian War]], both [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Stefanos Lightseeker\|Stefanos Lightseeker]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Alekos Lightseeker\|Alekos Lightseeker]] moved to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Calliope Lightseeker\|Calliope Lightseeker]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]]. They joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] and started working to build a new observatory to spread the teaches of Astrology and Astromancy to the people of the[[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]]. Eventually moved to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]] to continue their work there and make it their new home.

Having seen with his own eyes, how Astromancy can help people, Alekos dedicated a new branch of the Astromancy school to help those who want to use to to fight, and protect everything they hold dear.
