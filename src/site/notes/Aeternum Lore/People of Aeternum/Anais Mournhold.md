### Biography

One of the youngest sisters to join the service of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]]. As devout of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Orena (Goddess of Life)\|Orena (Goddess of Life)]], she made sure that the people of the city respect life and preserve it as much as possible. 

She was skilled in healing magics and often served as a healer outside of the remit of the Church. Having witnessed the events of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists\|Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists]] first hand, left her changed and instilled a doubt in her mind.

She remained in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] to help the victoms of the massacres.
