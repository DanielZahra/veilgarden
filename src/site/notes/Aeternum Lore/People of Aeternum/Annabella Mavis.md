### Summary

The rigid and strong queen of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] and the current wielder of the Crown one of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Valkorian Vestiges\|Valkorian Vestiges]].

Many fear her for the powers he she has swaying people, especially when using the crown’s full might.

### **Relationships and History**

Her son [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cameron Mavis\|Cameron Mavis]], was publically executed by the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] and her husband [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ternian Mavis\|Ternian Mavis]] was taken as a prisoner, again and did not survive to tell the tale. Her revenge will be coming.
Mother of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Elizabeth Mavis\|Elizabeth Mavis]] and now deceased [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cameron Mavis\|Cameron Mavis]].
