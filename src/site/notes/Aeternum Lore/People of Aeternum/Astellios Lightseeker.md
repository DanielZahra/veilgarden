### Summary

Astellios, is the youngest of the Lightseeker family, born and raised in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] he spent his childhood among astromancers, book and lived in the shadow of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]]. His father [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Stefanos Lightseeker\|Stefanos Lightseeker]] was a leading astromancer while his mother [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Calliope Lightseeker\|Calliope Lightseeker]] was a political figure in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]].

He was enrolled from a young age in the **Academiae**, where he studied **astromancy and astronomy**, following in his father’s and brother’s footsteps. Despite showing great promise, his father largely ignored him, favouring his elder brother **Alekos**, leaving Astellios longing for approval. He often found comfort during visits to **Lycia**, spending time with his mother and her gnomish assistant, **Rena Quiethand**.

At age 11, Astellios accompanied his father to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)\|Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)]] in search of a rare book and befriended [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]] the son of a book trader. The two remained close friends until Radegundh left years later to study giant runes in the **Falls of Gohondar**, which deeply affected Astellios.

Throwing himself into study, Astellios excelled beyond his peers and attracted the attention of **Vasilios Starcaller**, the charismatic son of the High Astromancer and a strong advocate for **Lycian Unification**. Seeking both mentorship and validation, Astellios became Vasilios’ devoted pupil and supporter of unification further encouraged by his mother’s shared views. Through Vasilios, he also gained access to restricted telescopes at the **Observatorium** and met **Yorgos Darktwilight**, who helped him explore the constellations that power astromancy.

As graduation neared, Astellios faced a choice: follow the expected path at the Observatorium like his brother, or do something daring to prove his worth. Inspired by Vasilios, he resolved to **travel the world** to learn from three legendary clans who drew power from the great constellations—the **Dragon**, the **Chalice**, and the **Archer**—and bring their knowledge back to elevate Stellaria.

His father mocked and opposed the idea, while his mother was concerned but understanding. To Astellios’ surprise, Alekos supported him. With Vasilios’ political maneuvering and Radegundh’s timely return to Stellaria, Astellios gained official approval for his journey—marking the start of his **quest for celestial knowledge** and personal recognition.

### Journey

As he left on his quest with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]], he eventually met with  [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]] and as they worked with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]], they all joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] and eventually called themselves the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]].

Astellios was mostly guided by his thrist for knowledge and curiosity. He put himself in the role if a knowledge seeker and dived deep into the creed of the Quills. As he journied the land he also realised that the power of Astromancy can do a lot of good outside of Stellario but can also be used as a dangerous weapon. As the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] got embroiled in more political affairs, [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] became a linchpin location in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]]. Astellios put aside his calm demenor and brought forth anger towards [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]] and specifically the archmage [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]], whom he has sworn to ruin.

As the events in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] were behind the group, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] focused on making sure to preserve the teaching of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] outside of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]] and using the Quills as a lunch pad made sure to setup with his brother [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Alekos Lightseeker\|Alekos Lightseeker]] new schools in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].

This purpose will remain secret and shared only with the most trusted of people.
