### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]] was born in a nobile family in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]], [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/House Stonecutter\|House Stonecutter]]. However he spent all of his childhood and teen years without knowing this, as he lived with is adoptive family in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Caershire\|Caershire]]. He loved music, song and as fine things could bring for a commoner family in the queendom. His dream was to someday attend the Bard Collage at [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Duztar’s Step\|Duztar’s Step]], but slowly learnt that for commoners this was not an eay feat.

Eventually his journey to find is real family brought him to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] where we met with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]]. This journey led him to become a coucil member of the new city [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]] and an inflential politican in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].  More details in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/The Midnight Note\|The Midnight Note]] 

A member of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]]
