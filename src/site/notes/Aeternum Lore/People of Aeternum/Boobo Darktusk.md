### Summary

The gentle chef orc, left his tribe a long time ago since he didn't share the same passion for pillaging and violence in general, but that doesn't mean he won't resolve to it if necessary. He works for a butcher shop and sometimes provides the gang with food.

His fortune turned sour and he ended up joining the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] alongside [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Moira Lockeheart\|Moira Lockeheart]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Rouge Owlfeather\|Rouge Owlfeather]]. His large frame makes him the natural enforcer of the group.

Having blindly followed [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]], Boobo now find himself richer and stronger and is more dedicated to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]].

### History

Having received a lot of training from [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Moira Lockeheart\|Moira Lockeheart]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Rouge Owlfeather\|Rouge Owlfeather]], at the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/End of the Sixth Valkorian War\|End of the Sixth Valkorian War]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boobo Darktusk\|Boobo Darktusk]] managed to slowly change his looks and make a quasi-perfect disguise over some months. 

With the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Silver Eagles\|Silver Eagles]] in dire need of new recruits, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boobo Darktusk\|Boobo Darktusk]] took the name of **Erol Steelring, **and infiltrated the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Silver Eagles\|Silver Eagles]] and was acting as a spy for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]].
