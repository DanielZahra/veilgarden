### Summary

Boris usually doesn't share much about himself or his past, except when he's trying to teach new members of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] something about the job. 

He’s the father of Varris, (a member of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]]). 

### Appearance and Personality

Always draped in black clothing and secretive, Boris does not seem like a man anyone would want to mess with.

### **Relationships and History**

Boris, has led the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]], to get mixed up in a secret mission for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] with ended with the capture of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] ‘s prince's capture and eventual execution which spiralled the region into some chaos. As the Palantine Empire roiled in the 6th Valkorian War, Boris earned the position of Shadowblade, which allows him to perform the machinations necessary to pit many people against each other and manipulate the war from the shadows. Ultimately, this allowed [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] to indirectly send the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] to hunt down and kill [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Draco Palantine\|Draco Palantine]] and kill him to end the war but mainly to vacate the seat of the Ebonmaster which he was ready to snatch for himself.

Eventually Boris got his adopted son [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] to bring him [[NOTION_PAGE:562aba2c-22e8-4903-a217-a93ae61da6a6\|NOTION_PAGE:562aba2c-22e8-4903-a217-a93ae61da6a6]] which set him in a position of power on the continent.
