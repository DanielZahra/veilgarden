### Summary

**Brand** was born in the small, snowy village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Frosthler\|Frosthler]], beyond the mountains of **Kas'Velar**. His birth was accompanied by an unprecedented phenomenon: a massive, sudden heatwave that melted the snow surrounding the house, causing widespread astonishment and confusion. He was born a healthy baby with one striking anomaly: half his hair was red, an unnatural sight in a village where all humans have light, ashen hair. Due to the strange circumstances of his birth, Brand was quickly branded as a **cursed child** by many villagers, leading to his parents being marginalized. His friends and family, however, remained supportive. Between the ages of two and five, Brand developed rough, red scales covering his backbone, along with small, curved, scaly horns pointing toward the ground. His parents strictly commanded him never to be seen shirtless to hide this secret.

Despite the bullying and isolation from his peers, Brand was deeply loved by his parents, who encouraged him to believe his uniqueness would lead to greatness. He maintained a close circle of three friends:

- **Bjorn:** A large, shy titanborn seeking a simple life in the village.
- **Frode:** A brilliant Elf aspiring to become a scholar and settle near a large library.
- **Gertrude:** A quick-witted, sharp-tongued Human girl who wished to travel and live in a warmer climate by the sea.

Brand, feeling caged in his native village, longed to travel the world to explore and discover the truth behind the "curse" or "higher power" that marked him.

At age 15, during the aftermath of a harsh raider attack, Brand was cornered by older bullies. They forcibly removed his jacket, exposing the scales on his back. Believing the fire phenomena at his birth made him immune to flames, the teens dragged him toward a bonfire, attempting to push his face into the fire, burning right side of his face and leaving a permanent scar.

Brand's screams drew the attention of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Gremolim Goldheart\|Gremolim Goldheart]], the leader of a traveling humanitarian group known as the **Spinners**. Gremolin quickly dispersed the gang. Examining Brand's scales and recalling the circumstances of his birth, he determined that Brand possessed **draconic blood** from a red, fiery dragon lineage. He explained that Brand was not cursed but special and would become more powerful in an environment that allowed him to grow. Gromolim suggested Brand travel with the Spinners to discover his true self and the legends of other draconic humanoids. Though initially reluctant to mix with strangers, Brand was thrilled by the revelation and the opportunity to travel. His parents agreed, and a few days later, Brand left Frosthler with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Spinners\|Spinners]].

### **The Journey and the Search**

The burn Brand sustained during the incident left a permanent scar, which he viewed not as a disability, but as a symbol of the moment he discovered his identity and gained the opportunity to travel. He initially struggled with social anxiety but found solace and peace assisting the group's chef, **Beldan Bog**. By working with fire and cooking for crowds, Brand overcame his inhibitions, transforming into a confident, positive individual who enjoyed making others happy. Over the next decade, Brand traveled widely with the Spinners, gathering fragments of legends suggesting that when all the humanoids born with draconic bloodlines finally meet, a significant event will occur. He focused on collecting pieces of this puzzle, believing that even baseless rumors might lead to a core truth. While traveling, Brand entered a village and was stunned to find **Gertrude** performing a beautiful song and dance routine with her own Spinner group. The two joyously reunited. Gertrude introduced Brand to one of her new acquaintances, lanky Beastkin called [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Flake of Snow\|Flake of Snow]], who had survived a childhood raid and left the village years ago.
Flake revealed she worked for an employer,a scholar named **Shi-rai, **who might know the answers Brand seeks. Shi-rai required capable people for a job in **Valkoria**. Flake was heading to **Bellmark's Landing** and then **Ordona** to meet him and was recruiting. Thrilled by the chance to finally find answers, Brand agreed to join her, viewing this as the culmination of his ten-year search.
