### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brenna\|Brenna]]  is a loving and bubbly woman, always filling the home with warmth and laughter. She tirelessly prepares hearty meals for the people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]], especially for her husband and children who eat more than fair share.
