### Summary

Originally from somewhere in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Cerulia/Cerulia\|Cerulia]], Bruni was the Head of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Bone Collectors\|Bone Collectors]]. A fearless warrior that was known to hunt the most dangerous creatures in the planes. Bruni was often travelling between the guild locations and recruiting the best talent he could find and sometimes going on the most dangerous jobs. At the end of the day he was a man of action.
