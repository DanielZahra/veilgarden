### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] ’ mother and ambassador for Stellaria in Lycia. She moves between Stellaria and Lycia frequently to ensure relations with Lycia remain strong. She officially takes a neutral stance on Lycian Unification but internally she is in favour of it (though [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Stefanos Lightseeker\|Stefanos Lightseeker]]  is unaware of this). Astellios is aware of his mother’s opinion since he often visits her alone in Lycia when he can.

Following the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] forcefully dragging [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] into the war resulting in them officially joining [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]]. 

During the confusion in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] Calliope saw her husband and son abducted by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]]’s troops to make [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] shy away for interfering, this however didn’t stop Astellios form rescuing his family and making Maev Regret her decision. Following this Calliope could not stand to work for Lycia any long and alongside the Lightseeker family, left [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]] and went to live in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].

Calliope put her political skills to use and worked mostly with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Klotan V Duskember\|Klotan V Duskember]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Marcus Steeljaw\|Marcus Steeljaw]] to make sure the people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] are kept safe and don’t end up as more war victims.

By the end of the war, having seen her family move to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]], after a long discussion with the Representatives of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], she was chosen as the best person to take over the Representative seat in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]] and usher a new place for the people of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]]
