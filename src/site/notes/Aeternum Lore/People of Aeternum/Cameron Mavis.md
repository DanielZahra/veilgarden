### Summary

The Prince of Nya'Tallin is not that 'useful' as his title does not bring any power, especially since his sister is in-line for the throne. He lived his life in that shadow but he's made the best of it. He's a free spirit who wants to see the world and travel around.


Eventually, he would have to go back and act as the General for his sister's armies.

During his travels with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Radical Dreamers\|Radical Dreamers]], he learnt that his father was imprisoned in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] ‘s capital and together with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Radical Dreamers\|Radical Dreamers]] he managed to get him out and try to escape back to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]].

Sightseeing, travelling and experiencing all that he can so that he can go back and tell his stories to his younger sister.

While travelling he does not want people to know his identity. He's going around with his bodyguard [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Jamari Grey\|Jamari Grey]] , but he's just getting started.

Eventually, his goal to save his father turned into an obsession which soured the prince and made him hateful towards the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] and its people.

### **Relationships and History**

He followed the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Radical Dreamers\|Radical Dreamers]] through the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] and eventually managed a daring rescue of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ternian Mavis\|Ternian Mavis]]. As they stayed hidden and disguised, and a few months later they ([[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cameron Mavis\|Cameron Mavis]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Jamari Grey\|Jamari Grey]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ternian Mavis\|Ternian Mavis]]) tried to make their escape while the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Radical Dreamers\|Radical Dreamers]], covered for them.

However the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] was onto them and intercepted their movements in the mountains with the involvement of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]].

The Prince eventually was brought back to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] and publically executed to humiliate the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] and it’s Queen.
