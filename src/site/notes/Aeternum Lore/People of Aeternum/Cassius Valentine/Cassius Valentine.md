### Summary

A strange individual that arrived to Aeternum from another Universe altogether. When he landed in Runaria he found that his previous magic and powers did not function as they did in his world in his world. He’s a special [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Unliving/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]] that inhibits the body of an elf with long white hair and blood red eyes. Before moving to Aeternum he absorbed the full soul of an Eternal within himself.  This makes him unique and almost ‘exists’ in a different category than the rest of the people of Aeternum.

It took many centuries to tame and harness the a small part power of that soul and Cassius Managed to create a Dark Paradise for himself in the eastern reach of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]] is now known as the Shadowlands. By Aeternum standards, Cassius is an Abomination that achieved immortality. None like him ever existed but, legenda from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]], call some of his kind the Nosferatu, Vampires or Blood Suckers. None know how long he has been in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Runaria\|Runaria]] or Aeternum, stories about the Pale Prince are sparse and mostly treated as myths.

Cassius favors the colors black and gold with hints of red. All facets seem to inherit the need to be flashy and have a sense for fashion, looking good whatever they are doing.

### Abilities

Alongside his enigmatic and impossible to explain magical talent, Cassius also wields 6 pistols which float around his shoulders and eliminate anything they comes with range and also hold a special bond with [[NOTION_PAGE:22d75a22-781a-8095-ac50-f6838a56d2c9\|NOTION_PAGE:22d75a22-781a-8095-ac50-f6838a56d2c9]], a rapier and an artifact said to house a powerful Nosferatu. Due to his power, he has become effectively immortal and does not age.

### Facets

To control the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Eternal\|Eternal]]’s soul Cassius has split himself in 9 different facets. These facets or bodies are all Cassius but they function all at the same time and over millennia have developed distinct abilities and specialties.  Each facet of Cassius has a [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Homunculus\|Homunculus]] that he uses as a bodyguard. He calls these Echoes of past heroes and are terrifyingly powerful.
