Living in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/The Shadowlands\|The Shadowlands]] is a challenge in itself, the ground is dry and barely fertile. The Gardner is the facet which is fully focused on bringing life to a land which should be dead. Focused on food supplies, natural, healing and life magic. The main pioneer behind the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Unliving/Risen\|Risen]] as they are the main example of life coming from death.
