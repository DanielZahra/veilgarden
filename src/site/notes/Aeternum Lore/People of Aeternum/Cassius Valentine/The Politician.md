This facet was mainly used to interface with people around Aeternum. However, when [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Valkorian Sunstriker\|Valkorian Sunstriker]] and his group, met with him in the edge of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/The Shadowlands\|The Shadowlands]], the talks didn’t go so well and Cassius’ arrogance got the better of him as he assumed this facet alone could handle the group. The Politician was destoryed, within seconds the rest of the Facets arrived but where too late, Valkorian and his group were afterwards immediately subdued by the Judge and the Vile.
