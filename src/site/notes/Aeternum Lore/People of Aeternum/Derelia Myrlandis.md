### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Derelia Myrlandis\|Derelia Myrlandis]] is one of the more senior knights in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Umbra\|Umbra]] and currently the Knight Commander of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Knight Orders of the Twelve\|Knight Orders of the Twelve]] within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]].

Derelia’s main role is to make sure all knights within her ranks are properly trained in both the faith and the strength of arms and magic required.

She is more on the serious side but those that know her well say that she has a golden heart.

### Story

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Derelia Myrlandis\|Derelia Myrlandis]] was originally adopted by a family from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yamagato/Yamagato\|Yamagato]] within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Kazanami Clan\|Kazanami Clan]]. She grew up to be a warrior following the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yamagato/Bushidō’\|Bushidō’]] code and be as honorable as possible. Coming from a religious family she has a strong believe in the twelve, but having ashen elf blood she wanted to explore the worlds and wonder what was in store for her.

Eventually she wound up joining the Knights of the Twelve and after rising in ranks she ended up in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] as the Knight Commander.
