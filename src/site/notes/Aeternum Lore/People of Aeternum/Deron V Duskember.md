### Summary

Young Mayor of Vakkengard, given the title after his father left to work in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].

Having studies and trained under [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Klotan V Duskember\|Klotan V Duskember]] allowed him to take the role and excel at it almost immediately.

### **Relationships and History**

His was captured and being by the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] on his own and saw his sister murdered. This gave him fuel to push Vakkengard to join the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].
