### Summary

The former price of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] and also the Ebon Master of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]]. Known for his cruelty shrewdness and ability move the Syndicate to do the Empire’s deeds while also making and moving a lot of gold on the side. 

### Skills, Abilities, and Possessions

Trained in the way of the spear, Draco was an excellent fighter and worth of the title of Prince and Ebon Master.  

### **Relationships and History**

During the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], Draco was betrayed by some of his own organization as they revealed his location to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]] which initiated a full assault to end the war and take his life. The [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] infiltrated the statue in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Falls of Gohondar\|Falls of Gohondar]] and managed to defeat the Draco, Gaia and Cirillo (his bodyguards) in the statue’s head chambers. This ultimately, spiraled into the end of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]].

In the late of October of 3213 it was clear that [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] has a hand in Draco’s betrayal, which led to Draco’s death and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] took the reigns as the new master of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]].
