### Summary

Raven was a hero to the people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Stormreach\|Stormreach]] in his time. Raven was from the village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]] and spent most of this time teaching swordmanship and the teaches of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/The Twelve (Gods)/The Twelve (Gods)\|The Twelve (Gods)]].


Around the year 2520 , [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Stormreach\|Stormreach]] was under attack by a cult of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/Draconians\|Draconians]]s that worshipped a corrupted [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Bestiary/Bestiary of Aeternum/Dragon\|Dragon]], that terrorized the region. The dragon was called ‘The White Death’ due to its affiny with water and ice magic.

A group of men and women from the north of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Stormreach\|Stormreach]] grouped together and went to hunt the dragon. The leader of this group was [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’\|Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’]].

Within a month, the brave people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Stormreach\|Stormreach]] had defeated the cultist but the dragon remained.

In a final confrontation, it is said that that when most of the people had perished to the dragon, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’\|Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’]] defeated the dragon in single combat but died to exhaustion within hours. The handful of survivors returned with the story and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’\|Ebony Leaf ‘Raven’]] alongside the rest of the group were named heroes of Stormreach.

Few years later, Raven was named a Saint of the Twelve and a sword monument was built in his name.
