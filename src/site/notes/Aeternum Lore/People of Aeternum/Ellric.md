### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ellric\|Ellric]] stands apart from the rest of his family. Unlike his strong and labor-focused kin, Ellric chose a different path, becoming a scholar. Though not physically strong, his sharp mind is unmatched, making him the intellectual of the family. He decided to leave the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]]  to learn and expand his knowledge beyond what he thought was possible.
