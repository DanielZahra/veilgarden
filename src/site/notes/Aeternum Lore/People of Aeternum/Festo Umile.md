### Summary

A man living on the outskirts of Ordona, a logger by trade that was helping to sell the best wood to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]].

### **Relationships and History**

His home was attacked by a group of wild people and worshippers of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)\|Foristra (Goddess of Nature)]].

Luckily for him the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] was close by and they managed to save both his daughter and his wife. Albeit his son was lost in the fire. The group sent him to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]] and asked him to speak to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Thori Stoneshaper\|Thori Stoneshaper]] as they knew a house was vacant within the village.
