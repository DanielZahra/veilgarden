### Summary

Born in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]] where an accident killed her family. She ran away from home and somehow managed to stow away on a ship that got he to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bhadir\|Bhadir]] where she learnt their ways, and language and got in a lot of trouble. 

Eventually, she met [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]] joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] as protector and a Novice in the order.

She owes a great debt to the king of Bhadir, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Lothar Blackmane\|Lothar Blackmane]], as he saved her from execution, she will never forget this and eventually, she wants to repay him, somehow.

### Appearance and Personality

White fur with block pockmark dots and dark-maned hair. Shorter than most of her kind. Piercing blue eyes. Under the fur and hooded leather lies the athletic build of someone that has been active their whole life.

Flake acts cool, and calm with a snarky tongue, until a fight breaks out and she fights like a mad woman.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

Born in the savage continent of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]] and spent a long time in the streets of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bhadir\|Bhadir]] has made Flake a strong tough and strong fighter. She doesn’t rely much on weapons as her claws are all she needs.

### **Relationships and History**

As she followed [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]], she took a job of protecting the new base of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] in Bellmark’s Landing while the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] worked to liberate the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].
