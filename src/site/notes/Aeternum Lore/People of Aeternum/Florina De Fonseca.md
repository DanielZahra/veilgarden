### Summary

Coming from a long line of families that make fine port wine. (the De Fonseca), Florina has had a comfortable upbringing as a rich citizen of the empire. She got involved in politics pretty early and eventually made her way as a Praetor for [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]. She was known to be cunning and ruthless but also business minded and she helped turn the city of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] into a very profitable hub for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]].

### **Relationships and History**

During her term as Preator, she was brought the Prince of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cameron Mavis\|Cameron Mavis]] and Florina was the one deciding and also responsible for making sure his death would be a show.

Little did she know that this would backfire, as the people were sick of this and revolted. She did not have many allies in this and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] ‘s main troops did not even get to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] in time as the people of the city took over.

The people were ready to burn her house down with her in it, but the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] changed this outcome. If she had to return to the empire she would be executed.

She’s being held under house arrest in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] and acting as a ‘advisor’ to the new council.

During the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/End of the Sixth Valkorian War\|End of the Sixth Valkorian War]], Queen [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Elizabeth Mavis\|Elizabeth Mavis]], of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] requested that [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Florina De Fonseca\|Florina De Fonseca]] be delivered as a prisoner for causing the death of her son and husband. Following this, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Florina De Fonseca\|Florina De Fonseca]] was publically executed for the people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Isyen Lenora\|Isyen Lenora]] to sate some of their vengeance against the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] while the war slowed down.
