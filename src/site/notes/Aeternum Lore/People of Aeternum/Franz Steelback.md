### Summary

A Dwarven store owner from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]]. He believes he has a talent for song, and usually greets most customers that way. Precious few are those that tell him that it drives the sales away.

### Appearance and Personality

He’s a very materialistic man and hardly gives any discounts to the locals let alone the rare visitor.
