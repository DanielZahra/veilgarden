### Summary

As highest ranking general of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]], Gaius wields the Artifact that holds extreme power and has not known defeat in a challenge of power.

### Appearance and Personality

His [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Jotun\|Jotun]] and [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Fiend/Fiendspawn\|Fiendspawn]] heritage makes him a scary figure on any battlefield. He cuts through regular soliders as if they were made of paper.
