### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Gangi Brimsteel ‘The Emberlord’\|Gangi Brimsteel ‘The Emberlord’]] is the current Emberlord of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] and comes from the long noble line of rules that have resided in Ember for as long as anyone remembers.

He is generally loved by the people of Emberdeep and his and his family’s rule has rarely been questioned.
