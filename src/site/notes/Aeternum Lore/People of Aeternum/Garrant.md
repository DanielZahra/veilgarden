### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Garrant\|Garrant]] is a skilled hunter with both the bow and sword. He has been the boon to all martial training and prowess within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]] for the last decade. he is a serious and dedicated man. Soft-spoken and on the quiet side, except when he is drilling his students. There is rarely a week where [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Garrant\|Garrant]] does not go out to hunt the best game for the Grove. 

He takes his job as a Warden very seriously and whenever [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Selenna\|Selenna]] is out and about you will see [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Garrant\|Garrant]] shadowing her every move.

### Skills

Garrant has mastered over a dozen weapons and fighting styles and usually carries multiple weapons on his person at any time. Some of the Mz’LUpakke people are almost scared of his way of fighting as it’s deadly and decisive.
