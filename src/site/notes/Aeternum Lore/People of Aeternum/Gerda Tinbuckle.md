### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Gerda Tinbuckle\|Gerda Tinbuckle]] runs both the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep Notable Places/Lost and Found Antiques\|Lost and Found Antiques]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep Notable Places/Candlelight Shelter\|Candlelight Shelter]]. She mainly runs the shop to suppler the shelter and the kids with enough money to eat, study and grow into fine people.

She doesn’t do this alone and it is known that a number of donations keep this operation running.
