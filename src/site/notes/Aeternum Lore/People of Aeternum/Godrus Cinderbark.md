### Summary

A young Ashen Elf from Vakkengard. He works in the Militia and is a farmer in out of the outlying farms.

He is reliable and very loyal but has a tendency to drink too much when he's stressed

### **Relationships and History**

After being part of the Liberation of Vakkengard and the village joining the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], he took his job as a Militia more seriously and started training people properly as they could no longer rely on the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] for support.

He later took a good example out of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Deron V Duskember\|Deron V Duskember]] and took the role as Milita Captain in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)\|Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)]] and by virtue of this he’s formally also part of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Silver Eagles\|Silver Eagles]].
