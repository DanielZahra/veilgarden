### Summary

Owner of the main general stores in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]] and single-handedly responsible for the majority of the trade that happens in a good part of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Northern Stormreach/Northern Stormreach\|Northern Stormreach]]. Most people know him as a greedy man, however they respect that he does a lot of work to bring in the money for food and support for [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]].
