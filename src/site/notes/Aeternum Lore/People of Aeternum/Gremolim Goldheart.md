### Summary

One of the leaders of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Spinners\|Spinners]]. A jacked up [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Jotun\|Jotun]] walking the plains and snow of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]] tells stories and helps everyone in need. He helped [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] in his time of need and introduced him to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Spinners\|Spinners]], which in turn started his journey.

### **Relationships and History**

Spending most of his time as a Spinner, he eventually joined [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Culinary Mythweavers\|Culinary Mythweavers]].
