### Summary

Grevan is rather famous around [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]], not many [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Elementians\|Elementians]] [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Beastkin/Felidari\|Felidari]] around town. He moved north from the south of Bhadir because it was too damn hot, the cold at howlcrest works well with his hot personality. He is a hunter and also sells his services as a bodyguard and known to be short-tempered.

Spends most of his time in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/The Howling Brew\|The Howling Brew]] drinking or working as the bouncer.
