### Origin

Leif was once a well-loved instructor in his small village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]], known for some wisdom and knowledge of lore and history. In his younger days, after leaving his hometown of Orcaglacier Port he spent some years traveling and experiencing life as an adventurer, he then eventually moved to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]] lived a quiet, contented life with his wife, Eira, and their two children Spot and Grimes, enjoying the peace of their simple existence. For Leif, the world outside their village was mostly memories and stories, not something that intruded on his daily life.

But that all changed on a cold winter night, many years ago. A group of ruthless bandits descended upon Leif and his family as they were traveling back to the village. The vicious bandits left death and destruction in their wake. Leif did not stand a chance despite his efforts to protect his family, he was powerless against the overwhelming force. He watched in horror as his beloved Eira and children were taken from him, their lives snuffed out in a senseless act of violence, Lief only survived by running like a coward in the darkness of the woods.

The grief was overwhelming, and for years, Leif withdrew from the world, retreating into his books and the safety of his home. He became a shadow of the man he once was, his days consumed by sorrow and regret. He cursed his own weakness, the inability to protect those he loved, and vowed never to let himself care for others again, for fear of losing them as well.

Yet as the years passed, something within Leif began to change. The pain of his loss did not diminish, but it began to transform into something else—a quiet resolve. He realized that he could not spend the rest of his days hiding from the world, letting the memory of his family fade into the mists of time. The world outside was still full of dangers, and there were still people who needed protection.

Later in life, Leif made a decision that surprised even himself. Immersing himself in the teachings of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)\|Foristra (Goddess of Nature)]] and delving into the history of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Beastkin/Beastkin\|Beastkin]] 
he learnt there is much more he wants to know and see. He would not allow his remaining years to be spent in hiding. Instead, he would step into the light once more, using his wisdom and knowledge to protect those around him. He knew he was not a warrior, but he believed that sometimes, the greatest battles are won with the mind, not the sword.

Leif decided to be the silent guardian for those in need. Though he still speaks little, his presence is reassuring, and his wisdom invaluable. He carries the memory of his family with him, using their loss as a reminder of why he must continue to fight for the safety and well-being of others. He may be shy and reserved, but when the time comes to stand up for what is right, Leif does so with the strength of a man who has known the deepest pain and emerged with a renewed purpose.

### Combat Style

Leif is an older man and sometimes it shows in combat, instead of chasing enemies around the battle field he summons Arcane Weapons to battle his foes. He has some skill in melee combat with his trusty Bo Staff, Thunderstruck. He has mastered the art of shaping lightning can turn his Thunderstruck into a lightning rod that spits sparks of lightning towards his foes.

He loves to stay in the middle of his companions to aid those who need it but is happy to take a hit for anyone if the need arises. Often chanting [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)/Foristra (Goddess of Nature)\|Foristra (Goddess of Nature)]]’s name whom he favors amounts the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/The Twelve (Gods)/The Twelve (Gods)\|The Twelve (Gods)]].
