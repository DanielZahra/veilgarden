### Summary

Hakim was born in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Zedrana\|Zedrana]]. His comes from a long line of gifted people that have worked with the Sultansworn of Zedrana. 

Hakim has a thick Zedranian accent. He is relatively quiet and usually waits before speaking. Dedicated to this work he never married or had kids but he comes from a rather large family.

> [!tip] 💡
> The Sultansworn are a group of gifted warriors with magical abilities that act as the royal guard of the Sultan.

The Soudsi family had a mixed heritage and this has allowed them to manipulate time through strange powers.

Hakim left Zedrana on a mission to Umbra. He was meant to track an artifact that the Sultan wanted. After 10 years of looking Hakim had given up, however this is where [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cassius Valentine/Cassius Valentine\|Cassius Valentine]] gave him a hint to where to look. 

The initial mission started when hakim was 20. Hakim has travelled around umbra for all this time looking for anything that would give him clues, he spent most of the time in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] gathering information.

### Events

He joins the party with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Caeldin Ironfaith\|Caeldin Ironfaith]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brundir Stonehammer\|Brundir Stonehammer]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Zarun\|Zarun]] at [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dune’s Crossing\|Dune’s Crossing]] where he is working as a guide with different parties as they explore the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Uralgatoa’s Dunes\|Uralgatoa’s Dunes]].

During the events of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists\|Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists]] [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Hakim Soudsi\|Hakim Soudsi]] sacrificed himself to tilt the void power and close a rift in reality using what little time magic mana he had left in him. He made his mark as a hero of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] even tough his name is rarely spoken openly.
