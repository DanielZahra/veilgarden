### Summary

Owner of the Salty Brew in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Flaxen Canyon\|Flaxen Canyon]], Helda is a tough ex-miner who now runs a taven for the canyon’s people. Mining is a tough and unforgiving job, it is not uncommon for miners to also drink heavily when they are off work. Helda, knows this and isn’t new to having to shove some urly customers out of her bar.
