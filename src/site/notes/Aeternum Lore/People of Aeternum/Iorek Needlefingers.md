### Summar

Iorek is one of the leaders of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]]. Not much is known about him as a man of his status is not meant to be a public figure.

### **Relationships and History**

He has met with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] a couple of times and has given Varris a couple of jobs.
