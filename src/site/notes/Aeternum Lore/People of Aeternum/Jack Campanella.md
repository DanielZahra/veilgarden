### Summary

A young man from the poor people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].  Sick of the Fighting caused by the** ****[[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]]****. **The people fund the wars by paying taxes and then they die for lofty ideals of some richer folk.

He is one of the leaders of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Copper Rats\|Copper Rats]]. Or at least seems to be on.

His goal was to set [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] free of the Empire. And started planning a revolt.

He wants the people to govern themselves, not some senate that is hundreds of miles away.

### Appearance and Personality

Red Haired, torn clothing, tattoos, ropes, gear. Cuts and bruises. He appears to be your typical cutpurse or street rat. However, he exudes an aura of authority and command.

### **Relationships and History**

He disrupted the Execution of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cameron Mavis\|Cameron Mavis]], this action launched [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] into a revolt. He managed to convince the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] to aid in the rebellion and not side with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]]. This series of events caused the Empire to lose control of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] which slowly spiralled into the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]].

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Jack Campanella\|Jack Campanella]] still quietly leads the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Copper Rats\|Copper Rats]], but they are now a much more peaceful organization and left their thieving behind to help out the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]]. They are building themselves a new home in Copper Alley from the ashes of ‘Little Messana’ in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].
