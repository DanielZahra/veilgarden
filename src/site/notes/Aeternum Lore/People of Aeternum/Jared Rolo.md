### Summary

Coming from a long line of Cartographers and a descendent of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Magnus Rolo\|Magnus Rolo]]. Jared’s main interest was to travel around Aeternum and map as much of it as possible.

Joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] as a novice with the hopes to climb in the ranks and manage to make the best maps in Aeternum.

### Appearance and Personality

A quiet, polite and respectful man, with an eye for detail. He has an acute fear of monsters and fighting in general and tries to stay out of trouble. 

However, he’s not against going into trouble to explore and find new places.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

He always carries a full set and backups of cartography gear to make sure he can map everything he can see.

### **Relationships and History**

In 3213 NA he joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] when they were preparing to set out for a long journey at sea towards the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Black Lagoon\|Black Lagoon]].

Having stayed in [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]]’s group he kept working with him and learning more about mapping, and chasing his goal of creating an updated map of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Runaria\|Runaria]] and perhaps, beyond. 

He moved to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]] with the rest of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] that were in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].
