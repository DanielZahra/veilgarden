### Summary

Kaito Lightbinder has been living his entire life in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Himoari\|Himoari]] alongside the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Hangetsu Clan\|Hangetsu Clan]]. His house was situated out in the woods as he preferred to be mostly left alone.

He practiced the study of stars from this studio and was mostly treated as an eccentric. Unfortunately no other Pale Elves remained as his family died in a fire accident in 3207.

### **Relationships and History**

Having met [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]], the 2 Astromancers shared knowledge and together dived deeper into the Fox constellation, which was not fully understood or discovered by the Astromancers at [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]].

Using this as a precursor to do more, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] convinced [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kaito Lightbinder\|Kaito Lightbinder]] to pack his things and go for a long journey around [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yamagato/Yamagato\|Yamagato]] sharing the knowledge of Astromancy with as many people as possible.

This was a godsend for Kaito as it gave him a new purpose to find happiness in his life.

Eventually with his work done in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yamagato/Yamagato\|Yamagato]], kaito took a long trip to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]] to visit the new Astromancy schools.
