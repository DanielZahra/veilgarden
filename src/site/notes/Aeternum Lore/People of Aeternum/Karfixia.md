### Summary

A charismatic [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/High Draconian\|High Draconian]] who had her Dragonspawn ‘pets’ escape from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dragon Lands\|Dragon Lands]]. She later tracked them down in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Cobalt Forest\|Cobalt Forest]] and spent almost a decade playing queen. Her kin caused a lot of damage to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Cobalt Forest\|Cobalt Forest]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Gazzna Bramblenock\|Gazzna Bramblenock]] the Azuresage eventually sent a group of adventurers to deal with the problem. 

The adventurers ended up dealing and with problem, killing [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Karfixia\|Karfixia]] in the process.
