### Summary

The Irontoe family original came from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] in Cerulia. They brought with them a lot mining and underground farming techniques that have helped the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] find resources where it seemed there weren’t any.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Karlatan Irontoe\|Karlatan Irontoe]] was govenring the town of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]] managing both shopping, fishing, underground farming and working with the Church to handle the Fiend contracts and portals through Saint Shiva’s Cathedral.

A calm and well mannred man, and very diplocatic allowed [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Karlatan Irontoe\|Karlatan Irontoe]] to negotiate a good deal for himself and [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]] at the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/End of the Sixth Valkorian War\|End of the Sixth Valkorian War]] when Witch’s End was placed to be part of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]] but still allowed to openly do business with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]]. [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Karlatan Irontoe\|Karlatan Irontoe]] acts as the representative of Witch’s end in the Valkorian City-States.
