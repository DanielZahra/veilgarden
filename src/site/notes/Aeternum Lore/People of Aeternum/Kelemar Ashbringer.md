### Summary

Father [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kelemar Ashbringer\|Kelemar Ashbringer]], is a priest of Graav who tends to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep Notable Places/Chapel of Graav (Emberdeep)\|Chapel of Graav (Emberdeep)]] in Emberdeep. He performs the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Rite of Sending\|Rite of Sending]] in the Dwarven way as describes in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Live by the hammer, die by the forge\|Live by the hammer, die by the forge]]
