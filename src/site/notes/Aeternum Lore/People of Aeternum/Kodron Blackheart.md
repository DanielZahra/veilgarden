### Summary

Kodron is a Draconian born and raised on the Black Lagoon, a Harsh lifestyle which produced strong people. He’s a seer of his people and his charge is to retell the Legend of the Vermillion Dragon and teach the other Draconians the history of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Black Lagoon\|Black Lagoon]] 

The Legend of the Vermillion Dragon mentioned that once upon a time when the dragon lands still had an emperor, a particularly strong emperor known as the Vermillion King left a legacy in writing (which now has been lost). The story says that he will never be gone and those who inherit his power and come together will bask in his full power's glory at get a chance to be as strong as the emperor himself.  Throughout history several people tried to find others, but as far as they know very few people have even found anyone.

### Appearance and Personality

This green-scaled shelled-draconian seems like a tough older man. He has an eye patch which visible scars underneath. Embedded in his chest is what seems to be a volcanic rock with seared skin around it. Soon after his birth he was involved in an accident and he ended up with a piece of hot rock embedded in him. Somehow he survived and ever since he was very strong in the affinity of magic with Lava.

### **Relationships and History**

After meeting the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] and agreeing to accompany them on their journey to discover more about the Vermillion King’s legend. The party was met by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]]. A deal was made so that [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kodron Blackheart\|Kodron Blackheart]] would accompany Maev until Valkoria’s situation was under control. The [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] were not happy with the outcome but they believed it would be the best for everyone to antagonize the Archmagus.

Having accompanied [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] and also helped her fulfill the prophecy and participate in the vermillion ritual left [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kodron Blackheart\|Kodron Blackheart]] fulfilled, but also empty as both [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] had a major disagreement where [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Brand\|Brand]] took the power of the Emperor for himself and lost control. 

Nobody has seen [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kodron Blackheart\|Kodron Blackheart]] since the battle in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]].
