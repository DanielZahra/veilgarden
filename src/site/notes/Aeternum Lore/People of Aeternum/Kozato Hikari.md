[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Kozato Hikari\|Kozato Hikari]] was adopted into the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Hikari Clan\|Hikari Clan]] when we was still a baby. He was traded to the clan as his father lost a duel with the clan leader of the Hikari. He spent the majority of his life bearing resentment and the people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yamawara\|Yamawara]] reminded him often of this.
