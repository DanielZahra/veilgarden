### Summary

An expert in tea making. She owns a tea and bath house on the outskirts of the  [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Himoari\|Himoari]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Yorimatsu\|Yorimatsu]] regions. Her business serves as a great stop for travelers and those in need of a good night’s rest.

### Appearance and Personality

Ling is soft-spoken, and calm, and walks slowly but seems to keep a vigilant eye on her surroundings. Her [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Celestial/Lightborn\|Lightborn]] heritage gave her bright purple eyes and very pale skin.

### History

She is a very good friend of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Igarashi Clan\|Igarashi Clan]] especially the young estranged son [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]].
