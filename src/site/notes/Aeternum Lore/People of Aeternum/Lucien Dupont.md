### Summary

The Elder in the village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Willowmist\|Willowmist]], is a peaceful soul who will try to help everyone in the village.

As Willowmist is in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Spiralling Woods\|Spiralling Woods]], he’s used to having weary travellers visit the village and his goal is to make sure they are welcomed with open arms.
