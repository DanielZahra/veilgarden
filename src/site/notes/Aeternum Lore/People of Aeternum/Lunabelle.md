### Summary

Lunabelle is a druid from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Azureglade/Azureglade\|Azureglade]] who has taken it upon herself to harmonize the wild life in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Cobalt Forest\|Cobalt Forest]] and usher peace as much as possible. She is often found performing rituals to tame and calm creatures around the glade.

Lunabelle was a close confidant, friend and mentor to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Gazzna Bramblenock\|Gazzna Bramblenock]]. 

Died in a ritual to try and preserve the peace in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Cobalt Forest\|Cobalt Forest]] and buried by a group of adventurers who were present for the failed ritual.
