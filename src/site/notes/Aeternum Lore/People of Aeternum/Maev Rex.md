### Summary

Born into a powerful and prestigious family in Argentcourt, she studied magic all her life. During her college days on a long training trip, she was involved in an accident where most of the students died in a freak accident involving a Fierce Fire Drake attack. Maev was the only one who survived, her originally red hair however turned white due to the stress of the event but her magical powers in the domain of fire magic became much fiercer and dangerous.

After decades of work, she became the Archmagus of War in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]]. She gained considerable power and influence but the power went to her head and she spend a lot of time and funding for her personal quest to gain the vermillion power.

### Vermillion Ritual

Her life’s work to perform the Vermillion Ritual was successfull as she unleashed the [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] unto Runaria, however the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] foiled her plans to take Adam’s power for herself and give [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]] a mayor power. Following [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] rise in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] and the end of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]] Maev was exposed and accused of abuse of power and severe war crimes making her role as an Archmage impossible, she was banished permanently from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lycia\|Lycia]]. She made sure to make it public and swore revenge on the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]]. 

### New Chapter

Following a couple of years of wondering, and exlopring her options and future she joined [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cassius Valentine/Cassius Valentine\|Cassius Valentine]] in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/The Shadowlands\|The Shadowlands]] as a Minister. She manages the relationships between Cassius, the[[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Archmagi of Lycia\|Archmagi of Lycia]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Blood Coin Creed\|Blood Coin Creed]]. But also handles a good chunk of international politics and trouble outside of the borders.
