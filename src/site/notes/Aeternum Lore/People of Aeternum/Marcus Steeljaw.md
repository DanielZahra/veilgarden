### Summary

The guard and army captain of Bellmark, is Tribune Steeljaw. This [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Draconian/Draconians\|Draconians]] got his name Steeljaw from an accident where his lower jaw was torn apart, he survived it and replaced his skin and jaw with a Steel contraption.
Towers over most men, he patrols the streets frequently and is loved by the people.
He comes from a long line of Dracconians that have served the empire. However, he inherited his fighting style from the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Jade Dragoons\|Jade Dragoons]] is back in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dragon Lands\|Dragon Lands]] even though, he never visited himself.

Following the creation of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], Marcus took up the mantle as one of the Councillors and took charge of the defense of the people. He formally joined the ranks of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Silver Eagles\|Silver Eagles]] as they were the official defense force for the new formed state.

### Appearance and Personality

He is a follower of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Iknos (God of Law)\|Iknos (God of Law)]] and respects people and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] ‘s Law. While now a citizen of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], his beliefs in sense of justice have not wavered and stills respects people for who they are and what they do more than where they are from.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

He owns a small dragonling pet that helps him send long-range messages. He fights with a large lance and is extremely quick on his feet, the Dragoon fighting style is rarely seen in the empire.

As the leader of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Silver Eagles\|Silver Eagles]] in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] Marcus was also the owner of the warship called** ‘Argent Spear’ **

### **Relationships and History**

He was involved in a large-scale operation to retrieve the Prince of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]], which ended in success but later led to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]‘s people revolted and gained a sort of independence. 

He got to know the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] very well and accompanied them on the long journey (mostly because he did not trust the new blood). Being a good man, before being a soldier, he abandoned the mantle of the Empire and swore to protect the people. 

Marcus joined the Council of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] to help bolster the soldiers and train them for the tough times to come. Thanks to his training the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]] made it out for he [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]] without stratospheric losses.
