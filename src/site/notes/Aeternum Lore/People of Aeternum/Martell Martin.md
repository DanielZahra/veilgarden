### Summary

A Lycian boy who was born in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Argentcourt\|Argentcourt]]. She was a researcher in Fire Magic and the Elemental Belt and has traveled back and forth to those Planes. 

During his birth there was an unfortunate accident, his mother’s home was set ablaze and she lost her life. Some events say it was an experiment gone wrong, but the magics involved indicate otherwise.

He was special, as he was a Fire [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Elementians\|Elementians]]. [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]], the Archmagus of War took him in, trained and set him to research a legend in relation to fire and dragons.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

He’s extremely skilled with Fire Magic and really hates the water (he cannot swim)

He mostly wearings fireproof leather and it would not be the first time has burned something to the ground.

### **Relationships and History**

He was sick and tired of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] ‘s experiments and he wanted to leave. He stole some supplies and documents and made a run for it.

He was chased all the way to Bellmark's Landing escaping a group of Shikari. He temporarily joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Copper Rats\|Copper Rats]] and made a living, but also managed to burn downa couple of buildings and became a wanted man.

Following which the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]], took a job to capture him, which they did but instead of turning him into the authorities or the Lycian Skihari, they saved his life and listened to his story and agreed to work with him.

As he spent more time with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Martell Martin\|Martell Martin]] decided that he wanted to do more good in his life and joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Quills of Ios\|Quills of Ios]] and devoted a lot of time and energy helping out the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Shi-rai Igarashi\|Shi-rai Igarashi]] carve out a new base within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]. As the Quills traveled to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian’s Seat\|Valkorian’s Seat]], Martell joined [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Flake of Snow\|Flake of Snow]] and used his fiery powers to burn the Forgotten plaguing the island.
