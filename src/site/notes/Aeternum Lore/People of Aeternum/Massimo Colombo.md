### Origin Story

A cocky Tribune who dabbled in alchemy bombs and avoided the front lines as much as possible.

Cruel and happy to step on others, Massimo was not generally liked by most people in or out of the Empire.

### **Relationships and History**

As Massimo, took over [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)\|Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)]] and killed [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]] ‘s sister Marika and caputed his brother [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Deron V Duskember\|Deron V Duskember]], he did not expect to find the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Radegundh V Duskember\|Radegundh V Duskember]] in tow inside the church of Vakkengard to take back the village and get revenge. He was killed in a fiery fight inside the church of the twelve and his head, held by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]] was the symbol the soldiers needed to leave Vakkengard alone.
