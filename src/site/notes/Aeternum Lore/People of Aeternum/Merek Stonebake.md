### Summary

A baker, beer brewer and innkeeper from [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]]. His family is famous for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Cuisine of Aeternum/Food/Ordona’s Black Olive Oil Focaccia\|Ordona’s Black Olive Oil Focaccia]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Cuisine of Aeternum/Food/Ordona’s Stone Lager\|Ordona’s Stone Lager]] 

He admires the workers of the town, especially the mayor.

Rough Golden Skin, Short Wavy black hair shaved on the left side, Blue eyes, and Smiles often as he sees people sampling his food and drink.
