### Summary

Mikey is a smooth talker and knows his way around haggling and making deals for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Bone Collectors\|Bone Collectors]]. He’s used to travelling to dangerous places while not getting involved in much fighting. He never leaves home without his trust hat.

A member of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Bone Collectors\|Bone Collectors]] in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]. He usually is more suited in scouting a job or area before the Bone Collector bounty hunters come in to do the job.

### **Relationships and History**

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Mikey Shirdown\|Mikey Shirdown]] had met the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]] and offered them to work with him if they ever made their way to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].
