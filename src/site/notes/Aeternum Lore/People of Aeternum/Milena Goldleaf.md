### Summary

One of the Companions of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Valkorian Sunstriker\|Valkorian Sunstriker]], the inheritor of the Violin. She has a fallout with Valkorian and tried to cancel her connections with him. Not much is known about this person.

### **Relationships and History**

Most of Milena’s history has been cancelled, but members of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] started unravelling her story and found out that her lineage still runs in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkoria\|Valkoria]] within the Bard College of Duzter’s Step.
