### Summary

Sister Mirelle has as important role in the Sanctuary of Dune’s Crossing. This trading post sees a lot of adventurers and explorers who often need help of the divine. 

She is a kind hearted woman to tries to make sure all those who wish to rest and get guidance from the twelve, they can do so in her sanctuary. She does all this while trying to keep the peace in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dune’s Crossing\|Dune’s Crossing]].
