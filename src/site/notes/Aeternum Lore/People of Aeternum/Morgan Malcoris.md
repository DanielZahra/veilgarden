### Summary

Known as Lord Morgan Malcoris, a Xifjak whose main goal and wants are related to money, power and control. By using smart connections, he managed to get the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] to participate in the Rites of Dominance on his behalf and somehow win.

This earned him the position as Duke of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Darkmoor/Darkmoor\|Darkmoor]].
