### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Morrgan\|Morrgan]] is the main carpenter in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]] and a towering figure, known for his strong build and capable hands. 
In his youth, **Morrgan **was the woodcutter for his own father before taking on the role of carpenter, ensuring the family tradition endured.
