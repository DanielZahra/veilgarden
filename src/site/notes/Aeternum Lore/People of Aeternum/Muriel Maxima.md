### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Muriel Maxima\|Muriel Maxima]] lived a relatively normal life on Nirvana among the Deva until her family was brutally mudered by a [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Celestial/Seraphim\|Seraphim]]. Unable to to anything due to the difficult politics between the two worlds in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lumerium (Plane of Light)/Lumerium (Plane of Light)\|Lumerium (Plane of Light)]], she abandoned everything and went on a quest for revenge. In her journey she met [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cassius Valentine/Cassius Valentine\|Cassius Valentine]] who struck a simple deal with her, she would help her exact revenge and she would serve within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/The Shadowlands\|The Shadowlands]]. This was child’s play for Cassius. After some years within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Spires of Enigma/Spires of Enigma\|Spires of Enigma]] [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Muriel Maxima\|Muriel Maxima]] was promoted to a Minister of Security to make sure the people of the Shadowlands are safe and protected from each other. She has been working closely with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] who handles the external problems.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Muriel Maxima\|Muriel Maxima]] is heavily involved with the training and missions dispensing for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Blackrose\|Blackrose]].
