### Summary

[[NOTION_PAGE:e0b35757-9b6c-4112-9e3e-dbbd93f6cbc8\|NOTION_PAGE:e0b35757-9b6c-4112-9e3e-dbbd93f6cbc8]] joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ironstone Consortium\|Ironstone Consortium]] as a runesmith. He is a man of very little words and when he does speak he is concise and to the point.

Following the Events at the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Amethyst Sea\|Amethyst Sea]], Rune a new role within the consortium and ended up in the village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]] in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stormreach/Stormreach\|Stormreach]] working as an Armorsmith. Never staying in one place for too long, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/OHI4234 ‘Rune’\|OHI4234 ‘Rune’]] moved to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] on a new mission and looking for work.
