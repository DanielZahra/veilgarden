### Summary

A good mannered [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Dwarf/Dwarves\|Dwarves]]  that works for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Children of Leven\|Children of Leven]]. He’s the captain of the Red Swallow. As a captain within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Children of Leven\|Children of Leven]] he is privy to most of their inner working about the family and striking business deals and making money around the world.

### Appearance and Personality

Loves stroking his beard as he talks. Has a very-very loud laugh.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

Odir is a skilled Captain that has sailed the seas of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Runaria\|Runaria]] for many years.

The Red Swallow is a wooden sailing ship, modeled after the traditional fishing boats of Bhadir. Built traditionally with a wooden hull, it is designed to be a safe and comfortable cruising vessel. **The Red Swallow** measures 25 meters in length, with a beam of 3 meters, and a draft of 1 meter. The main sail is a 50 square meters, and the boat can carry up to 20 passengers. The interior of the boat is simple, with a small galley, sleeping berths, and a small head.

### **Relationships and History**

Odir met the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] as Avnon wanted to know more about the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Children of Leven\|Children of Leven]]. They shared stories and agreed that someday they would visit the [[NOTION_PAGE:fd15bfbd-32ae-4130-b70c-39abc5acbd0a\|NOTION_PAGE:fd15bfbd-32ae-4130-b70c-39abc5acbd0a]].

In the meantime, Odir was happy to offer his ship’s services to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] against good payment. One trip took them to Gohondar’s Fall and the other to Valkorian’s Seat.

After the rebellion in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]], Odir took another long job with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]], this time, it was to take them to the treacherous seas of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Black Lagoon\|Black Lagoon]].

Having his crew threatened by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Maev Rex\|Maev Rex]] left Captain[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Odir Steelbranch\|Odir Steelbranch]], with a bad taste in his mouth and decided that working with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] was more trouble than it was worth. He escorted them home and remain in their good graces but didn’t take more work from them.
