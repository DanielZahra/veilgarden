### Summary

Pan was one of the Firstborn [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Unliving/Risen\|Risen]] to venture out into the world and explore on behalf of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Cassius Valentine/Cassius Valentine\|Cassius Valentine]]. His jounrey was perilous as he found that himself and his elf were treated as monsters and often hunted just for their looks.

Pan was asked to find [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Oberon’s Prism\|Oberon’s Prism]] in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Umbra\|Umbra]] by his master. However as another group had gained the artifact, Pan was set to follow the group and keep an eye on them form the shadows.

When disaster struck during the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists\|Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Books/Books of Aeternum/Legacy of the Blackfists]], Pan remained involved until the bitter end pushing Cassius’ agenda. Eventually after the destruction and combat was over, Pan returned to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Spires of Enigma/Spires of Enigma\|Spires of Enigma]] empty handed but with a large amount of information about [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Oberon’s Prism\|Oberon’s Prism]].
