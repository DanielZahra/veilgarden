### Summary

A [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Azzatan/Jotun\|Jotun]] man running a bakery, known to feed to poor in need. His bakery is situated in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]‘s Ditch Quarter. Some say that he barely makes any money as most of the food he makes is given away.

Pedro has affiliated himself with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Copper Rats\|Copper Rats]] shares similar goals, that is to help the poor downtrodden of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].

### Appearance and Personality

While being a peaceful man, Pedro has been down to beat down and bash some skills when necessary. His size and strength more than make up for his lack of training in fighting.

### **Relationships and History**

When the Ditch was burned down by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Florina De Fonseca\|Florina De Fonseca]] and her Guards, Pedro’s Bakery survived but most of the people moved out.

He temporarily moved that the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] ‘s Coffee shop to help the people in need in that area.

Through out the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], Pedro took a ‘seat’ in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] council mainly to make sure to protect the people of the city. Having seen the change in [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Marcus Steeljaw\|Marcus Steeljaw]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Klotan V Duskember\|Klotan V Duskember]] coming in with more knowhow about leadership Pedro slowly stepped away and returned to his bakery a proud man. He is still preparing large portions of bread of food for the poor of the city and there is never a day where his bakery’s chimney is not running.
