### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Prilla\|Prilla]] is already showing talent for the work her family does in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Lupakku’s Claw\|Lupakku’s Claw]]. Daughter of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Breggo\|Breggo]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Dinna\|Dinna]] and sister to [[NOTION_PAGE:5aaa07b8-78e0-4e62-9f96-4c45ffb4ad73\|NOTION_PAGE:5aaa07b8-78e0-4e62-9f96-4c45ffb4ad73]].

Prilla has already shown that she can get what she wants with a smile and wink. She helps around the Claw to make sure everything runs smoothly. She adores her mother [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Dinna\|Dinna]] and treats her as her idol.
