### Summary

Known as the Steel Mage. A retired powerful general and mage of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]], has a tower in the Horizon District, [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] known as the Steel Spire, since it is made of metal.

He mostly keeps to himself and it’s a rare occasion that he is seen outside.

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

He got the moniker of Steel Mage as he’s an Earth Human [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Elementians\|Elementians]] adept with shaping earth and metal.

During the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], it became known that [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Quintus Alberic\|Quintus Alberic]] was not only a general in the army but also a member of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] and acted as the bodyguard of the previous master who was murdred by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Draco Palantine\|Draco Palantine]]. [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Quintus Alberic\|Quintus Alberic]] joined the armies of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]] and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] on this attack on [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Falls of Gohondar\|Falls of Gohondar]], swearing to get vengeance.
