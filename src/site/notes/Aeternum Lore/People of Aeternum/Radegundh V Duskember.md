### Summary

**Birth and Family:**

Radegundh V. Duskember, an Ashen Elf, was born in **3190 NA** in **Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)**, a small Valkorian village built between the colossal ribcage of an ancient Titan. His family was well-off and respected:

- **Father:** *Klotan V. Duskember* – a book trader, scholar, swordsman, and village teacher.
- **Mother:** *Merovin (Verdurerni)* – gentle and artistic, played the bukkehorn, and tended the family’s small farm.
- **Siblings:** *Deron* (10 years older) and *Marika* (6 years older), both loving and protective.

**Childhood and Tragedy:**

Radegundh’s early years were happy and full of curiosity—books, music, and stories of Titans surrounded him. His mother’s death from illness when he was **six** marked his first great loss and deeply shaped his distrust of the gods, whose aid never came. The family grew closer; Marika took on a motherly role, and Klotan buried his grief in work and teaching.

**The Duskember Line and the Voror Bond:**

The Duskembers inherited both a vast **book collection** and a **sacred Titanborn bond**.

The mysterious “**V.**” in their names stands for **Voror**, a nomadic Titanborn lineage that aided their Ashen Elf ancestors centuries ago. The alliance was sealed with signet rings bearing the Voror rune, symbolizing eternal kinship and shared history between their peoples.

**Disillusionment and Beliefs:**

Through Klotan’s teachings, Radegundh learned of the gods’ failures—especially during the Ashen Elves’ suffering and the *Burning of the Evergreen Glade*. These stories fostered doubt and anger toward most of the Twelve, though he retained reverence for **Duztar**, god of craft and creation. His later love for smithing would strengthen this connection.

**Soul Dreaming:**

Radegundh was diagnosed with **Soul Dreaming (Soul Cycle)**—a rare condition allowing him to relive the memories of ancient beings through his soul’s reincarnated fragments.

- **Age 4:** First Dream — a vivid memory of a **Fire Titanborn**, Kagu Kozuke.
- **Age 6:** Second Dream — triggered by his mother’s death; memories of the first became clearer afterward.
- **Age 11:** Third Dream — after killing a bandit in self-defense, he dreamt from atop a volcano overlooking a Titanborn homeland resembling **Yamagato**, where that race had long been exterminated.

Upon learning of their extinction, Radegundh dyed his hair **dark red** to honour his Titanborn ancestor and began keeping a detailed **journal** of his dreams and life events.

**Character and Growth:**

He grew to be inquisitive, strong-willed, and passionate about history, craftsmanship, and the Titanborn. His friendship with **Godrus V. Cinderbark**, his fellow Ashen Elf and childhood companion, shaped his youth. The events of his early life—his mother’s death, divine silence, and the revelation of his Soul Dreams—instilled in him a complex faith: distrustful of gods, but reverent toward creation, ancestry, and the enduring power of memory.

### **Relationships and History**

Having gone back this his home town with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]], he found his village at the mercy of the Empire. He shaved his flaming orange hair to help mask himself and headed in ready to help his family.
