### Summary

As Nightingale of the Ebon Syndicate, she has the guild's interest at heart. Her main role in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]] is,  acting as the Fence and getting small-time thieves to earn money for the guild.

This Dwarven woman must have had a strange accident or has strange her as she has a full grown beard.

Her Favorite quote: **"Everything lost can be found, and everything found can be sold."**

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

She has a Black Sparrow pet named Fletch, which delivers her letters.

### **Relationships and History**

An acquaintance of [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] and therefore his son, she agreed to start giving some light jobs to allow him to ease into the guild's folds.
