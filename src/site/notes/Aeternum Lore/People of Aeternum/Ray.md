### **Origin**

Ray is the son of Flash and Dew, well known hunters in the village of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Howlcrest/Howlcrest\|Howlcrest]]. When he was barely a few months old, his parents went on a hunt for a dangerous beast that threatened the village. The beast was more ferocious that anything they had faced before, but together they managed to bring it down. Unfortunately, both of them were grievously injured in the process, as the beast carried a nasty disease that led to Dew’s death moments after the battle was won. Flash managed to make his way back to the village with Dew’s body, but a few days after he also succumbed to the disease, leaving Ray to be raised by his only remaining relative, his grandma Glim.

Glim was herself an excellent huntress in her day, having taught her son Flash and many up and coming hunters as well, including Dew. Ray was keen to follow in his heroic parents’ and grandma’s footsteps, and Glim taught him from a young age how to hunt. Though he was still small in stature, Ray was quickly learning the ways and making a name for himself. Glim often recounted fanciful stories about heroic hunters and archers of legend, and Ray loved every single one. His favourite was the tale of a Fellintran elf called Vyllis who supposedly shot an arrow straight at a shooting star and received a great boon, after which he had many great adventures before settling down to pass on the knowledge of his gifts to new generations. Ray often found himself keeping an eye on the stars at night in case he spotted a shooting star.

When he was just 14 years of age, Glim’s age caught up with her and she died peacefully. Moments before her death, with Ray sitting by her deathbed, she entrusted him with her old shortbow and told him that she knew he would use it well. Though he was left alone without family at a young age, Ray felt a warmth in his heart that his grandma and parents were with him and he would one day make them proud. He started taking on hunts of his own even though he was young, and could keep up with the more veteran hunters of the village.

A year later, at 15 years old, Ray was on his way back from a hunt and set up camp for the night. As he was resting by the campfire, he looked up at the sky as he often did at night, and spotted a star moving swiftly through the sky. Instinctively, Ray grabbed his grandma’s bow and swiftly notched an arrow and aimed at the sky. Feeling that familiar warmth within him, he released the arrow almost automatically, and watched the arrow fly towards the sky and vanish from sight. He immediately felt stupid at the thought that an arrow could ever really reach the sky and hit a star from such a distance, but suddenly he saw the shooting star plummet straight down like a bird in flight having just been shot down. Unsure if he was just imagining things, he walked in the direction of where the star might have fallen, and after some time he saw a light in a clearing. A small chunk of glowing rock stood in the middle, with an arrow stuck straight through it. Shocked in disbelief, Ray approached the rock and grabbed it in his palms. It was roughly the size of a grown man’s fist and had a slight warmth to it. The warmth of the rock seemed to seep straight through him and merged with the warmth from his family, and the rock was suddenly no more: it had vanished like it was never there to begin with!

Thinking he might have imagined the whole thing, Ray made his way back to camp and fell asleep. The next day, he woke up and remembered the events of the previous day, and figured that he probably just dreamt it all. As he was packing his gear to make his way back to the village, a feral wolf appeared staring silently at him. Snatching the bow and readying an arrow, Ray felt that inner warmth surge from within as the arrow lit up with a glow similar to the glowing rock from the previous day. Releasing the arrow in surprise, it shot with a stream of light that even in daylight seemed to radiate energy. The wolf was as shocked as Ray as the arrow hit it and bathed it in light that left a glow. It jumped off to scamper away but a column of light descended from the heavens and barred its way. Ray wasn’t even sure if he had summoned that himself or if it had just appeared out of thin air, but he felt he could somehow control this light. Feeling bad for the terrified wolf in front of him, he let go of the column of light and approached the injured wolf slowly. He felt a strong feeling of compassion within him that seemed to surge outwards towards the wolf, and he watched as the arrow slid out of the wolf’s flank and his wound sealed. The wolf, feeling reinvigorated but still terrified at the strange magic, fled as quickly as it could, and left Ray wondering whether this newfound power was the boon that Vyllis had supposedly received as well.

Ray returned to the village with a newfound resolve to use his new gifts to make his grandma and parents proud. He would protect the village and those in need, just like Vyllis did in the stories. Feeling closer than ever to his grandma, he took her bow and infused it with some of the light from within, calling it Glimmer of Dawn, his grandma’s birth name, in honour of her memory.

### Personality

Although Ray has had a somewhat tragic upbringing, he was brought up in a happy environment by his grandma, and this led him to always have a smiling and cheerful disposition. People in the village love Ray, and anyone who meets him is put at ease by his pleasant and friendly manners. He was also raised to be kind and caring, though he is still fairly young and has not experienced much of the cruelty and deceit of the outside world, so he can be fairly naïve.
