### Summary

Remi is is a Fairy who will often disguise himself as a [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Gnome/Gnomes\|Gnomes]]. He has been travelling around the worlds playing [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Rocktoise Spin\|Rocktoise Spin]] and challenging most people to games.

He is a sore loser who does not like it when he loses, especially in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Rocktoise Spin\|Rocktoise Spin]].

His current goal to recruit people to join the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Spin To Win\|Spin To Win]].
