### Summary

The current butler of the Stonecutters, comes from a common family and has been working with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Fredrick Stonecutter\|Fredrick Stonecutter]] for as long as he can remember.

### Appearance and Personality

Well dressed and knows his way around the entire household, Renley is not the nicest of people at a glace but his true personality shines in when it comes to working, with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Fredrick Stonecutter\|Fredrick Stonecutter]]. Beneath the stonecold face lies a good heart.
