### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Renogvin Blackfist\|Renogvin Blackfist]] is one of the older generation of Blackfists and uncle to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Thordin Blackfist\|Thordin Blackfist]].
[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Renogvin Blackfist\|Renogvin Blackfist]] is a peerless smith and has taken the role of overseeing most of the works being done in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep Notable Places/Blackfist Arms\|Blackfist Arms]].
