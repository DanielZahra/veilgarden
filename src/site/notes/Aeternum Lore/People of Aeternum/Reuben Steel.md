### Summary

A member of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/The Hoard\|The Hoard]], Reuben always wanted to be a merchant and his Fiendish heritage did not stop him from becoming a charming fellow with a way around money.

Get Richer and allow commerce to flourish. He is working to way through the ranks of the company by working around the Inner Valkorian Sea.

A green-skinned [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Fiend/Fiendspawn\|Fiendspawn]] man with. Big-bellied and extremely friend. He is always ready for a drink and a good talk about money.

### **Relationships and History**

In the 6th Valkorian War he set out to start working on a trading line within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].

With the connection that he built in the war and working closely with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]], Reuben was the first supplier to work in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian’s Seat\|Valkorian’s Seat]] which allowed [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/The Hoard\|The Hoard]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Reuben Steel\|Reuben Steel]] to setup the best trade routes in the center of a new geo-political region within the city of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dawnsreach\|Dawnsreach]].
