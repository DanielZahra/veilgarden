### Summary

A troublesome little Gnome to joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] to make a living in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]. Not much is known about her except that she has her own gang and is best friends with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Moira Lockeheart\|Moira Lockeheart]].

Having helped [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] with the coup in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] she is now busy preparing the rewards.
