### Summary

The elder of the wardens of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]], skilled in spirit magic and healing, she is often treated as a mother hen for many of the people in the Grove. [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Roweena\|Roweena]] is a patient woman who spends most of her time teaching the young ones the ways of the covenant. She does not hesitate to use her staff to protect her people. 

Many of the people in the Grove, can feel that she has never recovered from the searing and those that know her well, will know that she feels responsible for the deaths of Rokku and Mlikki. This however has never stopped her from showing up for those in need. 

While her work at the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/The Den\|The Den]] keeps her busy, she is often always seen at the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Fangroot\|Fangroot]]
