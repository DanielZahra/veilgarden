### Summary

Solera was a relatively unique [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Celestial/Seraphim\|Seraphim]]  who travelled to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Umbra\|Umbra]] and remained there for a very long time sharing the word and light of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Solas (God of Light)\|Solas (God of Light)]].

The people of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Umbra\|Umbra]] were used to living in the underground and didn’t heed much the word of the god of light. However [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Saint Solera\|Saint Solera]] had aquired [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Solera’s Bloom\|Solera’s Bloom]], a powerful artifact that allowed her to create a minor star from the **Celestial Aureole within the underground of Cerulia.**

### Death

Unftunately after Solera’s passing [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Solera’s Bloom\|Solera’s Bloom]] was lost for many ages until the year 3210 where a group from the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Order of the Wayfarers\|Order of the Wayfarers]] managed to track the relic down. However, they found that when returned to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]] the relic was not what it once was.
