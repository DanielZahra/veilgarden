### Summary

Samwise was one of the first members to set up shop and his ship to work with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Morningtide Sails\|Morningtide Sails]]. He runs a fishing boat that helps people travel between the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], especially those traveling the Inner Valkorian Sea.

A good man with a good heart, passionate about fishing, and an expert sailor.

His Sailing ship is called, **The Mirielle**, for his departed wife.
