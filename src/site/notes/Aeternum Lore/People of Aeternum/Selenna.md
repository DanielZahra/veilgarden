### Summary

With the last [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Spirit Guardian\|Spirit Guardian]] lost, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Selenna\|Selenna]] has been mostly spending time in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/The Den\|The Den]] controlling the mist as well as she can while also preparing [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Rikke\|Rikke]] for his journey.
