### Summary

Shiva was a powerful Duke who abandoned her post as Duke to travel Aeternum to spread the faith of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)\|Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)]]. She left [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Tenebrium (Plane of Darkness)\|Tenebrium (Plane of Darkness)]] after the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/Second Planar Divine War\|Second Planar Divine War]], to share loudly a message that Darkness and [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Fiend/Fiends\|Fiends]]s are not all evil or bad. Just like the people the in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Prime Material Planes\|Prime Material Planes]], they can be what they want.

She was also a champion of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Fiend/Fiendspawn\|Fiendspawn]] and defened them against discrimination they recievd almost evverywhere. She often avoided the topic of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Celestial/Celestials\|Celestials]]s as the Planar Divine war was too fresh.

She spent a lot of time in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkoria\|Valkoria]] which was a country less than 3 centuries old at the time. Every twilight, she held a sermon in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)\|Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)]]’s name and eventually gained popularity with the people, even more so after the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] approved her teachings. 

### **History**

Unfortunately in the year 2640 near [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]], where Shiva often held sermons, a group of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Luminals\|Luminals]] extremists, assaulted one of her sermons murdering almost all of the people there and crucifying Shiva to end her life. It is said that Shiva while being powerful, did not fight back against the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Luminals\|Luminals]] to prove their ways wrong.

By the year 2680, the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Church of the Twelve\|Church of the Twelve]] built a Cathedral in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]] and declared Shiva a Saint of the Twelve, dedicating the Cathedral to her.
