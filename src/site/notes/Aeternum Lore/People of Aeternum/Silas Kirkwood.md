### Summary

Silas one of the prodical sons of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/House Kirkwood\|House Kirkwood]], is a skilled warlock and has managed hundreds if not thousands of Fiend Contracts for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]].

He works diligently to maintain a good business deal and operational gate to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Darkmoor/Darkmoor\|Darkmoor]] within [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]], of course this is confidential and resides in a high security area within the Cathedral of Saint Shiva.

Silas, is an ambitious man and a perfectionist, which are good traits to have when drafting contracts.

### **History**

In the year 3213 when [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Witch’s End\|Witch’s End]] became a part of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]], Silas worked hand in hand with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Karlatan Irontoe\|Karlatan Irontoe]] to make sure that their ways and following of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)\|Orinda (Goddess of Darkness)]] is not disrupted by new ideas coming from the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].

They managed to secure a good agreement wich allowed them to keep their way of life, and not having to move evverything a new place.
