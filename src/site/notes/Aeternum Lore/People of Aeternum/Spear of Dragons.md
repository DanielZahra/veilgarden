### Summary

This old [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Beastkin/Beastkin\|Beastkin]] woman has a long history of adventure and travel. She trained with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Jade Dragoons\|Jade Dragoons]] and spent many decades moving around different continents. Eventually, she decided to open her own tavern in a small village in the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]]

### Skills, Abilities and Possessions

Many don’t really believe the stories Spear retells from her tough. But, some have seen her incredibly speedy reflexes come into play when someone was about to drop their food or drink in the Golden Pciness (her tavern).

### **Relationships and History**

It is said that she has a hand in Vakkengard joining the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]] as she was the one to sweet talked the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] into helping. She is still happily running their tavern in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)\|Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)]] and enjoying retirement.
