### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Taaro\|Taaro]] is a bit full of himself but is also a damn good fighter to back his ego, he also takes after [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Qukkiiri\|Qukkiiri]] and has her fiery passion and personality.

He is a weapon master and helps the people [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Mz’Lupakku/Mz’Lupakku\|Mz’Lupakku]] train in almost any weapon they choose.

He was the youngest trainee under [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Garrant\|Garrant]] and followed his teacheings to a T.
