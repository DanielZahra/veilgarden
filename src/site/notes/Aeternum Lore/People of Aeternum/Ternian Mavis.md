### Summary

King Ternian, a mage fighter of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Queendom of Nya’Tallin\|Queendom of Nya’Tallin]] and also the husband of Queen Annabella Mavis, has been a prisoner of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] for many decades. It was believed he was dead until his son the prince attempted a rescue mission which almost succeeded until he [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] stopped the escape over the mountains.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ternian Mavis\|Ternian Mavis]] did not survive the aftermath of the failure of the mission to rescue him, as his almost lifeless body was dragged to [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]].
