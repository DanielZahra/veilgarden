### Summary

A gentle older Dwarf that has lived in the empire his entire life, worked the quarries with the rest of the people of Ordona. married to Fari, but unfortunately, they never had children.

Thori is a worshipper of Iknos and would love to have a chapel built in Ordona, but he doesn't have the funds yet.

He is quick to forgive and is always friendly and open with visitors.

His life goals are to keep Peace and Order in ordona. Loyal to the empire He's quite old so eventually, he would love to find a proper mayor for Ordona.

### **Relationships and History**

He befriended the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] he they in turn entrusted him with a secret that lay beneath [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]]. The Crypt of Forgotten. Later during the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], Thori was approached by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]] with a proposition ,to abandon the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Palantine Empire\|Palantine Empire]] and push [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Ordona\|Ordona]] to be a part of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]]. As much as Thori treated Avnon like family, and wanted to like this idea, he considered this an unsafe and unjust betrayal of the empire, he did however help the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] approach Legatus [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Augusta Fenice\|Augusta Fenice]], from a secret passage. Up to this day, this communication between Thori and the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] was kept a secret.
