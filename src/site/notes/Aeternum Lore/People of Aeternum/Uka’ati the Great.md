### Summary

The name Uka’ati the Great is inherited among the the chieftains of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Lawaiʻa Tribe\|Lawaiʻa Tribe]]. When a decision is needed in the tribe the Great Uka’ati is called to make a verdict.

He is a powerful shaman but spends most of the time smoking and perming rituals for his people to protect them from the wild life that surrounds them.
