### Summary

Uriel has taken it upon himself to travel the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Uralgatoa’s Dunes\|Uralgatoa’s Dunes]] and make sure all the oasis are lit and serve as a hospitable place for travellers. 

As a member of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Pilgrims of the Everlight\|Pilgrims of the Everlight]] he makes do with what he has and spend most of his time on the road.

He is a good man is is known around [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dune’s Crossing\|Dune’s Crossing]] as a reliable guide to the desert.

### History

Saved by Caeldin and group from desert marauders.
