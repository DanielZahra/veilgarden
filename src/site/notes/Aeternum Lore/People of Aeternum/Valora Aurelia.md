### Summary

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Valora Aurelia\|Valora Aurelia]] is the Godbound of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Solas (God of Light)\|Solas (God of Light)]]. Hailing from Nirvana and representing Solas, she has spent the majority of her life making sure that the people of the world have shelter from the darkness and light that grows their crops and allows them to work.
