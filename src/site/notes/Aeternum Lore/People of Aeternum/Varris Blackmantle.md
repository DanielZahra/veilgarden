### Summary

**Varris Blackmantle** is a [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Species/Species Database/Elf/Fellintran Elf\|Fellintran Elf]]  born in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Arborea\|Arborea]], a city built on trees in **Fellintra**. His adoptive father, [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]] , was stranded on the planet after a failed mission, and his mother, **Varael** an elf , took him in as he was an abandoned child. Varris spent his early childhood immersed in elven traditions of fellintra, learning to respect nature, hunt in the woods, and live in harmony with the community. While his mother provided a life of quiet stability, his father, Boris, filled Varris's mind with fantastical stories of absurd adventures on [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkoria\|Valkoria]] and encounters in forbidden places. Eventually, Boris, tired of the tranquil forest life, decided to leave Fellintra. Varris, craving the excitement his father described, sneaked aboard his father’s cart and waited until they were far from shore before revealing himself.

Boris, though surprised, welcomed his son but explained that his work demanded secrecy. He taught Varris the basics of survival, provided him with rudimentary lodging, and tasked him with proving his worth by completing small jobs and favors around the city until he was noticed by the "right people." The only contact Boris provided was [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ragda Frey\|Ragda Frey]] , the owner of the pawn shop "Deep Well." Ragda, an old dwarf lady shrouded in unbelievable legends became Varris's primary source of work, assigning him small tasks like stealing trinkets or carrying contraband. Boris strictly warned Varris that earning Ragda's favor was essential to surviving the city. Varris quickly realized that the city ([[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]) was a harsh landscape dominated by selfishness, greed, and betrayal. As a stranger, he struggled to survive alone and sought out others who were also struggling. He formed a small, durable gang with four core members: [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boobo Darktusk\|Boobo Darktusk]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Rouge Owlfeather\|Rouge Owlfeather]], [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Moira Lockeheart\|Moira Lockeheart]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Avnon Songheart\|Avnon Songheart]].

Rouge and Moira grew up together in the same orphanage and teamed up to survive the city when they aged out. While many others passed through, these four individuals became Varris's core team for nearly every operation.

Eventually Varris join the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] and left on the journey of legends.
