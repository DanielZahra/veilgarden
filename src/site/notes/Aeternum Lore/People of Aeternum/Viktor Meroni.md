### Summary

A shady figure that has been known to take on dark jobs for the syndicate. He embodies the green and ruthlessness of the Syndicate very well.

### **Relationships and History**

He was present in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)\|Vakkengard (Titan’s Rest)]] when the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] took out a tribune of the empire. He and his team did not get involved and seemingly walked away without a scratch as their employer died.

[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Viktor Meroni\|Viktor Meroni]] kept close contact with [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Varris Blackmantle\|Varris Blackmantle]] and subsequently [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Boris Blackmantle\|Boris Blackmantle]], he was a big part of the coup within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]]. At the end of it, Boris awarded him the role of Shadowblade. He is now working within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Valkorian City-States\|Valkorian City-States]].
