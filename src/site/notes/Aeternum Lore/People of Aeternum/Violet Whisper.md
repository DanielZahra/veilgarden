### Summary

Violet was always a bit of a creepy kid. As she grew older her psionic powers grew. Since the psionic wars, it would not be the first time that people were born with strange mind-altering changes or powers.


Sold off as a stable kid and made a run for it, she now survives in the streets of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]]  trying to make a living and also trying to join the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Ebon Syndicate\|Ebon Syndicate]] 

She spent most of her time with her friend [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Aza Velarin\|Aza Velarin]] 

### Appearance and Personality

She tends to stay on her own and doesn't like a lot of people. Her mind powers are hard to control.

### **Relationships and History**

She met and worked with the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] on multiple occasions but was never too friendly with them.

Seeing how the Syndicate conducted business she betrayed them and joined the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Copper Rats\|Copper Rats]] which earned her a bounty of her head.

During the rebellion of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Bellmark’s Landing/Bellmark’s Landing\|Bellmark’s Landing]], somebody collected on the bounty as Violet was found dead in the streets with an arrow stuck in the back of her head.
