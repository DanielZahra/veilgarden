### Summary
[[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Watabe Hikari\|Watabe Hikari]] was the eldest son of the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Hikari Clan\|Hikari Clan]] in 3217 NA when the clan needed to call for the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Customs and Procedures/Gōgi-no-Masa\|Gōgi-no-Masa]]. He was beloved by the people and the eldest of 3 siblings.
