### Summary

Not much is known about Xerxes apart from his complete dominion over the easten part of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Tenebrium (Plane of Darkness)\|Tenebrium (Plane of Darkness)]] and absolute scary powers. it is said that he moves at the speed of light, and doesn’t even need to use weapons as a single fist of his can bring down a castle wall.

He stands at a towering height of 6 meters and has a vicious armored tail always tipped in poison.
