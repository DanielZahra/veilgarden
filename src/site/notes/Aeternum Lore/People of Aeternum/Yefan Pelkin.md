### Summary

Yefan has been the steward of the Quills for a long time. A long life full of knowledge, exploration and teaching the younger quills the values of such things.

### Appearance and Personality

He is small, even by Gnomish standards. His kind disposition and knowledge, alongside his storytelling skills, can instill a sense of wonder in anybody.

### History

Having aided tremendously the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] with their adventures and also allowed both [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Adam, Vermillion Emperor\|Adam, Vermillion Emperor]] and [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Ajax\|Ajax]] to roam free and find their people, Yefan was getting weary in his old age.

The mantle of Steward of the Quills was handed to [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]], Yefan aided the transition for a few months before finally passing away, a peaceful death within the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Dimension Library of the Quills of Ios\|Dimension Library of the Quills of Ios]].
