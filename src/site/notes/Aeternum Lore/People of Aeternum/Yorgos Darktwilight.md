### Summary

One of the caretakers at the Observatorium. While was not particularly proficinect in mana control, his knowledge of the workings of the Observatorium proper was second to none. He also had engineering knowledge and usually was the man to call when the telescopes and other ocular devices needed fixing.

### History

During the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Timelines and Events/Aeternum Timeline/The Sixth Valkorian War\|The Sixth Valkorian War]], he assisted the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Midnight Note\|Midnight Note]] infiltrate and escape [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Stellaria\|Stellaria]]. Unfortunately, he was seen multiple times and after the unification he was arrested and taken to the prisons of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Argentcourt\|Argentcourt]].

2 years later he managed was involved in a prison break engineered by [[Aeternum Lore/People of Aeternum/Astellios Lightseeker\|Astellios Lightseeker]] and Yorgos and his family reunited. He eventually planned an escape and join a group of people to join  agroup of trvellers seekeing safety in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Kas’Velar\|Kas’Velar]]. Eventually he landed in [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Spires of Enigma/Spires of Enigma\|Spires of Enigma]] and join the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Lifegivers\|Lifegivers]] where his family was mostly secure.
