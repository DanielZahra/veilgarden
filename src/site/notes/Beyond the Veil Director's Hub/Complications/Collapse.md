>As you are walking there is a major collapse of a roof, wall, tree or other heavy object. 

- Party members make individual skill checks against the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Complications/Collapse\|Collapse]] DC.
	- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Agility\|Agility]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Perception\|Perception]]


Critical Success: No Effect 
Success: You gain a [[Reaper’s Mark \|Reaper’s Mark ]]
Failure: You gain a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaper’s Mark\|Reaper’s Mark]] and a [[Fractured Arm \|Fractured Arm ]]
Critical Failure: You gain 2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaper’s Mark\|Reaper’s Mark]]s and a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Afflictions and Curses/Fractured Arm\|Fractured Arm]] The Director also rolls [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Luck\|Luck]] again.
