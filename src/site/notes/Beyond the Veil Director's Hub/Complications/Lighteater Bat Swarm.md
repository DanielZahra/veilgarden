>A swarm of Lighteater Bats descends on the party. The bats target any light source and gear the party would have available. 

  - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] makes a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against each creature's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] individually.

---
Critical Success: Any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light\|Light]] effect the party has is removed and any gear or objects which provide [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light\|Light]] are [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Broken\|Broken]].
Success: Any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light\|Light]] effect the party has is removed and any gear or objects which provide [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light\|Light]] are [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Damaged\|Damaged]]
Failure: No Effect.
