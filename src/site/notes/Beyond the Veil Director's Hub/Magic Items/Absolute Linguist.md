###### Passive
- You can speak and understand any language that is spoken to you.

---
>This magical tiara modifies your tongue and ears changing the sounds you make in real time as the sound hits your and as you emit any sounds. Any diplomat’s dream is to own one of these. In the early days where species from different worlds started to communicate  the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Absolute Linguist\|Absolute Linguist]] played a key role in allowing this communication to happen with a little less bloodshed.
