**Activation (****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]]****)**

- You can use the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Bonewarden’s Pass\|Bonewarden’s Pass]] to attempt to unlock any lock, or disable any device that **would normally be disabled, opened or changed by ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]]****.**
- You simply make a DC 15 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Flat Check\|Flat Check]] , on a success, you forgo any other skill check and any lock is open ot device disabled or changed.

> [!note] 💫
> Said to be crafted by an ancient Lich, this key carries an aura of respectful dread. Its skeletal prongs rearrange themselves like a disassembled hand finding its shape anew. It opens vaults, coffins, doors, and even mechanisms long rusted shut—provided the wielder dares to hold it steady when it starts “feeling” its way forward.
