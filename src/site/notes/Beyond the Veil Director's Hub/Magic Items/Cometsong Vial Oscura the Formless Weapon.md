> [!tip] 💡
> Oscura is [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Materials, Chemicals and Flora/Materials of Aeternum/Aeternite\|Aeternite]] in a pure liquid form, enchanted to be an extremely deadly weapon. Stored in a tempered glass and metallic vial, once invested this this artifact, the wielder can use an action and will their weapons to take shape based on what they need. The magical capacity if this weapon is incredible but that makes it all the harder to use, enchant and master. 
> When willed to take form the metal flows out of the vial and takes the shape the wielder wants, this shape takes over the properties of the weapon the master chooses and it attunes itself to the strongest trait of the master which makes it deadly in any hand. While being liquid in shape, Oscura once formed into a weapon shape is pretty much unbreakable and as strong and sharp as the hardest metals in the universe.
> 
> The [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Materials, Chemicals and Flora/Materials of Aeternum/Aeternite\|Aeternite]] used in Oscura has been ‘tainted’ with the Void from a being called Visra who wielded this artifact in the past.
