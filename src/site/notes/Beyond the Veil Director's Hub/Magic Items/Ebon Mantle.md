**Passive**

- Once per round, on your turn, you can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] as if it had reach 10. (The attack still counts as a melee attack).
- While wearing the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Ebon Mantle\|Ebon Mantle]] your strikes and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Attack\|Power Attack]]s gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Devastating\|Devastating]] Trait.

**Activation**

- You can become a Black Raven or Crow at will.

> A sweep of black leather edged in raven feathers, each quill tipped with sleek, lacquered leather. The hood narrows to a beak-like point that casts a long, predatory shadow across the wearer’s features. Even at rest the mantle seems to drink the light around it—ink-dark, storm-cold, and faintly smelling of wet feathers. Touch it and the plumage stirs like a living wing. Don it, and footsteps hush, edges soften, and the night leans closer to listen.
