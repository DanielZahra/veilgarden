**Passive**

- You gain a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] against [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fire/Fire Damage/Fire Damage\|Fire Damage]]
- You gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attuned Fire\|Attuned Fire]] to your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Unarmed Attack\|Unarmed Attack]] strikes.
- You gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Mana Shield\|Mana Shield]] Ability.

> [!note] 💫
> While wearing the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Emberguard Grip\|Emberguard Grip]] you can feel that your hands are on fire, but the feeling is warm and calming rather than pain and hurting. The same cannot be said for your foes.  In the bowels of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Emberdeep/Emberdeep\|Emberdeep]], the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Groups and Organisations/Groups and Orgnisations/Emberguard\|Emberguard]] has spent some time issuing some of these gauntlets to the guards for intense and fiery operations where weapons could not cut it. After a large number of burn victims and deaths due to excessive guard violence, the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Emberguard Grip\|Emberguard Grip]] were pulled from the policing force.
