**Free Activation**

You can activate your [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Favorite Sock\|Favorite Sock]] which allows you to use a single ability as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Signature\|Signature]]: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Repeat\|Repeat]]

[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Repeat\|Repeat]]: The first time you use this [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Signature\|Signature]] ability, it does not go on cooldown. This effect is resets with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Take a Break\|Take a Break]] 

---

> [!note] 💫
> This is said to have belonged to a king that refused to take off his favorite sock. The mana around the sock keeps this patched up sock from falling apart.
