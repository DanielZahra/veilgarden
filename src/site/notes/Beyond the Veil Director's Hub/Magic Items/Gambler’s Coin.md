**[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]]**** Action**

- When you make any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]], before you make your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]], you can instead roll the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Gambler’s Coin\|Gambler’s Coin]] which is a 1d2.
