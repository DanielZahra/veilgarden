**Free Activation**

- You roll one of your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]], a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] or an Ability that includes them made with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]]

---

> [!note] 💫
> The guiding hand is said to have been designed by a mathematical genious that was obsessed with not making mistakes. The mana poured into this bracelet corrects and moves the user’s hand into making sure their stries land true.

The mathamatical patterns on the bracelet, start to rewrite themselves when [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Guiding Hand\|Guiding Hand]] is activated.
