**Activation**

- Activating the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Homecoming Chronoscope\|Homecoming Chronoscope]] takes 10 minutes. 
- This item replicates the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]]. 
- It replicates the Return to Glyph function, but does not need a skill check or does it cost [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]].
- The [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Homecoming Chronoscope\|Homecoming Chronoscope]] is always locked to a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] upon creation. If the Glyph is destroyed the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Homecoming Chronoscope\|Homecoming Chronoscope]] loses its magic.
