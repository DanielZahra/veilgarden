##### Passive 

- When you wear [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Invincible\|Invincible]], at any point that you would die from unnatural causes, [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Invincible\|Invincible]] and your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] turn to dust and forever lost. When this happens you regain your full [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]] and half of your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaper’s Mark\|Reaper’s Mark]]s.

---
> When you Runeforge [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Invincible\|Invincible]] into your clothes or armor, a vacuum of void appears in the center piece. This swirling mass only activates at the time that you would take a deathblow in which case it absorbs it and immediately expends in mana to keep you alive. [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Invincible\|Invincible]] is highly sought by royalty, the extremely or the extremely wealthy when they have to take some dangerous work, travel or sometimes even attend a ball.
