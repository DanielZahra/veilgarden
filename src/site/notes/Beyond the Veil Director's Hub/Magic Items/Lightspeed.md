- This weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attuned Radiant\|Attuned Radiant]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Manafused/Manafused - Radiant\|Manafused - Radiant]] traits.

**Free Activation**

- You can activate [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Lightspeed\|Lightspeed]] after a successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Teleport\|Teleport]] to an adjacent spot to your target.
