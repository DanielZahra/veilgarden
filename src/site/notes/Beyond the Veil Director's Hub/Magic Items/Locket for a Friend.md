**Passive**

- If you have a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]], they gain one additional trait.
- This trait can be chosen from any rank that you have access to.

**Activation**

- You gain a special reaction:
    - When your  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] reaches 0 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]] and you are within 6 range, you can ‘draw’ the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] into the locket.
- You can release the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] in an adjacent spot using an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action.
- Only your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] can be pulled in the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Locket for a Friend\|Locket for a Friend]].

---

> [!note] 💫
> The softwood makes this locket feel warm and comfortable. When a companion is absorbed into the locket, the door opens and an ethereal arm reaches out to your companion, pulling them in.
