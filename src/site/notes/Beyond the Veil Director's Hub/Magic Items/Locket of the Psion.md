**Free Activation**

- On activation, you replace your base [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] value with your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] value.
- You gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Boon\|Major Boon]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Anima Damage\|Anima Damage]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
