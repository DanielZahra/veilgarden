> [[Mithrillum\|Mithrillum]] is the lightest metal in the known universe which is strong and flexible enough to create weaponry. The process of rune forging this metal is difficult but produces some of the fastest weapons in existence. These weapons tend to be light with finer details to keep their weight at a bare minimum.
