**Interact Action**

- When you make a successfull [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] you can activate [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Null\|Null]] and your weapons triggers a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Dispel Effect\|Dispel Effect]] using your roll as the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]] needed for the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Dispel Effect\|Dispel Effect]].

**Weapon Families**

- All

---

> [!note] 💫
> This rune is said to allow weapons to eat mana. Stories mention that the mana it eats goes to the void to power the monsters within.
