**Passive**

- When the rune forged with [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Phantasm’s Grasp\|Phantasm’s Grasp]] your weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Unseen\|Unseen]] Trait.
- Additionally, your weapon remains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Unseen\|Unseen]] even after you strike with it.
- [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Phantasm’s Grasp\|Phantasm’s Grasp]] can be made visible or turned back invisible with an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action.

**Weapon Families**

- All Weapons

> [!note] 💫
> Forged from distilled glass powder and alchemical vapors, Phantasm’s Grap makes a weapon entirely invisible to the naked eye. When pressed against a weapon, the weapon’s runes unravel into shimmering motes that seep into the metal, bending light around it. The weapon becomes unseen to mortal sight, its presence hidden until it strikes. 

Many previous owners have lost sight of their weapon after misplacing them.
