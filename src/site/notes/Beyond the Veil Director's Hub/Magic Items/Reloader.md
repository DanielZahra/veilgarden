**Passive**

- When you use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]] your weapon gains an additional shot before needing to be reloaded again.

**Weapon Families**

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Crossbows\|Crossbows]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Firearms\|Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Firearms]]

> [!note] 💫
> This magical [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Manatech\|Manatech]] contraption allows you to reload normally and magically clones the ammo every time. The second shot is the clone but it is impossible to distinguish between the two.

After a minute or two, the cloned ammo fired this away erodes away as mana keeping in while dissipates.
