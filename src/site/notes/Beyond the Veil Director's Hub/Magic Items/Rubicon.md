**Passive**

- This weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attuned Fire\|Attuned Fire]]  and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Manafused/Manafused - Fire\|Manafused - Fire]] traits.
- Your weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Concealable\|Concealable]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Quickdraw\|Quickdraw]] Traits.
- Your weapon does not take space in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]], it is instead a Worn item.

**Activation**

- When you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] with [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Rubicon\|Rubicon]] to hold your weapon you can choose to also activate a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light 8\|Light 8]].

When in danger you can activate [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Rubicon\|Rubicon]] as the ring transforms into a fiery blade your hand, as the blade emits flames and light the area around while also burning the foes you slice.
