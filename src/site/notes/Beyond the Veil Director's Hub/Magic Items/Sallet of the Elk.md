**Passive**

- You learn the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Charging Attack\|Charging Attack]] Ability.
- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Charging Attack\|Charging Attack]] on a successfull hit, If your attack beats the target’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]] DC the target gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] condition. ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Size Difference\|Size Difference]] applies for the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]] DC).

> [!note] 💫
> This comfortable sallet, has soft fur and leather interior to crushing the headbutting the wearer of the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Sallet of the Elk\|Sallet of the Elk]] usually does.

When warriors from [[Kas'Velar\|Kas'Velar]] wore these sallets in combat it is said they were bugling like elks, often attracting elf that happened to be close to the battlefield.
