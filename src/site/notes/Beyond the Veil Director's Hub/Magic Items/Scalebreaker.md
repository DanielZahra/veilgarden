> Originally designed to hunt down dragons, [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Scalebreaker\|Scalebreaker]] has been improved to massacre all kinds of Dragonkin or reptiles. Legend has it that the runes turns the weapons into Dragon bone. Owning one of these while travelling to the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Places/Location Database/Dragon Lands\|Dragon Lands]] is an effective death sentence.
