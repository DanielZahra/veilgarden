**Passive**

- This weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attuned Earth\|Attuned Earth]]  and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Manafused/Manafused - Earth\|Manafused - Earth]] traits.
- On a critical strike, the target gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
    - If they are wearing any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]], the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Damaged\|Damaged]] instead.

**Weapon Families**

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Hammers\|Hammers]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Axes\|Axes]] 

---

> [!note] 💫
> Scrap and Ruin got its name as the rune originally was intended to be used for tools. Like most other runes, it eventually got transferred and changes into a weapon and battle rune which is used to damage armor. 

When you strike with this weapon, anything it touches changes into slightly corroded rust and is damaged.
