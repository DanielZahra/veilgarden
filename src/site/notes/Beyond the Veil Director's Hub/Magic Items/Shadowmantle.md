> [!note] 💫
> A mantle from threads of pure shadow. Rather than just making you quiet, the fabric absorbs ambient light and sound within a few inches of your body. It allows the wearer to blend seamlessly into dark corners and muffle the clinking of stolen coins or the snap of a broken twig.
