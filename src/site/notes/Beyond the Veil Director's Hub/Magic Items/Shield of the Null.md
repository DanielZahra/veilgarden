**Reaction**

- You can use [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Shield of the Null\|Shield of the Null]] as a reaction to nullify any damage you have taken. 
- This can apply to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Direct Damage\|Direct Damage]] applied at the same time., to your self.
