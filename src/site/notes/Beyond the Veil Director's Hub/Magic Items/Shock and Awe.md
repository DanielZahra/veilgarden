> [!note] 💫
> Forged in the extreme heat, this oversized Gunlance has a core of Tempered Adamantium making the weapon itself close to indestructible. The finishing touches of the weapons are lines by Dark Mithril to avoid adding more weight but not losing any of the weapons strength. The gunlances Bladed edge creating from magical extremely sharp metal, which severs and cuts anything that it touches. The razer sharp blade, is almost invisibly thin, many injuries were had during the forging.
> The pommel and handle as rounded and finished with red dragon leather, as the weapon itself produced some major heat when firing. Next to the handle and on the top part of the Gunlance are a trigger and a topside pommel, these are designed to allow the wielder to switch stance and hold the weapon like an artillery cannon or firearm. The barrel chamber of the weapon is used to fire explosive shots for those unfortunate enough to be in front of the wielder. Inside the chamber, an Empyreal Sapphire is housed to channel the magic for both keeping the weapon cool but also as a burner, when the weapon swings magical thrusters are switched on to move the weapon with greater speeds, making this dangerous weapon even deadlier.
