**Activation**

- You can use an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Silverback Grip\|Silverback Grip]].
- While [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Silverback Grip\|Silverback Grip]] is active 
    - You grow 1 size larger.
    - You can hold two-handed weapons with 1 hand.
- This is considered a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Transformation\|Transformation]] and lasts until you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]] or you reuse [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Silverback Grip\|Silverback Grip]].
