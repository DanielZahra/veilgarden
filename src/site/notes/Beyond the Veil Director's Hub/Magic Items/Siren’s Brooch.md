> A mesmerizing brooch magically affixed to a magical silky blouse. The brooch is shaped like a blooming flower with a metallic phoenix . Whether they are shouting a demoralizing threat across a battlefield or whispering a charming lie at a royal gala, the magic ensures their voice commands absolute attention.
