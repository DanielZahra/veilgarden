**Passive**

While wearing [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Steamrolling\|Steamrolling]], you gain 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] and when you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Step\|Step]] you move 2 spaces rather than 1.

---

> [!note] 💫
> Forged where craft meets kinetic magic, these boots rest on a dozen of enchanted  magicked bead-rollers. You slide instead of walk as the beads shift and spin beneath the sole, as if the ground itself were offering no resistance. A soft blue glow pulses along the trim whenever momentum is building, and the leather straps hum faintly as the boots prepare to launch you into your next stride.
