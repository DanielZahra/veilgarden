- [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Tabula Rasa\|Tabula Rasa]] functions like an effective [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Catalyst\|Catalyst]]. You can plant a new ability within using the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Orchestrate Catalyst\|Orchestrate Catalyst]] at no cost.
