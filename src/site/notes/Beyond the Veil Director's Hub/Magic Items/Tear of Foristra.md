>The [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Tear of Foristra\|Tear of Foristra]] produces perfectly purified rain. This water is infused with purification magics that remove any taint, rot or malignant forces from the waters.

- When using the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Tear of Foristra\|Tear of Foristra]], you can trigger the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Control Weather\|Control Weather]] ritual with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]]. 
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Control Weather\|Control Weather]] ritual in this case can only produce rain for one hour over the next day. 
- Any water in the area that has curses, afflictions, taint or poisons is purified for the duration of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Control Weather\|Control Weather]].
