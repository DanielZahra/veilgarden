- [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/True Rose\|True Rose]] is a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Weapons/Rapier\|Rapier]] 
- The Artifact gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Deadly/Deadly 8\|Deadly 8]] 
- This weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attuned Void\|Attuned Void]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Manafused/Manafused - Void\|Manafused - Void]] traits.
- [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/True Rose\|True Rose]] ‘s base weapon damage is increased by one.
- While soulbound with [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/True Rose\|True Rose]] you gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Grav Speed\|Grav Speed]].
- Roselia’s Vestige: Once per day with 2 actions you can tap in the power of Roselia and grating yourself [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] on all [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]]s for until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]]. 
- When invested with [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/True Rose\|True Rose]] you can designate a coffin as your refuge. If you are close to death, you can use a reaction to teleport yourself to your coffin as long as you’re in the same plane of existence. This refuge can be switched once every 3 months.

> [!tip] 💡
> This rapier is a work of art, a  black gemstone attached to the pommel with a copper lined elegant handguard. The blade is sharp and has a faint  glow to it and is a bit shorter than a regular rapier which makes it much lighter. While wielding this artifact you feel that your natural reflexes are heightened. 
> True Rose houses the soul of an extremely ancient and powerful vampire known as ‘Roselia’. With her soul now completely fused with the blade, those who attune to this artifact can hear the temptations and whispers of his ancient being but also dip in her powers as they manifest through the weapon.
> 
> This blade used to be a rapier called Whistling Sting which has undergone a drastic change in appearance. The blade is now much shaper and serrated, the colours and designs much darker and elegant in appearance. The once very faint glow is not also visible and when the weapon is wielded there is a faint dark red colouring to it. 
> 
> As the blade was used to slay many a foe, from all kinds of races of creation, she started emitting a cold and dangerous aura. The weapon now is a connection to Rose's true powers of when she was free. The powers to capture souls before the move to the next planes and also magical ways to enhance the physical forms to become the ultimate weapon.
> 
> Legend has it that in the hands of  Silent Shadow, this artifact was involved in the slaying of 2 deities from another universe. The blade itself has a 3 small etchings with a name 'Crimson Roselia', 'Cassius Valentine' and 'Silent Shadow'
