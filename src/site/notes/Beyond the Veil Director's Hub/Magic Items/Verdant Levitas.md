**Passive**

While weaing the [[Beyond the Veil Director's Hub/Magic Items/Verdant Levitas\|Verdant Levitas]] you gain constant [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hover\|Hover]]. You can this effect on and off with an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action.

---

> [!note] 💫
> A signature achievement of Lycia’s college of creation these greaves use dual *aether-rotation disks* anchored beneath the foot. When activated, they hum with pale azure sigils that lift the wearer several inches above the earth. The embroidered root-patterns are not decorative—they channel ambient aether like a tree drawing water through soil. Though designed for aristocrats, battle-mages quickly adopted them to glide across uneven terrain and avoid pressure-triggered traps.
