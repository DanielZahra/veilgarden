# Monsters

Monsters in general have a stronger offense than defense, this is done to make them dangerous and move combat quicker. Obviously some specific monsters designed for a specific task like defense will be built differently.

## Monsters Modifications

In Veil, monsters do not use levels, instead their attributes can be modified by the Director to fit the narrative of their story. Monsters are rank as, Adept, Veteran and Master to indicate their ‘base’ abilities and effectiveness. However the rank doesn’t impact their rolls or damage.

When building a monster choose [[Minion\|Minion]], [[Horde\|Horde]],  [[Standard\|Standard]], [[Elite\|Elite]], [[Champion\|Champion]] or [[Boss\|Boss]] then choose a mastery level between [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adept\|Adept]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]]. Higher mastery creatures have access to stronger abilities and passive features and in general are stronger than their counterparts. 

Power buff, if you simply want to make a monster slightly stronger than intended, you **can add +2 to their Prowess rolls and all defenses.**

## Monster Building

When building monsters, it is ideal to use a spread that gives them at least 1 weakness. On top of this most monsters ideally have some boons and banes to specific damage types, effects and conditions when applicable.

- Defenses
    - Extreme +4
    - Strong +2
    - Normal 0
    - Weak -2
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]]
    - Monsters that use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]] have a specific [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] as they might not be using a shield.
    - This number is added to their base defenses since this is variable.
    - Such as [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]] +4 means that a Monster with Base [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] of 10, gets to 14 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]]
