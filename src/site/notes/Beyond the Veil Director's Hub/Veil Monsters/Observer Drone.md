#### Passive
- **Hover Drive**: The drone ignores [[Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] and can float over obstacles.

#### Actions & Reactions ⭐& 💫
- ⭐ [[Laser Emitter\|Laser Emitter]]:  4 ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant\|Radiant]], [[Ranged\|Ranged]] 12, [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]])
- ⭐ [[Target Lock\|Target Lock]]: 1 Action. The drone marks a target it can see. Its next [[Laser Emitter\|Laser Emitter]] attack against that target gains a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Boon\|Major Boon]].
- 💫 [[Evasive Protocol\|Evasive Protocol]]: Reaction. When targeted by a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range\|Range]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]], the drone makes an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Agility Skill Check\|Agility Skill Check]] to replace its [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] on a success it can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Step\|Step]] and cause the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] to become a Critical Failure.

#### Abilities ⭐
- ⭐⭐ [[Overcharge Beam\|Overcharge Beam]] (2 Actions, Cooldown): The drone fires a highly concentrated beam in a 10x2 meter [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Line/Line\|Line]]. Deals 7 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant\|Radiant]] damage against [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Basic Reflex\|Basic Reflex]].
