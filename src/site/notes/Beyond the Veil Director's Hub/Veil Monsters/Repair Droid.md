#### Passive
- **Industrial Chassis**: As a worker unit, this droid ignores [[Difficult Terrain\|Difficult Terrain]] caused by rubble or industrial hazards.

#### Actions & Reactions ⭐& 💫
- [[Welding Lasers\|Welding Lasers]]: 5 ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant\|Radiant]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]], [[Ranged\|Ranged]] 6, [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]])
- [[Jury-Rig\|Jury-Rig]]: 1 Action. The droid makes rapid repairs, restoring 5 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]] to an adjacent [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Construct\|Construct]] or itself. 
- [[Flash Flare\|Flash Flare]] 💫: Reaction. When targeted by a melee attack, the droid overloads its palm lenses with a bright flash, applying a Minor [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to the attacker's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll.

#### Abilities ⭐
- [[Twin Beam Sweep\|Twin Beam Sweep]] (2 Actions, Cooldown): The droid sweeps both palm lasers in a 3-meter [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cone\|Cone]]. Deals 6 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant\|Radiant]] damage and applies a Minor [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to the targets' next attack roll due to optical overload.
