> You charge yourself with the two fluxes of current Alternating (AC) and Direct (DC), as your ebb and flows switches between a fluid defensive state to a direct aggressive state.

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/AC-DC\|AC-DC]] is a special [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Conduit\|Conduit]] stance that allows you to switch between AC and DC
- You can use one action to switch between AC or DC 
- AC (Fluid State)
    - You gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] 
- DC (Direct State)
    - You gain 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] 
    - Once per round you can convert any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Direct Damage\|Direct Damage]] you deal to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Air Damage\|Air Damage]]
