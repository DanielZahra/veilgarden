> You focus your entire spirit into a single moment, as you amplify any ability that followes this moment of glory.

- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Amplification\|Amplification]] as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Combo\|Combo]] of another ability that your ally is using, the ability has it’s range, and area numbers doubled in dimension.
    - Example
        - 4x4 Burst becomes 8x8
        - 12 range becomes 24
        - 3 Cone becomes 6
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Amplification\|Amplification]] amplified an ability used by another willing creature that is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Combo\|Combo]] with you.
- You must be within 6 range of your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Combo\|Combo]] ally to use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Amplification\|Amplification]].
