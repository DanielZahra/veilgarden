> 
> You focus your power on cancelling a single effect, you use your own will to suppress the effect for a time.

Requirement: Target an effect that is dispellable, ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Dispel Effect\|Dispel Effect]])

---
- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Annul\|Annul]] you must target a single effect; this effect can be on a creature or in a space.
- When targeting an effect on a creature, you must be able to see the creature to target the effect.

---
To dispel an effect, you make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]] agaist the DC of the effect.  If you believe or know that a target has more than one effect, you choose a single one.

**Critical Success**: You dispel the effect completely.
**Success**: You dispel the effect for 10 minutes.
**Failure**: No Effect.
