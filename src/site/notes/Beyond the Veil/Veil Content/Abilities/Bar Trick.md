> You have spent enough time handling glass vials, potions or in a bar playing tricks with people. You rub your fingers in your leathery belt removing all moisture and getting dry tips, immediately allowing you to grab two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Elixir\|Elixir]]s or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Oil\|Oil]]s (or one of each)  from your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] and using them at the same time.

- When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Bar Trick\|Bar Trick]] you can use two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Elixir\|Elixir]]/[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Oil\|Oil]]  Items at the same time and you can also target 2 different creatures.
- The two aciton cost of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Bar Trick\|Bar Trick]] includes the two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] actions needed to pull the Exliris or Oils from your load out of use them.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Bar Trick\|Bar Trick]] can be used with [[Apothecary's Gear\|Apothecary's Gear]].
