> Your throw your weapon at a foe, making sure it cuts deep and sticks in their skin, as you charge towards them and ripping out your weapon pulling with it their innards.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]], for this [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] your weapon gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Thrown/Thrown\|Thrown]] trait up to your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Overpower/Overpower\|Overpower]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]] 
    - The target gains 2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Persistent/Persistent Damage\|Persistent Damage]], increased to 4 on a critical.
- After the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] you can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] to an adjacent spot to your target (up to your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]]), and retrieve your weapon.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Berserker’s Throw\|Berserker’s Throw]] cannot be used unless you’re wielding a weapon.
