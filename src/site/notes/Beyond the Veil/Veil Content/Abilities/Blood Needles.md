> Sharp needles of blood form around you, as they float ready to strike their targets or heal your allies.

- You use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Blood Needles\|Blood Needles]] to activate the ability giving yourself [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Blood Needles\|Blood Needles]] that float around you that can be shot at targets until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- You can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Blood Needles\|Blood Needles]] and make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Attack\|Power Attack]] dealing 3 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant Damage/Radiant Damage\|Radiant Damage]].
    - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] trait is activated only when your use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Blood Needles\|Blood Needles]] 
- You can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Blood Needles\|Blood Needles]] and heal an ally for 3 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]].
