> You grab your opponent and pull them close and stab them at the same time rendering their defenses useless.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]] 
- On a success the targets gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] and you deal your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] damage (using their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] to measure success).
- On failure, you only deal your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] damage.
- You only roll one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] check for [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Clinching Strike\|Clinching Strike]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]] lasts as long as you keep the creature in a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]].
