> Most creatures need freedom of movement to move, you target their legs, wings, or anything that allows movement and freeze it on place.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]].

****Failure: **Creature gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Lethargic\|Lethargic]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of their next turn\|End of their next turn]].**
