> You can tell an ally is going to get in trouble and you immediately rush to take the blow for them.

- You can activate the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take a Blow\|Take a Blow]] reaction as long as an ally is within your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] range.
- When you activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Defensive Rush\|Defensive Rush]]. you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] to an adjacent spot of an ally and activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take a Blow\|Take a Blow]].
