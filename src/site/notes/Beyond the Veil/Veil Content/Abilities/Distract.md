>You shout or make surprising sounds as you try to pull away your foe’s attention, as your ally gets advantages thanks to your distraction.

- When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Distract\|Distract]], you can use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] action , by targeting a creature. You must prepare [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Distract\|Distract]] on your turn and then use a reaction when an ally needs you.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] action DC is replaced by [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] if of the creature.
