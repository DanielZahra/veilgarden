> 
> You gorge on your target’s essence and devour their energies for your own.

- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Drain Essence\|Drain Essence]] you choose to target an attribute of a creature. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] 
- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]].
- You get to keep the buff as long as the target has their debuff.
