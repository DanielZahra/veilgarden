> 
> Your skills with equipment allows you handle everything as if it were a child’s toy.

- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Equipment Handler\|Equipment Handler]] allowing you to add 6 range to any thrown item or add 6 range to any other [[Gear and Consumables\|Gear and Consumables]] you would normally use.
    - eg. with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Equipment Handler\|Equipment Handler]] you can use an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Elixir\|Elixir]] on an ally 6 spaces away.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Equipment Handler\|Equipment Handler]] can only be used on [[Gear and Consumables\|Gear and Consumables]].
