> You burning desire purges negative mana or powers on your allies.

- You can target a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] from an ally and remove it.
- You can also target a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]] and reduce it to a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]].
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]]/ [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]] cannot be part of an existing condition, a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Stance\|Stance]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Aura\|Aura]] or if it is permanent.
