> Your body and weapons can channel the power of flames into your strikes.

- The creature’s held weapons or unarmed strikes deal an additional 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fire/Fire Damage/Fire Damage\|Fire Damage]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- Only one creature can have the effects of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Flaming Armaments\|Flaming Armaments]] at any point in time. If the ability is used on a different creature, the effect is applied to the new creature and removed from the previous one.
