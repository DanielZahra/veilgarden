> As you and your allies seek to strike your blows, you manipulate the gale and winds to make sure your strikes land true as your help adjust the aim.

- If you or an ally has a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Close Failure\|Close Failure]] with any attack, you can give them the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Graze\|Graze]] trait.
- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Gale’s Manipulation\|Gale’s Manipulation]] as a reaction out of your turn, or free action on your turn.
- You must be within 24 range of the TARGET to manipulate the attack.
