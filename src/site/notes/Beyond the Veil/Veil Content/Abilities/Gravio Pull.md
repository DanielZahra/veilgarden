> 
> You alter the gravity between you and a foe and suck them in towards you. As the creature is pulled in you strike them with your held weapon or unarmed.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]].
- On success, the creature takes your Melee Weapon Damage or Unarmed Damage.

---

**Critical Success**: Creature is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]] to a spot adjacent to you and they take Critical Damage from your Melee Weapon Damage or Unarmed Damage.
**Success:** Creature is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]] to a spot adjacent to you and they take normal from your Melee Weapon Damage or Unarmed Damage.
**Failure**: Creature is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]]  to half of the distance between you and the target and take no damage.**
**Critical Failure**: No Effect.
