> 
> The two of you flank a foe, as one of your moves in to stop their movement the other makes the finishing blow.

- To activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Heart Breaker\|Heart Breaker]] an ally must be in a position to make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]] attempt with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]].
- On a successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]] the second ally can make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] that is automatcially a critical hit.
- On a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]] failure, the second ally makes a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]].
