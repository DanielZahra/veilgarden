***Trigger*** : An ally within 2 of you is the target of a single target attack.

---

As part of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Intercept\|Intercept]] you can use a reaction to step, but must end your movement adjacent to the ally, and switch places with them changing the target to yourself. When doing this you also use your defences, possibly making the attack miss.
