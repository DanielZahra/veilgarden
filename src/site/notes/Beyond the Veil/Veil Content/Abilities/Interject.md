> You can save an ally from making a mess of things, or interfere politely to make sure the meaning goes through.

- When an ally makes a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Close Failure\|Close Failure]] during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Social Mode\|Social Mode]] or a Social Skill check, you can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Interject\|Interject]] to save their slip up.
- You give the ally a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Influence\|Influence]] for the skill check.
