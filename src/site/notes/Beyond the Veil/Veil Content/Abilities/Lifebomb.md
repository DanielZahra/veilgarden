> As life hangs in the balance like a coin, you take control of the balance and swing it you way.

- Trigger: You or an ally deal Critical Damage

---
- As the target suffers critical damage you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Lifebomb\|Lifebomb]] to deal an additional 4 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Anima Damage\|Anima Damage]].
- Alternatively, you can trigger [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Lifebomb\|Lifebomb]] to heal the creature that dealt the critical damage for 4 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]]
