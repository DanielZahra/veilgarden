> 
> As you are about to  take damage, you swifty infuse mana in your implement and create a shield of pure mana to absorb the impact.

- When you are about to take direct damage, you can use a reaction to give yourself 4 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Barrier\|Barrier]]. 
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Barrier\|Barrier]] is only active for the instance of the damage and is then removed.
