> [!note] 🗺️
> You choose the shake off an effect that attacks your mind.

- When you make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] Saving throw or you suffer the effects on your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] DC, you can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Mindfulness\|Mindfulness]] to increase your degree of success by one. 
- In the case where there is only a failure condition, you simply turn the failure into success.
