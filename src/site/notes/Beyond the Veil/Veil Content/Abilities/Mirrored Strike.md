> 
> You relfect an attack as if you had a mirror showing your opponent what’s up,

**Trigger: **You are the target of an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] 

---

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll, if your roll is higher than the creature attacking you, you relfect the attack back at them but using your roll as the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]].
- This attack uses all the traits, effects and damage based on the original creature.
