> You sync your senses to mana, magic, power or a natural effect.

- You take the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Identify Mana\|Identify Mana]] exploration activity in 1 action.
- The effect must be within 50 range of you.
- You must be able to see, or sense the effect in some way.

---

**Critical Success**: You get all the information about the effects and the source. If the source is outside of your range, you get the direction it is in.
**Success**: You get all the information about the effects.
**Failure**: You get a single piece of information about the effect.
**Critical Failure**: You fail to get any information.
