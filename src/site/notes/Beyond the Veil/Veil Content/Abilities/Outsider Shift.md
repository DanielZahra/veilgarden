- You gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Aberrant\|Aberrant]] creature type.
	- You gain the traits associated with your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Transformation\|Transformation]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
	    - These traits are chosen during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Preparation\|Preparation]]
- Equipment
    - You also gain a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Natural Weapon\|Natural Weapon]]
        - This weapon can be any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]] choice from the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Weapons/Weapons\|Weapons]]
            - [[Ranged\|Ranged]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Thrown/Thrown\|Thrown]] weapons cannot be selected.
        - Your weapon gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Free-Hand\|Free-Hand]] Trait.
        - This weapon lasts as long as the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Outsider Shift\|Outsider Shift]] lasts, but cannot be put away.
        - The weapon can be used for any ability that has the weapon type as a requirement.
            - eg. If you transform to have a sword for a hand, you can use sword abilities if you know them.
        - Weapon traits acquired through Equipment Mastery can still be applied to your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Transformation\|Transformation]] weapon.
    - If you are already holding a weapon, you can choose to merge the weapon in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Transformation\|Transformation]]but it does not gain the benefits of the  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Natural Weapon\|Natural Weapon]]

- During the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Downtime Activity\|Downtime Activity]] you can perform a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Runeforging\|Runeforging]] on your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Outsider Shift\|Outsider Shift]] weapon. If the applied rune has a specific weapon family it belongs to, it only activates when your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Natural Weapon\|Natural Weapon]] belongs to the chosen family. 
- If you have an Equipment Mastery that unlocks traits for your weapons. These are chosen during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]]. You can have up to 2 different weapons set up this way. (2 More can be added with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Features and Passives/Weaponized Arsenal\|Weaponized Arsenal]])
