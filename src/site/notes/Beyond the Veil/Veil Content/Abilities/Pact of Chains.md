> Your pact conjures chains of darkness that bind your foes.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]].
- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Features and Passives/Pact Edict\|Pact Edict]] to change [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]]

| Degree           | Effect                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Critical Success | The targets gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]].                     |
| Success          | The targets gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]].                           |
| Failure          | The target gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Exposed\|Exposed]] until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of this turn\|End of this turn]] . |
