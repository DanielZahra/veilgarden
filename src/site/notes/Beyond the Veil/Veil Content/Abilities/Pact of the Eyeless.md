> 
> You utter a pact of power that bleeds the colour and light from a creature’s eye as their scherla, iris and pupil become fully black.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]].
- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Features and Passives/Pact Edict\|Pact Edict]] to change [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blurred Vision\|Blurred Vision]] into [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blind\|Blind]].

| Critical Success | The targets gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blurred Vision\|Blurred Vision]] until end of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]]         |
