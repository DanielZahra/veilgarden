> 
> As you foe charges next to you, or past you, you strike them before they get to strike you.

**Trigger: **A creatures moves** into a space** [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adjacent\|Adjacent]] to you.

---

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] as a reaction
- Can only be used by [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]]  weapons or unarmed attacks from an adjacent space to the target.
- On a success, any action the creature was making is interrupted.
    - Abilities and Ranged Attacks are cancelled and (wasted if they had limited use)
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] is interrupted and their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] becomes 0 until the end of their turn. (Your choice)
