> Sometimes combat skill isn’t enough to dodge a blow and brain over brawn is the way to go.

- When an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] beats your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]], you can use a reaction to make [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Smarts Skill Check\|Smarts Skill Check]].
- You set your base [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] to the result rolled until [[Start of target's next turn\|Start of target's next turn]].
- If your total result is lower than your current base[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] you can choose to ignore [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Quick Witted\|Quick Witted]].
