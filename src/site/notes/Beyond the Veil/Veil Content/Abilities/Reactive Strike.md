> As your foe drops their guard, you are ready to strike at a moment’s notice.

- Trigger: A creature adjacent to you uses a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] Trait action [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range\|Range]] Attack, or an ability with a range other than Self, Adjacent, Melee, or Emanation.

---
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaction\|Reaction]].
- Can only be used by [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]] weapons or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Unarmed Attack\|Unarmed Attack]]  from an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adjacent\|Adjacent]] space to the target.
- On a success, any action the creature was making is interrupted.
    - Abilities and Ranged Attacks are cancelled and (wasted if they had limited use)
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] is interrupted and their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] becomes 0 until the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Start of target’s next turn\|Start of target’s next turn]]. (Your choice)
