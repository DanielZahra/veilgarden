> In the face of danger you use your own armor as a shield from harm.

Trigger: You are about to take [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Direct Damage\|Direct Damage]].

---

- Your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Hardness/Hardness\|Hardness]] and you can block damage once.
- **Damage blocked can be of any type as long as the source is not ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Anima Damage\|Anima Damage]]**** or ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Persistent/Persistent Damage\|Persistent Damage]]****.**
