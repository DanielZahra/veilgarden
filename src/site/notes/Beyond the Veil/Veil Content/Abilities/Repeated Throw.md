> Why throw one weapon when you can throw 3.

- 3 x [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]]
- For every successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] you make, the creature immediately gains a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to the base damage type of your weapon until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of this turn\|End of this turn]].
	- This [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] is applied to the second or third of your attacks made with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Repeated Throw\|Repeated Throw]].
