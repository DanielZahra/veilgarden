> [!tip] 💡
> You set yourself in a position to counter any failed attacks against you.

Requirement: You can only trigger [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Riposte Stance\|Riposte Stance]] with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]] weapon.

---

- When a creature makes a failed [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] against you, you can counter with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] as a reaction.
