> 
> You slam an opponent with your shield overwhelming them.

**Requirement:**  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Size Difference\|Size Difference]] applies.

---

-  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] with your held shield, [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Overpower/Overpowers\|Overpowers]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]] 
    - On a success, the target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]]d 2 and gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] until the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of this turn\|End of this turn]].
    - On a critical success, the target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]]d 4 and gains [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]] until  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of this turn\|End of this turn]]. and also gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] condition.
