> 
> When you throw your weapon, you immediately draw another weapon in your hands to keep your close foes at bay.

- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Throwing\|Throwing]] weapons, and you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]], you draw another weapon as a Free action.
    - With the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Quickdraw\|Quickdraw]] trait, you already draw when attacking, but this leaves your hands empty after you strike, meaning you cannot parry or use reaction that require a held weapon.
