You manipulate a small particle of mana around you and allow it to shed light in a 8 emanation. This light particle always remains at your side. 

Alternatively you can touch an object of medium size or smaller, or that a creature is carrying or wearing and make the object glow creating a 8 emanation of light. 

You can only have 1 instance of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Spirit Light\|Spirit Light]].
