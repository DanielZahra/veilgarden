> 
> As you build up momentum by striking your foes, your speed increases and you chain your attacks together like a combo of light. As you unleash your [[Starstriker's Momentum\|Starstriker's Momentum]], your attacks move at the speed of light and are unleashed on your foe.

[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Star Strike\|Star Strike]] has no [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cooldown\|Cooldown]] 

---

- To use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Star Strike\|Star Strike]] you must have at least 1 charge of [[Starstriker's Momentum\|Starstriker's Momentum]]. 
    - For every charge of [[Starstriker's Momentum\|Starstriker's Momentum]] you have, you make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] roll, for each successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] instead of dealing damage, you add your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Unarmed Fighting\|Unarmed Fighting]] damage to a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Attack\|Power Attack]]. Critical Successes add two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Unarmed Fighting\|Unarmed Fighting]] damage counts.
    - Any additional **damage **from other features, items or abilities is only added once per [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Star Strike\|Star Strike]] on the final roll. 
- You make then make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Attack\|Power Attack]] and for every successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] gained from [[Starstriker's Momentum\|Starstriker's Momentum]] you roll an additional [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Unarmed Fighting\|Unarmed Fighting]] damage die
