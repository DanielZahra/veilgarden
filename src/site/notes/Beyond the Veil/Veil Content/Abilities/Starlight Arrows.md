> 
> You summon specs as Astral lights that float around you. When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Starlight Arrows\|Starlight Arrows]] you can point your finger at a creature you can see and one of the specs of light will become a arrows of light and zipping towards the creatures.

- Once per round you can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Starlight Arrows\|Starlight Arrows]] and deal 2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Radiant/Radiant Damage/Radiant Damage\|Radiant Damage]] to a target.
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Starlight Arrows\|Starlight Arrows]] don’t need a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]].
