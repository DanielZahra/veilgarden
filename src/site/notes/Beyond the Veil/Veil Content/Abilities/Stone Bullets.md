> You stomp the ground raising bullets shaped from stone to do your bidding, they float above your shoulders ready to be aimed at your foes.

- You use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Stone Bullets\|Stone Bullets]] to activate the ability giving summoning stones that float around which can be shot until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- You can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Stone Bullets\|Stone Bullets]] and make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Attack\|Power Attack]] dealing 3 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Crushing Damage\|Crushing Damage]].
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] trait is activate only when your use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Stone Bullets\|Stone Bullets]]
