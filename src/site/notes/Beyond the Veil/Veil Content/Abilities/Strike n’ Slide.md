> Using the floor as if it were slippery and easy to move on, your agility allows you easy movement between, under and around your foes. This creates the opening you need to strike.

**Requirement**: You can only use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Strike n’ Slide\|Strike n’ Slide]] with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]] weapon.

---
- You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Slip Past\|Slip Past]] through or towards a creature and quickly strike it while making sure you have a space to retreat. 
- If you end your stride within melee reach of at least one enemy, you can make a melee [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] against that enemy. Then you can step away.
- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Strike n’ Slide\|Strike n’ Slide]] while Burrowing, Climbing, Flying, or Swimming instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
