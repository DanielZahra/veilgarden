> 
> You meet out a foe’s attack with your own power. Striking their attack or weapon with your own.

- When you are the target of an attack, you make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]].
- If your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veil Roll\|Veil Roll]] total is higher than the incoming attack, the attack becomes a Critical Failure.
- If the attacker was in your weapon range, you also deal damage to them.
