> 
> You make distracting noises, use derogatory words, or insult your foes in an attempt to taunt them and force them to target you.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]].

---
**Critical Success**: The target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Fixated\|Fixated]] on you until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
**Success**: The target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Fixated\|Fixated]] on you until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of their next turn\|End of their next turn]].
**Failure**: No Effect
