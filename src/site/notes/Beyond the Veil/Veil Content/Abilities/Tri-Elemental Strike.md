> You infuse the elements in an allied creature’s weapons or body. The next time you strike, your elemental powers unleash.

- The next [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] the creature makes deals an additional 2[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fire/Fire Damage/Fire Damage\|Fire Damage]],  2[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Water Damage\|Water Damage]] and  2[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Air Damage\|Air Damage]].
- If the creature under the effects of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Tri-Elemental Strike\|Tri-Elemental Strike]], makes a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]], it is turned into [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Tri-Elemental Strike\|Tri-Elemental Strike]] lasts until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of their next turn\|End of their next turn]] but expires after being used once.
