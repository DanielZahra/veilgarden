> [!note] 
> Time stops as you parry a foe’s attack, you use this fraction of a second to turn their attack against them as you strike them before they can react.

**Trigger: **You successfully use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Parry\|Parry]] against a foe within your weapon reach.

---

- Following a successful [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Parry\|Parry]] you can use a reaction to make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]].
