> Your armaments get a dark sheen and covered in void energy. Dark matter slowly float off in the air around you as the void reacts to the natural mana in the air.

- Strikes deal additional [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Void Damage\|Void Damage]] 1 until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- Only one creature can have the effects of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Void Armaments\|Void Armaments]] at any point in time. If the ability is used on a different creature, the effect is applied to the new creature and removed from the previous one.
