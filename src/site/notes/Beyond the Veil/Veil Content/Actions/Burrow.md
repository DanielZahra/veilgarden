When you gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Burrow Speed\|Burrow Speed]] you can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Burrow\|Burrow]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]]. When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Burrow\|Burrow]] you can decide to remain underground gaining [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]], however you need to use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Burrow\|Burrow]] again to move out again.
