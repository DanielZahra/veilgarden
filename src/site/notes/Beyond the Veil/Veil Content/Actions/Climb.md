> Climbing is the act of scaling an obstacle that is hard to clear with normal movement.

**Requirement: Must have at least 1 free hand.**

**Critical Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Climb\|Climb]] at your normal [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]].
**Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Climb\|Climb]] at your half your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]].
**Failure:** You fail to climb.
**Critical Failure:** You fall.
