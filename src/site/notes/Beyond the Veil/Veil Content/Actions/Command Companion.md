You spend an action to give your companion 2 actions. You need an active [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] to use this action. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Command Companion\|Command Companion]] can only be used once per turn and only a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] can be commanded at a time.
