Requirement: You must have [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Tinkering Tools\|Tinkering Tools]] in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]].

---
This action allows you to disarm a trap or another complex device. Often, a device requires numerous successes before becoming disabled, depending on its construction and complexity. Your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Tinkering Skill Check\|Tinkering Skill Check]] result determines your progress.

---
**Critical Success:**  You disable the device, or you achieve two successes toward disabling a device requiring more than one success. You leave no trace of your tampering, and you can rearm the device later, if that type of device can be rearmed.
**Success:** You disable the device, or you achieve one success toward disabling a device that requires more than one success.
**Failure:** You fail to disable the device quickly, further attempts will require you to take the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Dismantle Device\|Dismantle Device]] exploration activity.
**Critical Failure:** You trigger the device.
