When you use an ability with a duration, you can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/End Effect\|End Effect]] to stop the ability and make the effect go away. You can only use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/End Effect\|End Effect]] on abilities you used.
