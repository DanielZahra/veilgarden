You must use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Escape\|Escape]] to get out of a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]]. condition. You make [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] Check to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Escape\|Escape]].

---
**Critical Success:** You release yourself from [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]] and can immediately [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] up to half your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]].
**Success:** You release yourself from [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]].
**Failure:** You remain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]].
