>You glance at a creature’s biology, features and stance and try to deduct some of its strengths and weaknesses. 

Requirement: You must be aware of, and see a creature.

---
You roll a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Smarts Skill Check\|Smarts Skill Check]] check against the creature’s DC. Depending on your success you gain variable information about the creature. 

---

**Critical Success:** You get the creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] Scores. You also learn of damage [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] or Damage immunity it has.
**Success:** You choose between learning about [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Basic Defenses\|Basic Defenses]] or damage [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] or Damage immunity it has.
**Failure:**  The Director gives you a single bit of information, i.e. a single defense or a Boon, Bane or Immunity
**Critical Failure:** Nothing.

---
You can only use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Find Weakness\|Find Weakness]] once per turn.
