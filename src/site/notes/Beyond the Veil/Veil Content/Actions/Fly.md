The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Fly\|Fly]] can be taken when you are [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Flying\|Flying]] or have [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Flying Speed\|Flying Speed]]. The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Fly\|Fly]] action allows you to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] using your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Flying Speed\|Flying Speed]]. You can use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Fly\|Fly]]  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] 0 distance to hover in place. If you're airborne at the end of your turn and didn't use any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] action this round, you fall.
