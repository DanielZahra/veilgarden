> Save yourself from [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Falling\|Falling]] or catch a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Falling\|Falling]] ally as long as you have something to grab on to.

Requirements: You are not [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Restrained\|Restrained]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]]. 

---
When you fall off or past an edge or other handhold, you can try to grab it, potentially stopping your fall. You must succeed at your choice of an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Agility Skill Check\|Agility Skill Check]], usually at the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Climb\|Climb]] DC. If you grab the edge or handhold, you can then [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Climb\|Climb]] up using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]]. As part of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grab On\|Grab On]] you can let go of an item and drop it to give yourself a free hand. If the fall or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]] is coming from another creature or ability you use their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] as the check.

---
**Critical Success:** You grab the edge or handhold, whether or not you have a hand free, typically by using a suitable held item to catch yourself (catching a battle axe on a ledge, for example). You still take [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Falling\|Falling]] damage from the distance so far, but you treat the fall as though it were 6 shorter.
**Success:** If you have at least one hand free, you grab the edge or handhold, stopping your fall. You still take damage from the distance fallen so far, but you treat the fall as though it were 4 shorter. If you have no hands free, you continue to fall as if you had failed the check.
**Critical Failure:** You continue to fall, and if you've fallen 4 or more before you use this reaction, you take [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Falling\|Falling]] Damage (1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Crushing Damage\|Crushing Damage]] for every 2 spaces).
