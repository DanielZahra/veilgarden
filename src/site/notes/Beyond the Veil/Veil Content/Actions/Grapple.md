> Grab an opponent, keeping them in place.

Requirements: You have at least one free hand and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Size Difference\|Size Difference]] applies.

---
[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] check against the target's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]]. You can Grapple a target you already have [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]]. (If you’re already grappling an opponent with a hand, you can use the same hand to keep the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]]).

---
**Critical Success:** Your target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Exposed\|Exposed]] until you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Release\|Release]] them or they [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Escape\|Escape]].
**Success:** Your target is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Held\|Held]] until you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Release\|Release]] them or they [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Escape\|Escape]].
**Failure:** You fail to grab your target.

---
When you have a creature in a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]], you can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] yourself and the creature you have grappled as if you are in [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Difficult Terrain 2\|Difficult Terrain 2]] without breaking the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]]. You can only move the creature if you are in an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adjacent\|Adjacent]] spot to it.
