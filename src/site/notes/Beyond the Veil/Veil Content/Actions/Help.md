>Set up your allies to help them out.

Trigger: An ally is about to use an action that requires a skill check or attack roll. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] can also be used to help an ally recover from persistent damage.

---
Requirements: The ally is willing to accept your help, and you have prepared to help (see below).

---
You try to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] your ally with a task. To use this [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaction\|Reaction]], you must first prepare to help, usually by using an action during your turn. You explain  how you're trying to help, and the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] will determine whether you can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] your ally.

---
**Critical Success:** DC is reduced by 4.
**Success:** DC is reduced by 2.
**Critical Failure:** DC is increased by 2.

---
The typical DC is for a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] action is the same DC as the original check; the Director can decide to lower the DC if your help provides a significant change. If the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] action is being used against a creature, the creature’s DC is used instead.

---
[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Help\|Help]] cannot be used during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Social Mode\|Social Mode]] or Social skill checks.
