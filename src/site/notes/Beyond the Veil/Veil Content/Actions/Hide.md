>You huddle behind standard cover, heavy cover or deeper into concealment to become [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]]. 

Requirements: You must be in standard cover, heavy cover or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Concealed\|Concealed]].

---
Different creatures have different [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Awareness\|Awareness]]. You might be simultaneously hidden from one creature but seen by another. You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Subterfuge Skill Check\|Subterfuge Skill Check]] and record your resulting result as a DC for any seekers.

---
**Success:** You are now [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] 
**Failure:** You are still visible.
