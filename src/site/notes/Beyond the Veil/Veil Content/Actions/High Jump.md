You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]], then attempt a DC 12 Athletics check to jump vertically. If you didn't [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] at least 2 spaces, you automatically fail. This DC might be increased or decreased due to the situation, as determined by the GM.

**Critical Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] up to 4 spaces vertically and 2 spaces horizontally.
**Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] up to 3 spaces vertically and 1 space horizontally.**
**Failure:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] normally.
**Critical Failure:** You fall [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]]  in your space.
