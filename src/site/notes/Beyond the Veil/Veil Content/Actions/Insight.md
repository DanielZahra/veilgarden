> You carefully observe an intelligent being to try and discern their reactions and affinity to conversation.

Requirements: You must be in earshot of the target and have a very clear view of their body language and tone for **at least 1 minute** to be able to make an this check.

---
[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Insight\|Insight]] is typically used during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Social Mode\|Social Mode]] where [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Close the Veil\|Close the Veil]] is active. The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Intuition Skill Check\|Intuition Skill Check]] results are only given to you, and not your allies. You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Insight\|Insight]] after about 1 minute of conversation and you do not need to call it out before you start counting a minute. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] potentially exposes a target's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Social Affinity\|Social Affinity]].

---
**Critical Success:** You get a correct assessment of all affinities. ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Deception\|Deception]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Intimidation\|Intimidation]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Diplomacy\|Diplomacy]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Charm\|Charm]])
**Success:** You get the most beneficial affinity to target (The target’s lowest).
**Failure:** You get no information.
