> Interact allows you to use items from your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] or the environment. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] is used to open doors, flip a lever, or flip a table.

You require a free hand to use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] unless an ability or feature specifically allows you to not need a free hand or you are using the action with your held item. When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] with your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] you can switch a held item with another item in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]]. (eg, you can switch between a weapon in your hands to another from your loadout). Unless specified most items or objects require a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action to be taken form your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] and used.  If an item requires more than one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]], you simply spend the required amount of actions to use the item.
