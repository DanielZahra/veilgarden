The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] may roll these in [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Secret\|Secret]]. Knowledge checks are only made when the knowledge needed isn't a simple things within a domain. Knowledge checks can only be made **ONCE** per character per topic. Knowledge checks are meant to be generous, when you have knowledge about a domain that is relevant for the topic, it is assumed that you can roll. When you have multiple domains that can be used for the check, you gain a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] for each relevant topic. Such as If you have both [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Knowledge Topics/Mammal Knowledge\|Mammal Knowledge]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Knowledge Topics/Region Knowledge\|Region Knowledge]], and you’re trying to figure out if you know which mammals live in a range of mountains, this roll gets a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] for possessing both topics.

When making a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Knowledge check\|Knowledge check]] using specialized [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Knowledge\|Knowledge]] you make the check with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]].

---
**Critical Success:** You know everything there is to know about the topic.
**Success:** You know plenty about this topic.
**Failure:** You know little about this topic.
**Critical Failure:** You know nothing about this topic.
