You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]], then attempt a DC 8 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] check to make a long jump in the direction you were moving. If you didn't [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] at least 2 spaces, you automatically fail your check. The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] might increase or decrease this DC depending on the situation.

**Critical Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] horizontally 8 spaces or up to your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]], whichever is higher.
**Success:** You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] horizontally 6 spaces.
**Failure:** You make a normal horizontal [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]] .
**Critical Failure:** You make a normal horizontal [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Jump\|Jump]], then fall and land [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]].
