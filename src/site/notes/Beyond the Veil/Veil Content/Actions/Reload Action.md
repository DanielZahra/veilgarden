> While all weapons need some amount of time to get into position, many ranged weapons also need to be loaded and reloaded.

Requirement: To use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]] you need a free hand. In the case of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Two Handed\|Two Handed]][[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reload/Reload\|Reload]] weapon, you can use one of the hands holding the weapon to use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]].

---
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]] allows you to load your held weapon with ammunition.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]] action is needed for all weapons with the  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reload/Reload\|Reload]] trait. When [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reload/Reload\|Reload]] trait is followed by a number, it requires additional [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]] actions to prepare the weapon. (Such as [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reload/Reload 2\|Reload 2]])
- If an item takes 2 or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Reload Action\|Reload Action]]s to reload, you can spend some of those actions during one turn and the rest during your next turn.
