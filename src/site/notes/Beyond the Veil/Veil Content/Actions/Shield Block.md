> While you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]] you can use a reaction to block damage up to the shield’s Hardness. You snap your shield in place to ward off a blow. Your shield prevents you from taking an amount of damage up to the shield's Hardness. You take any remaining damage.

Trigger: While you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]] you can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaction\|Reaction]] to block damage up to the shield’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Hardness/Hardness\|Hardness]]. Damage blocked can be of any type except [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Persistent/Persistent\|Persistent]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Anima\|Anima]].
