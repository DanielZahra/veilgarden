> Push an opponent away

**Requirement:**  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Size Difference\|Size Difference]] applies.

---
You push a creature away from you.  Attempt a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] Attack  against your target's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]].

Critical Success: You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]] your target up to 2 away from you. 
Success: You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Force Move\|Force Move]] your target up to 1 away from you.
Critical Failure: You fall [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]].
