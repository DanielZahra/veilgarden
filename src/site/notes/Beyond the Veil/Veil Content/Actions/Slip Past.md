> You tumble through, squeeze, or move past an opponent's space.

You can try to move through the space of one enemy. You [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] up to half your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]]. During this movement, attempt an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Agility Skill Check\|Agility Skill Check]] against the enemy's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] as soon as you try to enter its space. You can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Slip Past\|Slip Past]] using any type of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]]. Using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Slip Past\|Slip Past]] through a willing creature doesn't require a skill check. 

Success: You gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Swift\|Swift]] condition. If you don't have enough [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] to move all the way through its space, you get the same effect as a failure, unless you use 2 actions for [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Slip Past\|Slip Past]], which allows you to move at your normal speed.
Failure: Your movement ends, and you trigger reactions as if you had moved out of the space you started in.
