Requirements: You must be [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] 

---
You attempt to move to another place while staying [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] . [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]]  up to half your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]]. When you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sneak\|Sneak]] you must end your movement in a position where you would be [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]].  You can spend up to 3 actions to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sneak\|Sneak]] allowing you to move further.
You roll a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Subterfuge Skill Check\|Subterfuge Skill Check]] and record your resulting result as a DC for any seekers

---
Success: You manage to sneak to your destination and remain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] 
Failure: You give away your position and are no longer [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]]
