You scan an area for signs of creatures or objects, possibly including secret doors or hazards. Choose an area to scan. The director determines the area you can scan with one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Spot\|Spot]] action in a 5x5 burst  The Director might impose a penalty if you search far away from you or adjust the number of actions it takes to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Spot\|Spot]] a particularly cluttered area.

Make single secret [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]] against to the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Subterfuge\|Subterfuge]] DC of any [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] creatures in the area, or the DC to detect each object in the area (as determined by the director or by someone Concealing the Object). 

---
Critical Success: Any undetected or hidden entity you critically succeeded against is no longer [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]]. You learn the location of objects in the area you critically succeeded against.
Success: Any undetected entity you succeeded against becomes hidden from you instead of undetected, and any hidden entity you succeeded against becomes observed by you. You learn the location of any object or get a clue to its whereabouts, as determined by the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]].
Failure: Nothing

---
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Spot\|Spot]] has a range of 100, for every range increment above 100, you take a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]].
    - You automatically Critically fail a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Spot\|Spot]] check on the third increment or higher.

---
In the case of a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Trap\|Trap]], the  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]] is made against the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] of the creature that deployed the trap.
