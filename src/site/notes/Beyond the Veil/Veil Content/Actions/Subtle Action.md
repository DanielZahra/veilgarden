> You can use your abilities or take actions while remaining in hiding.

Requirements: You must be [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] 

---
- When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Subtle Action\|Subtle Action]] the following ability or action used can be hidden from others. In [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Encounter Mode\|Encounter Mode]] you can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Subtle Action\|Subtle Action]] at the end of your turn and activate it at the start of your next turn. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Subtle Action\|Subtle Action]] can be used with any action or ability. If the ability or action you use has a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]]  trait, [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Subtle Action\|Subtle Action]] fails automatically.
- Using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Subtle Action\|Subtle Action]] starts [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Encounter Mode\|Encounter Mode]] unless the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] indicates that it is not necessary.
- You roll a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Subterfuge Skill Check\|Subterfuge Skill Check]]

---
Success: On a success you retain your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] condition.
Failure: You are no longer [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hidden\|Hidden]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]].
