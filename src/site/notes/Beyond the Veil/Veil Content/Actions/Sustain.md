- Some Abilities, Features or Items require a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] action to activate them.
- If the effect has a sustained duration, its duration extends until the end of your next turn. (Sustaining more than once in the same turn doesn't extend the duration to subsequent turns.) 
- When you use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] ability initially, the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] effect can also be triggered immediately as part of the action that is used to activate the ability.
- If an ability can be sustained but doesn't list how long, it can be sustained up to 1 minutes or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- An effect might list an additional benefit that occurs if you Sustain it, and this can even appear on effects that don't have a sustained duration. If the effect has both a special benefit and a sustained duration, your Sustain action extends the duration as well as having the special benefit.
- If your Sustain action is [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Interrupt\|Interrupt]], the ability ends.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Sustain\|Sustain]] abilities can be used once per turn.
