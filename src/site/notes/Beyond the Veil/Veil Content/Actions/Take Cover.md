> [Use something to avoid harm to yourself.

Requirements: You are in a position that allows you to gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]].

---
- You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take Cover\|Take Cover]] from up to 1 spaces away and as part of the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take Cover\|Take Cover]] you can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]] 1 space to move into [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]].
- You gain , [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light Cover\|Light Cover]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Standard Cover\|Standard Cover]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Heavy Cover\|Heavy Cover]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]] lasts until you use any action with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] or the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]] is destroyed.
