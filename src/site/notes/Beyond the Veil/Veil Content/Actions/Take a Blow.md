> Take a blow for your allies.

Trigger: An adjacent creature is about to take damage from [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] 

---
You can use this [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaction\|Reaction]] to stand in the way of the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Attack\|Attack]] and take the damage yourself.  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take a Blow\|Take a Blow]] can only be used if the targeted creature was the only target of the attack, and it was an attack that is not auditory or an environmental effects that you cannot block. When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Take a Blow\|Take a Blow]] you do not apply your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Basic Defenses\|Basic Defenses]] but if you have a damage [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]], it applies.
