> Trip an opponent

Requirements: You have at least one hand free and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Size Difference\|Size Difference]] applies.

---

You try to knock a creature to the ground. Attempt a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] check against the target's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]].

Critical Success: The target falls, lands [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] , and takes 2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Crushing Damage\|Crushing Damage]].
Success: The target falls and lands [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]].
Critical Failure: You lose your balance, fall, and land [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]].
