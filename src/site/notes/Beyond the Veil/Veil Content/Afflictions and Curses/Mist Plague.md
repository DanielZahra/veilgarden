### Curse

Within [[Aeternum Lore/Places of Aeternum/Mistvale\|Mistvale]] the spiritual mist can sometimes plague people. After some time their body starts to dematerialize and become misty. Slowly deteriorating and becoming nothing.

### Possible Healing

The [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Organizations of Aeternum/Mist Reavers\|Mist Reavers]] are said to have a powerful drug that condenses the mists within one’s body using the mists to gain power instead of losing their body to it. However the side effects might not be worth living for.
