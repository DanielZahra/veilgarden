>Your body starts turning to stone

- The creature’s body becomes an Object. 
- A creature that stays Petrified for 30 days, dies and cannot be revived.
- If the Object is destroyed, the creature also dies and cannot be revived.

### Healing

- A piece of the body or magic essence of what caused the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Afflictions and Curses/Petrification\|Petrification]].
- Bury the petrified creature object in mud.
- Perform a softening ritual that takes 10 hours.
