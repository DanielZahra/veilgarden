### Curse

This curse cannot be removed per se, creatures that have a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Afflictions and Curses/Shared Soul\|Shared Soul]] have to live with it. This can be a minor curse that comes at birth such as [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Species of Aeternum/Celestial/Lightborn\|Lightborn]] or [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Species of Aeternum/Fiend/Fiendspawn\|Fiendspawn]], which alters their looks. In other cases such as revival of [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Species of Aeternum/Unliving/Nosferatu\|Nosferatu]] soul take over the impact is much more severe.
