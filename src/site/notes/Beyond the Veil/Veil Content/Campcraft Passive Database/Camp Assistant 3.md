The party gains an NPC/Assistant that helps them manage the camp, inventory and Assist Downtime Activities. The party gains one additional Downtime Activities. (This is a single activity and not one per player character). The NPC is created by the Director. The skill checks made by the NPC are still rolled by any of the Player Characters.
