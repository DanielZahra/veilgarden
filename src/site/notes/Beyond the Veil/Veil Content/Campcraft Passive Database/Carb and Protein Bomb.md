When preparing Carb and Protein Bomb you can choose between: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Agility\|Agility]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Restoration\|Restoration]]. You start the next day with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Boon\|Boon]] for the chosen skill, this lasts until End of Next Rest/Reused. You can only have one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Supplement\|Supplement]] effect active. This buff is gained through the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Cooking\|Cooking]] downtime activity.
