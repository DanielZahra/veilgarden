When preparing Fortune Cookies, you gain a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Supplement\|Supplement]] that allow you to re-roll a single Veil Roll and take the new result. This effect can be only used once per Rest. You can only have one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Supplement\|Supplement]] effect active. This buff is gained through the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Cooking\|Cooking]] downtime activity.
