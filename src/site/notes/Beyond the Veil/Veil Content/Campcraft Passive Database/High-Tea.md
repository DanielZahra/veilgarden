When preparing High-Tea you can choose between: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Perception\|Perception]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Influence\|Influence]] You start the next day with a Boon for the chosen skill, this lasts until End of Next Rest/Reused. You can only have one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Supplement\|Supplement]] effect active. This buff is gained through the Cooking downtime activity.
