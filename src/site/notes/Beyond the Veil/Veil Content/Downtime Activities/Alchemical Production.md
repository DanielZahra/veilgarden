> You spend additional time to increase the yield of consumables created when using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Alchemy Kit\|Alchemy Kit]].

Choose a single Consumable item to focus on and make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Alchemy Kit\|Alchemy Kit]]  Skill check the DC is based on [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Item DC\|Item DC]].

**Critical Success**: You double the total yield.
**Success**: You increase the total yield by half of the normally produced stack to a minimum of 1.
