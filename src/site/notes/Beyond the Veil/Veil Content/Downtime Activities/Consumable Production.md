> You spend additional time to increase the yield of consumables 

Choose a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Consumable\|Consumable]] item to focus on and make the required  Skill check the DC is based on [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Item DC\|Item DC]].

**Critical Success**: You double the total yield.
**Success**: You increase the total yield by half of the normally produced stack to a minimum of 1.
