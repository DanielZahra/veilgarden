- You spend your downtime preparing a special meal to prepare your group for what is to come.
- This gives the group a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Supplement\|Supplement]] buff.
- No Skill Check Required but you need to have a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Culinary Kit\|Culinary Kit]] 
- You and your allies can only choose one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Cooking\|Cooking]] activity per rest.
