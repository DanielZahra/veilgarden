> [!tip] 💡
> Use the disguise station to create a disguise, including props, costumes and makeup if necessary.

You create a set of disguises, costumes and makeup to pass yourself and other allies off as someone or something else.

When you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Create a Disguise\|Create a Disguise]] you roll a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Subterfuge\|Subterfuge]] skill check.  The result is then used as a DC for any checks made to detect your deception.

---

A creature typically has no reason to roll against your Disguise unless you’re trying to: 

- Imitate someone they know.
- You are interacting with them directly which can cause suspicion.
- You’re in a place that you’re not supposed to be in.
- Your actions make you stand out.
- etc
