> You move around a settlement, talk to people and try to gather information about a particular topic.

You ask a question that needs answers.

---
**Critical Success**: You gather all there is to know from the local populace.
**Success**: You gather some information about the topic.
**Failure**: You gather no information.
**Critical Failure**: You get cheated into buying false information you lose 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]].
