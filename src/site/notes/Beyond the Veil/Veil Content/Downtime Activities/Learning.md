> To use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Learning\|Learning]] downtime activity you must have an entity to help you learn a topic.

Requirement: To study a domain, you must have an object that contains study material, or be in a situation where you can learn (such as having a teacher or be in a place of learning). The Director can clarify whether you have enough material to take the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Learning\|Learning]] Activity.

---
You spend downtime studying appropriate material. Roll a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Smarts Skill Check\|Smarts Skill Check]] **+ Learning bonuses**; succeed if the total meets or exceed your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Knowledge\|Knowledge]] DC. On failure, you gain **Deep Study** on your **next** attempt for [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Learning\|Learning]] this Domain (stacking for the same Domain).

Success: You gain the Domain as part of your known knowledge.
Failure: You gain [[Deep Study\|Deep Study]] + 2

---
[[Deep Study\|Deep Study]]: When you fail a Learning Roll, the next learning roll about the same Domain is made with a Bonus. This Bonus stacks on itself to ensure that eventually you succeed.
