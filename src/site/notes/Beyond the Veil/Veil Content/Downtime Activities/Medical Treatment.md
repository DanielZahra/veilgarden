- You spend downtime treating yourself or an ally to recover. DC is 15. During [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]] you can help recover more than 2 [[Reaper's Mark\|Reaper's Mark]]s. 

**Critical Success: **Recover all Marks**
****Success: **Recover 1 Additional Marks**
****Critical Failure: **Your treatment creates a complication and the creature only recovers 1 Mark, Total.
