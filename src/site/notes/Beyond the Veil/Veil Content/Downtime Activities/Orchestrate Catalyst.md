- During this downtime activity, you can create a catalyst, and store a single ability within. This ability can be used once per rest.
- Catalysts are usually rings, necklances etc. To activate a catalyst you must have a free hand that can touch the catalyst.
- The ability chosen can be any ability of adept level, from Disciplines or Equipment Masteries. For equipment mastery, the Catalyst cannot be activated unless you have the required Equipment held or equipped.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Orchestrate Catalyst\|Orchestrate Catalyst]] costs 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]]. (This is the cost to buy the item).
