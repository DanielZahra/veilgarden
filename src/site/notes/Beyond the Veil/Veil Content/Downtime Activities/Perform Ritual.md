> Some rituals need to be performed as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Downtime Activity\|Downtime Activity]].

When rituals require skill checks, use the following:

Primary Skill DC X

Secondary Skill DC X-5

---

One can immediately attempt the primary skill check against the DC. This is often hard or risky to do. Before making any rolls, the ritualists must all be declared. Once the ritual starts interruptions or new additions cause it to fail.

Secondary Skill Checks are always 5 DC less than the primary.

**Critical Success:** Primary Skill check gets a +4 Bonus ******Success: **Primary Skill Check gets a +2 Bonus** ****Critical Failure:** Primary skill check gets a -4 Penalty.

---

Example Ritual

If the Ritual DC is 20. The primary DC is 20, and the secondary DC is 15. If the party makes 2 successful secondary checks, the primary roll is made with a +4.
