- You consult your owned maps, the terrain and use your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Navigation Kit\|Navigation Kit]] to find the best possible route for the coming day.
- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Smarts\|Smarts]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Perception\|Perception]] skill check. On a success on the next day you cover 1.5 times the distance you normally would.
