- You spend your downtime researching a topic. This allows you to make a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Knowledge\|Knowledge]] check provided you have a source for such knowledge such as books or people with the knowledge willing to help you.
- To make this [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Knowledge\|Knowledge]] you don’t need to know the Domain yourself.
