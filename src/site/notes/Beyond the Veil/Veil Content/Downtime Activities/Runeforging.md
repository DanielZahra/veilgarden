> [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Runeforging\|Runeforging]] is the process where a skilled artisan embed a rune within a weapon, this alters the very nature of the weapons or armor, including magically altering the material of the weapon or giving them elemental properties and giving it magical qualities.

# Embedding

- Embedding runes is a process which takes time but cannot damage the weapon or the rune.
- Weapon
    - You can Embed a single Weapon Rune into an appropriate Weapon Type.
    - Runes for [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Unarmed Attack\|Unarmed Attack]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Natural Weapon\|Natural Weapon]] are applied through [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]].
- Armor
    - You can Embed a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] Rune into an appropriate [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] Type.

# Removing a Rune

Removing a rune from an existing weapon on the other-hand is tricky business and requires a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] skill check through the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Artificing Kit\|Artificing Kit]], the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Item DC\|Item DC]] is based on the mastery level of the rune.

- Adept Rune DC 8
- Veteran Rune DC 12
- Master Rune DC 16

Success: Both item and rune are preserved and usable**
****Failure: **During extraction the rune’s power shatters, you can choose to destroy the item or the rune, preserving the other item. Your choice.**
****Critical Failure: **Both item and the rune shatter and cannot be used permanently.
