> [!tip] 💡
> Sell the loot you have earned, pillaged or stolen.

You find buyers and make deals with the locals to get the most out of your loot. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Sell Loot\|Sell Loot]] allows you to sell magical gear, or precious individual items in your possession. You make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Influence\|Influence]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Smarts\|Smarts]] skill check. After you make this skill check, you cannot repeat the skill check within the same location for at least a week. When you make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Sell Loot\|Sell Loot]] check, you can sell multiple items at once.

---

**Critical Success: **You can sell at full value +2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]] if the at least once of the items has 4 or more [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]] value. (You only get +2 total for all items)**
****Success: **You can sell at full value.**
****Failure: **You can sell at half value.
**Critical Failure: **You fail to sell at any value.

---

Most sales of items with 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]] are not worth’s a trader’s time and it’s considered an automatic critical failure.
