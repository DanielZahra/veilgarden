> [!tip] 💡
> You spend additional time to increase the yield of consumables created when using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Tinkering Tools\|Tinkering Tools]].

Choose a single Consumable item to focus on and make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Tinkering Tools\|Tinkering Tools]]  ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]] ) Skill check the DC of the skill check is based on the item.

**Critical Success: **You double the total yield.**
****Success: **You increase the total yield by half of the normally produced stack to a minimum of 1.
