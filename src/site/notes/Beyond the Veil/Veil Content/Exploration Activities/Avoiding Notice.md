- You travel carefully and attempt to remain out of sight. When encountering an entity that would potentially spot you, you instead make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Subterfuge Skill Check\|Subterfuge Skill Check]] and the creature does not notice you, or you are [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Undetected\|Undetected]] from the creature.
- When entering [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Encounter Mode\|Encounter Mode]] and you succeed at [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Avoiding Notice\|Avoiding Notice]], you can deploy into [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Cover\|Cover]] or a good hiding spot. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] will indicate the locations.
