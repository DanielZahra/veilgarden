> [!tip] 💡
> Keeping a lookout for dangerous creatures or ambushes.

You make a secret [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]] and, on success you can spot any other creature. When an encouter starts and you had [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Keep Watch\|Keep Watch]] your skill check result might expose enemes during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Deployment\|Deployment]] and show you their location on a battlemap if it is being used.

---

When other creatures are trying to ambush the party, hide from the party, or are simply present, the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]] makes a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Secret\|Secret]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]] for you. The result of this roll or the highest [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Awareness\|Awareness]] of the group is used as a DC for any enemies [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Subterfuge\|Subterfuge]] checks.
