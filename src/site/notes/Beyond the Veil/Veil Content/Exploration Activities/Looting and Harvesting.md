You spend 10 minutes, looking for loot, skinning a beast, identifying valuable objects

Depending on the looting you are doing, the Director might call for different skill checks. (eg. Perception can be used when you’re looking for objects, while tinkering can be used when you’re extracting treasure from a device.) [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Restoration\|Restoration]] or a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Knowledge\|Knowledge]] check might be used for skinning an animal etc.

**Critical Success: **The Loot Size is increased by 2 (maximum of 8)**
****Success: **Loot Size is increased by 1**
****Failure: **Loot Size is decreased by 1**
****Critical Failure: **Loot Size is decreased by 2 (minimum of 0)
**Looting is not replaced by ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scavenge\|Scavenge]]****. And while all loot gives ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]]**** not all loot can give magical treasures.**
