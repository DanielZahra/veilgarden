> [!tip] 💡
> You make sure that during exploration you and your allies keep a strong formation and if danger breaks out you’re always ready.

- You make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Influence Skill Check\|Influence Skill Check]] against the DC of an encounter. 
- On a success you increase the size of your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Deployment\|Deployment]] area for your party.
- Alternatively, on a success you can also deploy in positions you choose, if normally you would be locked to a static position. This [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Deployment\|Deployment]] alternative does not increase the area.
