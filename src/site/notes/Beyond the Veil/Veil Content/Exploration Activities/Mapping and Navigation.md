> [!tip] 💡
> Map your route and avoid getting lost

Requirement: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Navigation Kit\|Navigation Kit]] in [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] 

---

- You lead the group through difficult terrain, dense forests, or confusing dungeon layouts typically making [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]].
- When you map a location, all skills checks in relation to finding your way or avoid getting lost have the DC reduced by 5.
- When using a VTT, Mapping activates Fog of War Visibility. What this means is that when you’re exploring a larger dungeon or area, with Mapping you will see all the place you explore, without it you will see only what you see (assuming it’s dark) and you have to remember the rest.
