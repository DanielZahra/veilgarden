> You perform a ritual from your [[Ritual Tome\|Ritual Tome]]

When a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Ritual\|Ritual]] requires a skill check, you can use any skill that is allowed within the ritual. To initiative a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Ritual\|Ritual]], one participant is designated as the [[Primary ritual caster\|Primary ritual caster]]. Their skill check is rolled against the Skill Check DC of the ritual. The primary ritual caster can immediately roll against the Skill Check DC without assistance but this is often risky.

---
All other participants who are assisting declare their intent to help in the ritual make an assisting roll which is the (Skill Check DC - 4). All assisting rolls must be declared and rolled before the primary ritualist makes their check. Once the ritual starts interruptions or new additions cause it to fail. Upon rolling their aid in the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Ritual\|Ritual]] is resolved as follows.

**Critical Success:** Primary Skill check gets a +4 Bonus 
**Success:** Primary Skill Check gets a +2 Bonus
**Critical Failure:** Primary skill check gets a -4 Penalty.

---
Example Ritual:
If the Ritual DC is 20. The assisting DC is 16.  If the party makes 2 successful secondary checks, the primary roll is made with a +4.
