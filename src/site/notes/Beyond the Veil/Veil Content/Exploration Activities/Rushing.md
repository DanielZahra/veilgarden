You move as fast as you can, while rushing you cannot use other passive [[Exploration Activities\|Exploration Activities]]. You move at 3 times your speed.

You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Rushing\|Rushing]] once without any penalty. On any subsequent use, [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Rushing\|Rushing]] requires a DC 8 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Athletics Skill Check\|Athletics Skill Check]] (Group Check), which is increased by 2 for every subsequent use.

Example: If you use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Rushing\|Rushing]] for once in a dungeon turn and use it again in the next turn, you have to make a DC 8 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Athletics\|Athletics]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Group Skill Check\|Group Skill Check]]. If you use it again, the DC becomes 10.

---

When the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Group Skill Check\|Group Skill Check]] is made, on a success the party keeps up their [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Rushing\|Rushing]]. On Failure, the party can slow down or decide to abandon the creature or creatures that are failing, possibly turning the check into a success, but the creature is left behind.

**Critical Success: **You gain 2 successes**
****Success: **You gain 1 success**
****Failure: **You gain a failure**
****Critical Failure: **You gain 2 failures
