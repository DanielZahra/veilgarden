> [!tip] 💡
> While you scour a location you’re looking for hidden objects, weird switches, odd items, secret passages or hidden treasure.

While [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scouring\|Scouring]], you tap walls, lift rugs, look in vases and pots.

[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scouring\|Scouring]] is always rolled as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Secret\|Secret]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Tinkering Skill Check\|Tinkering Skill Check]]. This check is rolled by the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]], when you call to make an active [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scouring\|Scouring]] check, it’s still a secret roll but it takes 10 minutes.

[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scouring\|Scouring]] allows you to find: Hidden Traps, hidden treasure, Passages or Doors, Hidden Treasure, Odd items that look out of place.

---

[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Scouring\|Scouring]] does not include keeping a lookout or an ear out for danger creatures, that is done in [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Keep Watch\|Keep Watch]].

---

Environment descriptions that are clearly visible to the party also just described and not part of scouring. Eg. a large treasure chest in the middle of the room is simply there and cannot be missed.
