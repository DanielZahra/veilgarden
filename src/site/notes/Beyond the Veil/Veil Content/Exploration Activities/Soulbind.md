Using magical gear and mana has a toll.  To effectively use magical gear you need to spend time performing a Soulbinding to the magic items. This allows you to channel mana and activate the effects of the items.

- You can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Soulbind\|Soulbind]] up to 6 magical items. 
- You can use[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Soulbind\|Soulbind]] during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Preparation\|Preparation]] or as an [[Exploration Activities\|Exploration Activities]]
- Multiple creatures can [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Soulbind\|Soulbind]] to the same item.
- Once a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Soulbind\|Soulbind]] is created, it cannot be released until you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]].
- A [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Soulbind\|Soulbind]] lasts until you rest or die.
