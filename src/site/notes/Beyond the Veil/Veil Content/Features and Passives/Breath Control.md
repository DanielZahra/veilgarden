Description:: You gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Abilities/Second Wind\|Second Wind]] Ability. When you make [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Athletics Skill Check\|Athletics Skill Check]] for running long distances you increase your degree of success by 1. You can hold your breath for 10 times the normal amount. Damage you take from suffocation is halved.
