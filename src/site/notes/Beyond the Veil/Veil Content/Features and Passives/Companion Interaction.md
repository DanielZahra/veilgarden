- Your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] can use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action to interact with objects in the environment. 
- Examples
    - Carry an item from one creature or place to another
    - Flip a switch
    - Snatch a key, 
    - etc.
- They cannot use consumables (that is done with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Features and Passives/Companion Loadout\|Companion Loadout]])
