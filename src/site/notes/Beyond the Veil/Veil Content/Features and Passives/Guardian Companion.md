- Your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] has features which resemble weapons or can use actual weapons.
    - When choosing weapons as a function the chosen weapon is merged with the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]]’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Natural Weapon\|Natural Weapon]], this includes all their basic traits.
    - If they are using features which resemble weapons, these are chosen when you create, summon or find the with through a ritual.
        - e.g. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Bind Ossified Ally\|Bind Ossified Ally]] or [[Beastmaster's Bond\|Beastmaster's Bond]] 
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Features and Passives/Guardian Companion\|Guardian Companion]] allows your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Companion\|Companion]] to wield a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/One Handed\|One Handed]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]] weapon  and a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Weapons/Shield\|Shield]].
- They also gain the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Guard\|Guard]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Parry\|Parry]] ﻿actions.
