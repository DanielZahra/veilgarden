>You have trained your sense of smell to match that of a dog or wolf. It is now a reliable sense to move you through dangerous area where vision is not reliable.

- When making a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Perception Skill Check\|Perception Skill Check]] during [[Exploration Activities\|Exploration Activities]], if you are in [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Darkness\|Darkness]] and cannot see you can use your smell.
- This avoids the automatic critical failure applied from [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blind\|Blind]].
