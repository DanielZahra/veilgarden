- Once per turn, when you achieve a Critical Success on a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] roll against a foe, you can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Free Action\|Free Action]] to give a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Bane\|Bane]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] to any other foe within [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range 12\|Range 12]].
