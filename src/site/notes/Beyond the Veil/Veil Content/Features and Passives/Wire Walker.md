>Maybe you were meant to work in the circus, you're incredibly adept at walking on tight wires or ropes.

- When you make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Check/Agility Skill Check\|Agility Skill Check]] to maintain your balance while walking over a very narrow space you increase your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/degree of success\|degree of success]] by 1. 
- These checks are typically done while you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Step\|Step]] not [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Move\|Move]].
