- A backpack holds all your gear. Use this to store items you might need to use but that are not in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]]. It takes 3 Actions to use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Backpack interaction\|Backpack interaction]].
- You (as a party) shares the same spaces [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Backpack\|Backpack]] and it can contain 20 items. (Can be increased through [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Campcraft/Survival & Wellbeing\|Survival & Wellbeing]])
- You still need to wear a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Backpack\|Backpack]] to have access to it.
- Items with a stack, can 1 backpack slot.
