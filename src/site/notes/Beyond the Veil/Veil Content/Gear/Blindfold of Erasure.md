> [!tip] 💡
> While wearing the blindfold and meditating you can control the mana and erase abilities or skills you have gained. This meditations feels as if you are running a piece of wet cloth over a chalkboard removing some of the writing.

- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Blindfold of Erasure\|Blindfold of Erasure]] can be used to forget an ability of skill feat.
- It can be used by taking the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]] downtime activity. When the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]] is complete, the ability or skill feat is removed and you gain the XP spend back.
