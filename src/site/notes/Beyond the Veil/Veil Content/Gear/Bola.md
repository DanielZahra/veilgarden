- You can throw a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Bola\|Bola]] up to 12 meters , this allows you to make a ranged [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Trip\|Trip]] attempt.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Bola\|Bola]] must be held, but does not need a free hand to use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Trip\|Trip]].
- When you fail to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Trip\|Trip]] a creature you must move to the location of the creature at the time of the attempt or an adjacent spot and use an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] to recover your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Bola\|Bola]].
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Bola\|Bola]] can trip up to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Large\|Large]] creature size.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Bola\|Bola]]  must be in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] to be used.
