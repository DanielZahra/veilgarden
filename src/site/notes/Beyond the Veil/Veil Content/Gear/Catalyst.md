- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Catalyst\|Catalyst]] can be designed as rings, circlets, necklaces, earrings etc. Typically worn anywhere on the body. You can prepare a maximum of one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Catalyst\|Catalyst]]  per day during [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Preparation\|Preparation]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Catalyst\|Catalyst]] need one free hand to be activated. Their activation cost is based on the ability stored within.
- They are created using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Orchestrate Catalyst\|Orchestrate Catalyst]].
- A [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Catalyst\|Catalyst]] can be used once per [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]].
