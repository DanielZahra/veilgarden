- Putting on the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Cold Weather Gear\|Cold Weather Gear]] takes one hour. When in weather conditions relating to cold, saving throws made to stave off the cold are always one degree of success higher.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Cold Weather Gear\|Cold Weather Gear]] must be in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] to have effect.
