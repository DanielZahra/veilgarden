> [!note] ⚔️
> A box with a stick foam substance that explodes when a creature moves in its proximity. 

- You deploy a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Trap\|Trap]] in a 3x3 Burst 
- When triggered, the area becomes [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Difficult Terrain 1\|Difficult Terrain 1]].
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hover\|Hover]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Flying\|Flying]] creatures are immune.

**Trigger: **A creature uses an action with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Movement\|Movement]] trait within the area of the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Trap\|Trap]].
