- Putting on the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Heat Weather Gear\|Heat Weather Gear]] takes one hour. When in weather conditions relating to sweltering heat, saving throws made to stave off the said heat are always one degree of success higher.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Heat Weather Gear\|Heat Weather Gear]] must be in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] to have effect.
