>When using a Mana Scroll or [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Soul Fragment\|Soul Fragment]]  your body and mind absorb the knowledge as if you have always knew the ability within. Unfortunately these effects are temporary. Typically a Mana Scroll is created by someone to allow an ability to be used by another creature. A Soul Fragment can be naturally created when a soul gifted in mana control dies, leaving a crystallized memory behind.

- A [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)\|Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)]] has an ability imprinted within which allows you to use the ability.
- You can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Meditation\|Meditation]] downtime activity to consume (read/learn) the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)\|Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)]] and you learn the ability within until your next [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)\|Mana Scroll-Soul Fragment (Adept)]] are typically followed by an ability name which indicates which ability is stored within.

---
- Alternatively you can use a Mana Scroll through your [Loadout](app://obsidian.md/Loadout), this method costs 3 [Interact](app://obsidian.md/Interact) actions and it consumes the scroll after 1 use.
