> [!note] ⚔️
> You deploy a [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Manatech\|Manatech]] extra dimensional trap that activates when a creature steps inside, dropping them in a deep hole.

- You deploy a 1x1 Burst
- A Creature that moves into the space starts [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Falling\|Falling]].
    - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Minor Void Pit\|Minor Void Pit]] is 2 spaces deep. A [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/High Jump\|High Jump]] is required leave the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Minor Void Pit\|Minor Void Pit]].
- A creature can attempt a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grab On\|Grab On]] when falling. 
- Creatures larger than the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Minor Void Pit\|Minor Void Pit]] cannot fall in it.
