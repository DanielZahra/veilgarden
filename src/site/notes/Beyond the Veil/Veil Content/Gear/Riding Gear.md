- Putting on the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Riding Gear\|Riding Gear]] takes one hour. This includes gear for the rider but also the mount saddle. Saving throws or skill checks made that that test your riding ability such as not falling off the mount, the results are always one degree of success higher.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Riding Gear\|Riding Gear]] must be in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]] to have effect.
