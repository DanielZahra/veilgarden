- This is a 20 meter of strong rope.
- It comes with some pitons to allow it to be tied and secured to different locations and also it has knots to allow easy scaling or decending in dangerous situations.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Rope Gear\|Rope Gear]] gives you a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Boon\|Major Boon]] to checks made when using it to climb, or descend.
