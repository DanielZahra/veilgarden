- You throw the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Smoke Bomb\|Smoke Bomb]] in a point and it creates a 4x4 Burst of smoke until [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/End of Encounter\|End of Encounter]].
- Creatures in the smoke are [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Concealed\|Concealed]].
- Creatures in the smoke gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blurred Vision\|Blurred Vision]].
- When targeting an entity and the smoke is between you (line of sight) and the entity, the entity is also [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Concealed\|Concealed]] even if neither of you are in the smoke.
