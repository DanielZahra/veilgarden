> [!note] ⚔️
> The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Tazer Cube\|Tazer Cube]] has a quick charge of energy that zaps the muscles which breaks the tension of a grapple.

- You make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Prowess\|Prowess]] check with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]] against a creature’s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Fortitude\|Fortitude]]. If the creature has another creature in  [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]] on a success, you break the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Grapple\|Grapple]].
- You can also use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Tazer Cube\|Tazer Cube]] on an ally to allow to make an [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Escape\|Escape]] check with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Advantage\|Advantage]] as a reaction.
