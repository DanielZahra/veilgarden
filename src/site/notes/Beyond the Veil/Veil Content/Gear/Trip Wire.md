> [!note] ⚔️
> An invisible magical nylon, that tangles itself to anything that walks though it.

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Trip Wire\|Trip Wire]]  has a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Thrown/Thrown\|Thrown]] range of 6
- You deploy a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Trap\|Trap]] in a 3x1 Line
- When triggered, the creature must make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reflex\|Reflex]] Saving throw, they gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Prone\|Prone]] on failure, and an additional 2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Crushing Damage\|Crushing Damage]] on critical failure.
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Hover\|Hover]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Flying\|Flying]] creatures are immune.
