- The party can carry [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Wearable Infinite Lantern\|Wearable Infinite Lantern]] on their belts, backpack attachments etc. You can use a single [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Actions/Interact\|Interact]] action to turn the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Wearable Infinite Lantern\|Wearable Infinite Lantern]] on or off.
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Wearable Infinite Lantern\|Wearable Infinite Lantern]] has [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Light/Light 4\|Light 4]]. To use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Wearable Infinite Lantern\|Wearable Infinite Lantern]], it must be placed in your [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Loadout\|Loadout]].
