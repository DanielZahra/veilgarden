# [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Bladebinder\|Bladebinder]] Core Mastery

- Main Feature
    - In [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Preparation\|Preparation]] you choose a Weapon Family and a Single Weapons to tether your soul to. As an action you can have the weapon hover next to you and you can use this without needing to hold it, as it gains the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Free-Hand\|Free-Hand]]. The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Masteries/Bladebinder\|Bladebinder]] ignores all restrictions of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Free-Hand\|Free-Hand]] 
        - To choose a weapons family you have have the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Equipment Mastery\|Equipment Mastery]]
    - At Veteran you can have 2 weapons hovering next to you (Same family)
    - At Master you can have 3 weapons hovering next to you (Same Family)
- Abilities
    - Adept
        - Melee weapon used at range
        - Range weapon used as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Swift\|Swift]] 
    - Veteran
    - Master
