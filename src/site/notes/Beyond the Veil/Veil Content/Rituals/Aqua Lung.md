> [!note] 
> You prepare dark squid ink to paint a tidal rune directly over your throats. As the magical ink dries, your lungs will temporarily adapt to filter oxygen from the sea or other fresh water sources.

---

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Aqua Lung\|Aqua Lung]] lasts for 24 hours.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Aqua Lung\|Aqua Lung]] gives any creature the ability to ‘breathe’ air through normal or sea water.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Aqua Lung\|Aqua Lung]] can be applied to up to 10 creatures.
