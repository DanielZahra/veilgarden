### Ritual

>You gather a good number of bones, to shape a skeletal being. You use 3 droplets of your own blood to give the skeleton life energy, to bind them to you, and to share your own power with them.

As you channel energy, the bones merge and connect into a being of un-life that is bound to you and does your bidding without asking questions.
