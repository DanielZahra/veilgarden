Bind Spirit is essentially a a stronger version of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Voice the Dead\|Voice the Dead]] but it binds a deal soul to the locaiton, creating an Ethereal ghost

When a spirit is bound, the primary caster give the ghost a promise or contract of sorts that allows the ghost to end the binding on certain conditions. The tougher the conditions the the higher the DC of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Bind Spirit\|Bind Spirit]]
