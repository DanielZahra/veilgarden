> [!note] 
> You place your fingers on the temple of a creature as the ritual goes on, you can see, hear, taste, feel and smell what the creature sees and hears. 

---

**Requirements: **Target creature must be a willing creature.

---

- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Borrowed Senses\|Borrowed Senses]], the primary caster can sense through all the senses of the target creature. (Sight, Hearing, Taste and Feeling).

**Critical Success: **[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Borrowed Senses\|Borrowed Senses]] has a duration of 1 hour.**
****Success: **[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Borrowed Senses\|Borrowed Senses]] has a duration of 10 minutes.
