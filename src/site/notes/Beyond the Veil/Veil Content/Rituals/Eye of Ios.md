- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] has 6 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Flying\|Flying]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Speed\|Speed]] and 8 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Defense\|Defense]].
- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] is destroyed if it takes any damage.
- The primary caster uses their own skill checks for the eye.
    - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] can mainly perceive (sight only) and hide using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Subterfuge\|Subterfuge]].
    - It cannot hear or smell.
- While [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] is active the primary caster becomes [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Conditions/Blind\|Blind]], but can hear and talk normally.
    - You can end [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] as an action.

Critical Success: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] has a duration of 1 hour.
Success: [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Eye of Ios\|Eye of Ios]] has a duration of 10 minutes.
