> You draw a glyph with a drop of your own blood and mud mixed with sand infused in [[Aeternum Lore/Lore Glossary/Mana\|Mana]]. This allows you to perceive the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Belonging\|Glyph of Belonging]] at a 50 kilometer range. 

---
Requirements: The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Belonging\|Glyph of Belonging]] must be placed on a willing creature or object. If the creature dies or object is destroyed, the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Belonging\|Glyph of Belonging]] ends.

---
- You must use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Belonging\|Glyph of Belonging]]and set an object or creature as the 'target', this takes 1 hour.
- At any point you can use an action to pin point the direction of the target as long as they are within 50 kilometers.
- You can only have one [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Belonging\|Glyph of Belonging]] active at a time. 
- The duration is 1 year or until the object or creature is dead or destroyed.
