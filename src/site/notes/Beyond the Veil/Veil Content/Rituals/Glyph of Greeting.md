You prepare a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Greeting\|Glyph of Greeting]] in an unoccupied space. You can record your voice within the glyph’s magic. To a Maximum of 25 words. When a creature moves within 10 of the Glyph, the Glyph will repeat the recorded line every **minute.** The Glyph is also sometimes used as a protection spell against intruders. Any creature not wishing to be detected must make a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Subterfuge\|Subterfuge]] check against the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Greeting\|Glyph of Greeting]].

The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Greeting\|Glyph of Greeting]]'s [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Awareness\|Awareness]] is set to the [[Primary ritual caster\|Primary ritual caster]]'s roll.
