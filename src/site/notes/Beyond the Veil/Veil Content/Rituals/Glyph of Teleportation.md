> [!note] 
> You combine a mixture of [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Materials, Chemicals and Flora/Materials of Aeternum/Aeternite\|Aeternite]] with liquified silver. This mixture is then used as paint to create a magical circle with a doorway within. This locks the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Glyph\|Glyph]] to this location and acts as a doorway to other places in the worlds.

---

**Requirements: The ****[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]]**** need to be placed in a static flat ground or a wall which is at least 5x5 space. Any creature or objects that fit the 5x5 space can travel through.**

---

- Creation (10 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]])
    - The creation process takes 1 Downtime Activity to create
    - DC 15 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]] 
        - You can increase the size of the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] to 10x10, this increases the DC to 20.
- Glyph to Glyph  (1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]])
    - To activate the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]], you simply spend the 1 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]].
    - You can only activate a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] you have traced in your [[Ritual Tome\|Ritual Tome]].
    - No skill checks are involved.
    - Activation takes 10 minutes.
    - With Glyph to Glyph you simple go from one Glyph in your Ritual Tome to another one you have added.
    - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] remains active for 1 minute, or until the primary caster uses 3 actions to close it.
- Return to Glyph (2 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]])
    - You can use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] directly from anywhere and ‘return’ to one of the Glyphs you have traced in your tome.
    - This is a DC10 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Tinkering\|Tinkering]] check.
    - Activation takes 10 minutes.
    - The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] remains active for 1 minute, or until the primary caster uses 3 actions to close it.
- Ritual Memory
    - You can spend 1 hour as an exploration activity to trace a specific [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] in a location to your  [[Ritual Tome\|Ritual Tome]].
    - You can repeat this exploration activity, as many times as you want to add more Glyphs to your tome, this allows you to travel between them.
- Destruction
    - A [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] that gets damaged, does not function and needs to be created from scratch. 
    - The creator of a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Glyph of Teleportation\|Glyph of Teleportation]] can modify the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Glyph\|Glyph]] without damaging it, everyone that would have traced the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Glyph\|Glyph]] before will not be able to use it again without retracing it.
