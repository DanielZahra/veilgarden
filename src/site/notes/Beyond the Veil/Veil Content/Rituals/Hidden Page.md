- When using the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Hidden Page\|Hidden Page]] ritual, you take the contents of a page and make it invisible to the naked eye. 
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Hidden Page\|Hidden Page]] can hide text up to 200 words long. 
- The roll of the [[Primary ritual caster\|Primary ritual caster]] creates a DC for any creature trying to read or perceive text.


Critical Success: Text Hidden for 1 year.
Success: Text Hidden for 1 day.
