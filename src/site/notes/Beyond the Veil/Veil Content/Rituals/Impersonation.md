> [!note] 
> You alter the mana in their air around you. Your looks, voice, psyche, all get altered to appear like your target. The more you know the target the easier it is to assimilate them, over downtime to connect and become your target.

---

**Requirements: To **use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] the primary ritualists but be familiar with the target they are going to impersonate. The ritual DC is based on how well you know the target.

---

- The DC of [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] is based on the Table Below.
- When you make the primary roll, the result is used as a DC for your illusion should any creature require to roll against you.
    - Typically [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Perception\|Perception]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Intuition\|Intuition]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Skills/Smarts\|Smarts]].
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] lasts until you [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]] 
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] changes changes your genetic composition, skin, hair, eyes, vocal chords etc. 
- Limitations: 
    - You cannot use [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] on creatures of a different [[Creature Size\|Creature Size]]. The target must be a person/kin.
    - [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] does not give you the target’s abilities, skills or possessions. 

---

**Critical Success: **You create a perfect [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]]. Any attempt to detect this illusion is made with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]].  Kindreds creatures to your target can be suspicious of you.**
****Success: **You create an imperfect [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Impersonation\|Impersonation]] and those that know the target might see through the illusion. Confidants and Kindreds creatures to your target can be suspicious of you.**
****Failure: **The ritual fails.
