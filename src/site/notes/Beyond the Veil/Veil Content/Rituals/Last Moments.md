>Your eyes glow a pale green and you witness the last moments of the corpse and see what they saw, and hear what they heard in the last minute of life.

- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Last Moments\|Last Moments]] you must target a corpse within [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adjacent\|Adjacent]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range\|Range]]. 
- For [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Last Moments\|Last Moments]] to work, the corpse must have belonged to a creature that had at least sight or hearing.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Last Moments\|Last Moments]] lasts for 1 minute, the last minute of the creature's life.
