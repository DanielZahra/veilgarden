> [!note] 
> You setup a mirror and draw your desired costume, face and minor physical alterations on the mirror itself. When you’re done with this magical ritual the illusion becomes real enough for you to take on the looks that you drew on the mirror, however this fails on touch.

---

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Mirror of Disguises\|Mirror of Disguises]] allows you to[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Create a Disguise\|Create a Disguise]] without the downtime activity, however the disguise is an illusion and not physical, a creature touching the illusion will realise it’s not real.
- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Mirror of Disguises\|Mirror of Disguises]] is limited to one target.
