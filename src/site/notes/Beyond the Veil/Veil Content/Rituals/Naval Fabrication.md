> [!note] 
> The fabricators have to sit in a circle while holding hands very close to a body of water. The materials needed to build your naval vehile need to be placed close by.  Through precise mana control you the primary ritualist uses their knowledge and follows the blueprint for building the vechile while sharing it telepatically with the rest of the ritual members as they slowly fabricate a boat or ship. 
> This magcial ritual ‘eats’ away at the materials prepared as it fabricates the necessary vechile fiber by fiber.

---

**Requirements: **The primary Ritualist needs to have [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Knowledge Topics/Naval and Sailing Knowledge\|Naval and Sailing Knowledge]] Knowledge to use this ritual.

---

- You can use this ritual to summon a: (TBD stats) Cost goes up by type (wealth cost)
