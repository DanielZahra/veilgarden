> [!note] 🗺️
> You create a ritual cirucle with light mana fused paint and place the object in the middle. Small pieces of paper are written, which include questions one might have around the subject object. Once the ritual is concluded. The answers to the questions surface in the mind of the ritualists.

Requirements: You must have the object in question in your possession or touch range.

---

- The Primary for [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Oggetto Historia\|Oggetto Historia]] is 14.
- You must write a number of questions in a specific order. Every question added increases the Primary by 2.
- There is no limit to the questions.
- Some quesitons might have no answer.
- Once used on an object, this ritual cannot be used on the same object for a **year.**
- Example Questions
    - Who created it?
    - When was it created?
    - Who last held it?
