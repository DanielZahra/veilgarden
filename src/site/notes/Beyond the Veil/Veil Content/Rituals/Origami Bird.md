> [!note] 
> You write a message that is upto 50 words long on a parchment,  fold this parchment into the shape of a bird. You the ritual is infused with mana, the origami birds takes the shape of an ethereal bird. The bird understand the destination and starts flying to the recipient as it flies through the ethereal plane.

When arriving at its destination the bird shifts itself back into a parchment in the hands of the target.

---

**Requirements: **The primary caster MUST know the target of the ritual and must have met them physicially to be able to target them.

---

- The message can be written in any language as long as it is 50 words.
- The bird travels at around 1000 kilometers per 24 hours.
- When the target is more than 1 week away (in travel time), the birds arrives on the 7th day regardless of the distance.
    - This is also the time it takes for the message to arrive on other planes of existence.

---

**Critical Success: **The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Origami Bird\|Origami Bird]] arrives at the destination instantly.**
****Success: **The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Origami Bird\|Origami Bird]] travels normally.
