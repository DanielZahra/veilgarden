> [!note] 
> You prepare the bodies of the dead. Each culture handles this differently. The most typical rite of sending is a pray performance in words and dance which is said to help usher to the soul to their respective deserved god and plane and into the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Soul Cycle/Soul Cycle\|Soul Cycle]]. Several ritualists make their own offering and prayers.

---

Requirements: The bodies of the dead must be within a 50 radius of the ritual.

---

- The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Rites of Sending\|Rites of Sending]] move the souls of the dead into the [[Notion/Aeternum Wiki/Soul Cycle/Soul Cycle\|Soul Cycle]] and their bodies typically cannot be revived into or turn into undead creatures.
