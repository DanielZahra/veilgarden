> [!note] 
> You draw a focusing circle and focus your energies on dispelling a magical effect within your reach.

---

Requirements: You must be within 5 range of an effect to target it and the effect effect must be dispellable, ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Dispel Effect\|Dispel Effect]])

---

- When using [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Ritual of Dispelling\|Ritual of Dispelling]]  you must target a single effect; this effect can be on a creature or in a space.
- When targeting an effect on a creature, you must be able to see the creature to target the effect.

---

Critical Success: You dispel the effect completely.
Success: You dispel the effect for 10 minutes.
Failure: No Effect.
