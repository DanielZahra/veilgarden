- The target creature must be within [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adjacent\|Adjacent]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range\|Range]], but they don't have to be a participant or willing.
- You magically sense the creature's Sense of Truth. 
- As soon as the creature lies, you can immediately tell. 
- The ritual effect lasts for 3 questions or 5 minutes whichever is over first.
- The DC of the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Ritual of Truth\|Ritual of Truth]] is 15 or the target's [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Will\|Will]] whichever is higher.

**Critical Success**: You can sense the creature's intent to lie even when they don't speak.
**Success**: When a creature talks you can sense whether there is a lie in their words or not.
