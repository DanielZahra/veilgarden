>You unlock the essence of your vitality, life and endurance. You become 10 years younger and healthier

---

Requirements: This ritual can only be performed/attempted once per target creature.

---

- On a success the target gain the following:
    - 2 maximum [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]] 
    - They become 10 years younger
    - Remove all [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Affliction\|Affliction]]
- On Failure the target gains a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Curse\|Curse]].
