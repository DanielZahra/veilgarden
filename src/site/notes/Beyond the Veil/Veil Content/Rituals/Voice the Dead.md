> A small cut on your finger is enough to release enough blood for you to create a ritual circle from your own blood. You anatomically build whatever you can from the dead flesh or skeletal remains of a creature; the better you can compose this, the more likely the ritual will succeed.

As you activate the ritual, you must recite the exact words ‘Rise o’ dead one and use me as a vessel for your voice’. You can do this in 3 languages to make sure the spirit understands at least one of these languages.

As the ritual unfolds, mana infuses the dead creature, and a spiritual energy slowly starts inhibiting the one who spoke the exact words. As long as the caster permits it, the spirits slowly takes over their voice and hearing, their eyes glow in a deep light green colour. As the dead spirit now possesses the caster, you can ask your questions.

---

Requirements: You need a substantial (head, skull, heart, arm, legs, etc.) part of the body, vital organ, or skeleton of a creature to use this Ritual.

- [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Rituals/Voice the Dead\|Voice the Dead]]’s uses [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Tier DC Modifier\|Tier DC Modifier]].
- Any of the casters involved can recite the necessary words.
- The corpse must understand one of the languages used; otherwise, the Ritual fails.
- The dead creature must not have been dead for longer than 200 years.
- Whether this ritual fails or succeeds, it cannot be repeated on the same creature for eternity. 
- When the ritual succeeds, the dead spirit uses the host as a voice and ears, but the host can still speak with their own voice.
- You can ask the dead spirit four questions, and it can answer truthfully or deceive, as it has its own whims and wants.
