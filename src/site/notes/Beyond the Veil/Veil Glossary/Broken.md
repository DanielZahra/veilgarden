- Broken is used for [[Gear and Consumables\|Gear and Consumables]]. A broken item cannot be used and also cannot be repaired with [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Field Repair\|Field Repair]]. 
- You can restore a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Broken\|Broken]] item with the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Gear Maintenance\|Gear Maintenance]] [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Downtime Activity\|Downtime Activity]].
- Broken [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Weapons/Weapons\|Weapons]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Protection\|Protection]] receive a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Major Bane\|Major Bane]] to Defense or Prowess until you Rest.
