### What is the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]] or the Adventurer’s Camp?

The [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]] system represents the party’s collective gear, inventory and a place where they rest. This ‘system’ simplifies the inventory management of a group. This has multiple components as follows:

- **Downtime**
    - When a party rests they can perform [[Downtime Activities\|Downtime Activities]]
    - These can include planning the road/studying/training etc
- **Inventory**
	- Purchased items are replenished over a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Downtime Rest\|Downtime Rest]] while in a town.

# [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]] Unlock System

When you play as a party you share the camp and all the unlocks within are owned by your party, no hero survives alone. All the options are purchasable through [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]], found as loot or rewarded. [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]] is still ‘locked’ by the party’s rank using Adept/Veteran/Master.
The party can use the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Wealth\|Wealth]] Points while they are in a town, city or safehaven. In the middle of the wilderness or a dungeon, new purchases cannot be made.
