[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]] is the third and final rank you can achieve in any given field. A master defines the very best of the best or strongest in any category. There is very little one cannot do when they master their craft.

When you reach the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]] rank you can learn an additional [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Mastery\|Mastery]] (maximum of 6) and upgrade your [[Masteries\|Masteries]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]]. You can start choosing [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]] abilities.
You also gain can gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] (maximum of 5) can upgrade [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]]or gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Master\|Master]] [[Skill Abilities\|Skill Abilities]], [[Minor Skill Feats\|Minor Skill Feats]], [[Skill Feats\|Skill Feats]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Tactical Skill\|Tactical Skill]].
