[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Power Strike\|Power Strike]] is a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Strike\|Strike]] that deals half damage on a failure.

**Critical Success:** You deal double weapon damage  
**Success:** You deal normal weapon damage  
**Failure:** You deal half **total** damage  
**Critical Failure:** You deal no damage
