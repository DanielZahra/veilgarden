### [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Quick Rest\|Quick Rest]] (6 hours)

- When resting, the party replenishes all [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Consumable\|consumable]]s that they have unlocked through [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]]. Any item that is not placed in the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Backpack\|Backpack]] is discarded.
- When resting, you recover two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaper’s Mark\|Reaper’s Mark]]s.
- When resting you can also perform [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Training\|Training]].
