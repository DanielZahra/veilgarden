You can only [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]] **once** per day (24 hours). Resting takes a 6 or 12 hours for you to set up camp, secure the perimeter, eat, drink, sleep and prepare for the next day. This time also assumes and includes you performing downtime activities. Everything that doesn’t get recovered with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Exploration Activities/Take a Break\|Take a Break]] Recovers with a Rest.  If you decide to rest multiple times in a row to recover further, the above rule of one rest per day still applies so it takes 2 days to accumulate 2 rests.

### Sleeping and Travel

When you are travelling through non-dangerous places ([[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Director\|Director]]’s choice), and you need to sleep for the day, you can decide to simply sleep without triggering a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]]. This allows you to retain all preparations you have made but you also gain no benefit from a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Rest\|Rest]].

There are two types of resting; you can choose to simply recover as quickly as possible or take some extra time to take downtime.

### [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Rest/Quick Rest\|Quick Rest]] (6 hours)

- When resting, the party replenishes all [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Consumable\|consumable]]s that they have unlocked through [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Campcraft\|Campcraft]]. Any item that is not placed in the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Gear/Backpack\|Backpack]] is discarded.
- When resting, you recover two [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reaper’s Mark\|Reaper’s Mark]]s.
- When resting you can also perform [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Content/Downtime Activities/Training\|Training]].
