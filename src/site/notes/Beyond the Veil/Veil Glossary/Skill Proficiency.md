[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] represents your knowledge and 'skill' within one of the [[Skills\|Skills]]. You gain any features within a skill, you must have the skill as a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] and you can use a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] to raise a skill within the ranks.
