Most creatures that require breathing can hold their breath for a minute/10 rounds before they start [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Suffocating\|Suffocating]]. When [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Suffocating\|Suffocating]] you take 5 [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Vitality\|Vitality]] damage per round.
