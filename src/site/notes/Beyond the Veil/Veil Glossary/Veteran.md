[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]] is the second rank you gain. Veterans are the professionals who are typically some of the best in their fields in a given discipline or skill.

When you reach the [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]] rank you can learn an additional [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Mastery\|Mastery]] (maximum of 5) and upgrade your [[Masteries\|Masteries]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]]. You can start choosing [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]] abilities and upgrading [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Adept\|Adept]] abilities  with a [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Signature\|Signature]] effect. 
You also gain can gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] (maximum of 4) can upgrade [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Skill Proficiency\|Skill Proficiency]] to [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]] or gain [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Veteran\|Veteran]], [[Skill Abilities\|Skill Abilities]], [[Minor Skill Feats\|Minor Skill Feats]], [[Skill Feats\|Skill Feats]] and [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Tactical Skill\|Tactical Skill]].
