[[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Weapon Range\|Weapon Range]] is a term used for weapons attacks that allow you to use the specified attack within any range that the weapon allows, be it [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Melee\|Melee]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Reach\|Reach]], [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Thrown/Thrown\|Thrown]] or [[Beyond the Veil/Veil Glossary/Range/Range\|Range]]
